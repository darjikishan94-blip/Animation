10.3 (a) The refractive index of glass is 1.5. What is the speed of light in glass? (Speed of light in vacuum is 3.0 × 108 m s–1) (b) Is the speed of light in glass independent of the colour of light? If not, which of the two colours red and violet travels slower in a glass prism?

Given

  • n = 1.5; c = 3.0 × 108 m/s.

Asked

(a) v in glass (b) does it depend on colour?

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) v = c/n = 3.0 × 108/1.5 = 2.0 × 108 m/s.
  2. (b) No: n depends on the wavelength (dispersion). n is larger for violet than for red, so violet travels slower.

(a) 2.0 × 108 m/s (b) No; violet is slower.
