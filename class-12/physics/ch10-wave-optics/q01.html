10.1 Monochromatic light of wavelength 589 nm is incident from air on a water surface. What are the wavelength, frequency and speed of (a) reflected, and (b) refracted light? Refractive index of water is 1.33.

Given

  • λ = 589 nm in air; nwater = 1.33; c = 3 × 108 m/s.

Asked

λ, ν and v for (a) the reflected (b) the refracted light.

How the animation is drawn

  • Wavefront spacing to scale between the media (589 : 443); speeds slowed down.

Answer

  1. (a) Reflected light stays in air: λ = 589 nm, v = 3 × 108 m/s, ν = c/λ = 3 × 108/589 × 10−9 ≈ 5.09 × 1014 Hz.
  2. (b) The frequency does not change on refraction: ν = 5.09 × 1014 Hz. v = c/n = 3 × 108/1.33 ≈ 2.26 × 108 m/s; λ = v/ν = 589/1.33 ≈ 443 nm.

(a) 589 nm, 5.09 × 1014 Hz, 3 × 108 m/s (b) 443 nm, 5.09 × 1014 Hz, 2.26 × 108 m/s.
