10.4 In a Young’s double-slit experiment, the slits are separated by 0.28 mm and the screen is placed 1.4 m away. The distance between the central bright fringe and the fourth bright fringe is measured to be 1.2 cm. Determine the wavelength of light used in the experiment.

Given

  • d = 0.28 mm; D = 1.4 m; y4 = 1.2 cm (4th bright fringe).

Asked

λ.

How the animation is drawn

  • Slit separation and distances not to scale; the fringe pattern on the screen is to scale with itself.

Answer

  1. Bright fringes are at yn = nλD/d.
  2. λ = y4d/(4D) = 1.2 × 10−2 × 0.28 × 10−3/(4 × 1.4) = 6.0 × 10−7 m = 600 nm.

600 nm.
