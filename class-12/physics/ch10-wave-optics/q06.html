10.6 A beam of light consisting of two wavelengths, 650 nm and 520 nm, is used to obtain interference fringes in a Young’s double-slit experiment. (a) Find the distance of the third bright fringe on the screen from the central maximum for wavelength 650 nm. (b) What is the least distance from the central maximum where the bright fringes due to both the wavelengths coincide?

Given

  • λ₁ = 650 nm; λ₂ = 520 nm. The reprint does not give the slit separation d or the screen distance D.

Asked

(a) y of the 3rd bright fringe of 650 nm (b) the nearest coincidence.

How the animation is drawn

  • Positions in units of D/d (to scale with each other).

Answer

  1. (a) y3 = 3λ₁D/d = 1950 nm × D/d.
  2. (b) Coincidence needs n₁ × 650 = n₂ × 520, i.e. n₁/n₂ = 4/5. The first is n₁ = 4, n₂ = 5: y = 4 × 650 nm × D/d = 2600 nm × D/d.
  3. With d = 2 mm and D = 1.2 m (the values behind the NCERT answers): (a) 1.17 mm (b) 1.56 mm.

(a) 1950 nm × D/d (1.17 mm) (b) 2600 nm × D/d (1.56 mm).
