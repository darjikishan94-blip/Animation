9.13 A small telescope has an objective lens of focal length 144 cm and an eyepiece of focal length 6.0 cm. What is the magnifying power of the telescope? What is the separation between the objective and the eyepiece?

Given

  • fo = 144 cm; fe = 6.0 cm; normal adjustment.

Asked

M; separation.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. M = fo/fe = 144/6.0 = 24.
  2. Separation = fo + fe = 150 cm.

24; 150 cm.
