9.19 A screen is placed 90 cm from an object. The image of the object on the screen is formed by a convex lens at two different locations separated by 20 cm. Determine the focal length of the lens.

Given

  • D = 90 cm; the two lens positions are d = 20 cm apart.

Asked

f.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. The two positions are interchangeable (u ↔ v): u + v = 90 and v − u = 20, so u = 35 cm and v = 55 cm.
  2. 1/f = 1/35 + 1/55, so f = 35 × 55/90 ≈ 21.4 cm (equivalently f = (D2 − d2)/4D).

≈ 21.4 cm.
