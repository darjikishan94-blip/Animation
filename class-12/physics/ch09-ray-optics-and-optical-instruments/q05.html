9.5 A small bulb is placed at the bottom of a tank containing water to a depth of 80cm. What is the area of the surface of water through which light from the bulb can emerge out? Refractive index of water is 1.33. (Consider the bulb to be a point source.)

Given

  • Depth h = 0.80 m; n = 1.33.

Asked

The area through which light emerges.

How the animation is drawn

  • 2.5 px per cm.

Answer

  1. Critical angle: sin C = 1/1.33, so C ≈ 48.8°. Rays meeting the surface at more than C are totally reflected.
  2. Radius of the circle: r = h tan C = 0.80 × 1.14 ≈ 0.91 m.
  3. Area = πr2 ≈ 3.14 × 0.83 ≈ 2.6 m2.

≈ 2.6 m2.
