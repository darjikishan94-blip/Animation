9.8 A beam of light converges at a point P. Now a lens is placed in the path of the convergent beam 12 cm from P. At what point does the beam converge if the lens is (a) a convex lens of focal length 20 cm, and (b) a concave lens of focal length 16 cm?

Given

  • P is 12 cm beyond the lens: a virtual object, u = +12 cm. (a) f = +20 cm (b) f = −16 cm. Cartesian sign convention: distances measured from the pole/optical centre, positive in the direction of the incident light.

Asked

Where the beam now converges.

How the animation is drawn

  • 8 px per cm; dashed: where the beam would have gone.

Answer

  1. 1/v − 1/u = 1/f with u = +12 cm.
  2. (a) 1/v = 1/20 + 1/12 = 8/60, so v = 7.5 cm: the beam converges 7.5 cm beyond the lens.
  3. (b) 1/v = −1/16 + 1/12 = 1/48, so v = 48 cm: it converges 48 cm beyond the lens.

(a) 7.5 cm from the lens (b) 48 cm from the lens.
