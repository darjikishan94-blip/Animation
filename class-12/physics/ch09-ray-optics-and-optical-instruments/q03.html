9.3 A tank is filled with water to a height of 12.5 cm. The apparent depth of a needle lying at the bottom of the tank is measured by a microscope to be 9.4 cm. What is the refractive index of water? If water is replaced by a liquid of refractive index 1.63 up to the same height, by what distance would the microscope have to be moved to focus on the needle again?

Given

  • Real depth 12.5 cm; apparent depth 9.4 cm; new liquid n = 1.63.

Asked

n of water; how far the microscope moves.

How the animation is drawn

  • 16 px per cm; viewed nearly straight down (rays drawn wider for clarity).

Answer

  1. n = real depth/apparent depth = 12.5/9.4 ≈ 1.33.
  2. New apparent depth = 12.5/1.63 ≈ 7.67 cm.
  3. The needle now seems 9.4 − 7.67 ≈ 1.7 cm higher: the microscope must be moved up by about 1.7 cm.

n ≈ 1.33; move the microscope up by ≈ 1.7 cm.
