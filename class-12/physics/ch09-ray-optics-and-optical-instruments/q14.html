9.14 (a) A giant refracting telescope at an observatory has an objective lens of focal length 15 m. If an eyepiece of focal length 1.0 cm is used, what is the angular magnification of the telescope? (b) If this telescope is used to view the moon, what is the diameter of the image of the moon formed by the objective lens? The diameter of the moon is 3.48 × 106 m, and the radius of lunar orbit is 3.8 × 108 m.

Given

  • fo = 15 m; fe = 0.010 m; moon diameter 3.48 × 106 m at 3.8 × 108 m.

Asked

(a) M (b) the diameter of the moon’s image.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) M = fo/fe = 15/0.010 = 1500.
  2. (b) The moon subtends α = 3.48 × 106/3.8 × 108 ≈ 9.16 × 10−3 rad. The image (in the focal plane) has diameter foα = 15 × 9.16 × 10−3 ≈ 0.137 m = 13.7 cm.

(a) 1500 (b) ≈ 13.7 cm.
