9.11 A compound microscope consists of an objective lens of focal length 2.0 cm and an eyepiece of focal length 6.25 cm separated by a distance of 15 cm. How far from the objective should an object be placed in order to obtain the final image at (a) the least distance of distinct vision (25 cm), and (b) at infinity? What is the magnifying power of the microscope in each case?

Given

  • fo = 2.0 cm; fe = 6.25 cm; separation 15 cm; D = 25 cm.

Asked

Object distance and magnifying power for (a) final image at 25 cm (b) at infinity.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Eyepiece: ve = −25 cm, so 1/ue = −1/25 − 1/6.25 = −0.2, ue = −5 cm. The objective’s image is 15 − 5 = 10 cm from it: 1/uo = 1/10 − 1/2 = −0.4, uo = −2.5 cm.
  2. M = (vo/|uo|)(1 + D/fe) = (10/2.5)(1 + 4) = 20.
  3. (b) ue = −6.25 cm, so vo = 15 − 6.25 = 8.75 cm; 1/uo = 1/8.75 − 1/2, uo ≈ −2.59 cm.
  4. M = (8.75/2.59)(25/6.25) ≈ 3.375 × 4 = 13.5.

(a) 2.5 cm; M = 20 (b) ≈ 2.59 cm; M = 13.5.
