9.9 An object of size 3.0 cm is placed 14cm in front of a concave lens of focal length 21 cm. Describe the image produced by the lens. What happens if the object is moved further away from the lens?

Given

  • h = 3.0 cm; u = −14 cm; f = −21 cm. Cartesian sign convention: distances measured from the pole/optical centre, positive in the direction of the incident light.

Asked

The image; what happens as the object moves away.

How the animation is drawn

  • 12 px per cm in both directions.

Answer

  1. 1/v = 1/f + 1/u = −1/21 − 1/14 = −5/42, so v = −8.4 cm: on the same side as the object.
  2. m = v/u = (−8.4)/(−14) = 0.6: virtual, erect, 1.8 cm tall.
  3. As the object moves farther away, the image moves towards the focus (21 cm) and becomes smaller; it never goes beyond F.

Virtual, erect, 1.8 cm, 8.4 cm from the lens; it moves towards F and shrinks.
