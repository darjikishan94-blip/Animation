9.4 Figures 9.27(a) and (b) show refraction of a ray in air incident at 60° with the normal to a glass-air and water-air interface, respectively. Predict the angle of refraction in glass when the angle of incidence in water is 45° with the normal to a water-glass interface [Fig. 9.27(c)].

Given

  • Fig. 9.27(a): 60° in air, 35° in glass. (b): 60° in air, 47° in water. (c): 45° in water, into glass.

Asked

The angle of refraction in glass in (c).

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. nglass = sin 60°/sin 35° = 0.866/0.574 ≈ 1.51; nwater = sin 60°/sin 47° = 0.866/0.731 ≈ 1.18 (values from the figure).
  2. Water to glass: nwater sin 45° = nglass sin r, so sin r = 1.18 × 0.707/1.51 ≈ 0.555 and r ≈ 33.7°.
  3. (Some printings of the answer give 38.68°; the figure’s values give 33.7°.)

≈ 33.7°.
