9.17 (a) Figure 9.28 shows a cross-section of a ‘light pipe’ made of a glass fibre of refractive index 1.68. The outer covering of the pipe is made of a material of refractive index 1.44. What is the range of the angles of the incident rays with the axis of the pipe for which total reflections inside the pipe take place, as shown in the figure. (b) What is the answer if there is no outer covering of the pipe?

Given

  • Core n = 1.68; cladding n = 1.44; (b) no cladding (air outside).

Asked

The range of incidence angles for total internal reflection.

How the animation is drawn

  • Ray paths computed with Snell’s law; the fibre is shortened.

Answer

  1. (a) Critical angle at the core–cladding wall: sin C = 1.44/1.68 = 0.857, C ≈ 59°.
  2. Inside, a ray at angle r to the axis meets the wall at 90° − r, so total reflection needs r ≤ 31°.
  3. At the end face sin i = 1.68 sin r ≤ 1.68 sin 31° ≈ 0.865, so i ≤ 60°: rays with 0 < i < 60° are trapped.
  4. (b) Without cladding, sin C = 1/1.68, C ≈ 36.5°; then r ≤ 53.5°, which needs sin i ≤ 1.68 sin 53.5° ≈ 1.35 — always true. All rays (0 < i < 90°) are totally reflected.

(a) 0 < i < 60° (b) all angles of incidence.
