9.16 A small pin fixed on a table top is viewed from above from a distance of 50 cm. By what distance would the pin appear to be raised if it is viewed from the same point through a 15 cm thick glass slab held parallel to the table? Refractive index of glass = 1.5. Does the answer depend on the location of the slab?

Given

  • Slab thickness t = 15 cm; n = 1.5.

Asked

The apparent rise; does it depend on where the slab is?

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Shift = t(1 − 1/n) = 15(1 − 1/1.5) = 5 cm.
  2. For near-normal viewing it does not depend on where the slab is placed between the pin and the eye.

5 cm; no.
