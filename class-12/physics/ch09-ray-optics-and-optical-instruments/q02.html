9.2 A 4.5 cm needle is placed 12 cm away from a convex mirror of focal length 15 cm. Give the location of the image and the magnification. Describe what happens as the needle is moved farther from the mirror.

Given

  • h = 4.5 cm; u = −12 cm; f = +15 cm (convex). Cartesian sign convention: distances measured from the pole/optical centre, positive in the direction of the incident light.

Asked

Image position and magnification; what happens as the needle moves away.

How the animation is drawn

  • 10 px per cm in both directions.

Answer

  1. 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/15 + 1/12 = 9/60, so v = 6.7 cm (behind the mirror).
  2. m = −v/u = −6.7/(−12) = 0.56: the image is virtual, erect, 2.5 cm tall.
  3. As the needle moves farther away, the image moves towards the focus (but never past it) and becomes smaller.

6.7 cm behind the mirror; m = 0.56 (2.5 cm, virtual, erect); it shrinks and approaches F.
