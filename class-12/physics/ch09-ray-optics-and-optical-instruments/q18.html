9.18 The image of a small electric bulb fixed on the wall of a room is to be obtained on the opposite wall 3 m away by means of a large convex lens. What is the maximum possible focal length of the lens required for the purpose?

Given

  • Object-to-screen distance D = 3 m.

Asked

The maximum focal length.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. With u + v = D (magnitudes) and 1/u + 1/v = 1/f, real solutions exist only if D ≥ 4f.
  2. fmax = D/4 = 0.75 m.

0.75 m.
