9.10 What is the focal length of a convex lens of focal length 30 cm in contact with a concave lens of focal length 20 cm? Is the system a converging or a diverging lens? Ignore thickness of the lenses.

Given

  • f₁ = +30 cm; f₂ = −20 cm; in contact.

Asked

F; converging or diverging?

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. 1/F = 1/f₁ + 1/f₂ = 1/30 − 1/20 = −1/60, so F = −60 cm.
  2. Negative: the combination is a diverging lens of focal length 60 cm.

−60 cm; diverging.
