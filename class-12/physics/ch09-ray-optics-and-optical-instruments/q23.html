9.23 (a) At what distance should the lens be held from the card sheet in Exercise 9.22 in order to view the squares distinctly with the maximum possible magnifying power? (b) What is the magnification in this case? (c) Is the magnification equal to the magnifying power in this case? Explain.

Given

  • f = 9 cm; image at D = 25 cm for maximum magnifying power.

Asked

(a) u (b) m (c) is m equal to the magnifying power?

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) v = −25 cm: 1/u = 1/v − 1/f = −1/25 − 1/9 = −34/225, so u ≈ −6.6 cm.
  2. (b) m = v/u = 25/6.6 ≈ 3.8.
  3. (c) Yes: with the image at 25 cm, the angle it subtends (h′/25) divided by the angle of the object at 25 cm (h/25) equals h′/h = m.

(a) ≈ 6.6 cm (b) ≈ 3.8 (c) Yes.
