1.2 The electrostatic force on a small sphere of charge 0.4 μC due to another small sphere of charge –0.8 μC in air is 0.2 N. (a) What is the distance between the two spheres? (b) What is the force on the second sphere due to the first?

Given

  • q₁ = 0.4 μC, q₂ = −0.8 μC; F = 0.2 N. (k = 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N m2 C−2; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 C2 N−1 m−2.)

Asked

(a) The separation (b) the force on the second sphere.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) r2 = k|q₁q₂|/F = 9 × 109 × 0.4 × 10−6 × 0.8 × 10−6/0.2 = 0.0144 m2, so r = 0.12 m.
  2. (b) By Newton’s third law: 0.2 N, attractive, directed towards the first sphere.

(a) 12 cm (b) 0.2 N, attractive.
