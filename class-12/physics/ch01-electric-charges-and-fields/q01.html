1.1 What is the force between two small charged spheres having charges of 2 × 10–7C and 3 × 10–7C placed 30 cm apart in air?

Given

  • q₁ = 2 × 10−7 C, q₂ = 3 × 10−7 C, r = 0.30 m, in air. (k = 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N m2 C−2; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 C2 N−1 m−2.)

Asked

The force between them.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. F = kq₁q₂/r2 = 9 × 109 × 2 × 10−7 × 3 × 10−7/0.302 = 6 × 10−3 N.
  2. Both charges are positive, so the force is repulsive.

6 × 10−3 N, repulsive.
