1.13 Figure 1.30 shows tracks of three charged particles in a uniform electrostatic field. Give the signs of the three charges. Which particle has the highest charge to mass ratio?

Given

  • Fig. 1.30: positive plate above, negative below (field downward); tracks 1 and 2 bend upward, track 3 bends downward and most.

Asked

Signs of the charges; the largest q/m.

How the animation is drawn

  • Tracks follow Fig. 1.30; equal entry speeds assumed.

Answer

  1. The field points from the + plate to the − plate (downward). A positive charge is pushed down, a negative charge up.
  2. Particles 1 and 2 bend up: negative. Particle 3 bends down: positive.
  3. The sideways deflection (for the same speed) is ∝ q/m; particle 3 is deflected most, so it has the highest q/m.

1 and 2 negative, 3 positive; 3 has the highest q/m.
