1.18 A point charge of 2.0 μC is at the centre of a cubic Gaussian surface 9.0 cm on edge. What is the net electric flux through the surface?

Given

  • q = 2.0 μC inside.

Asked

The net flux.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Φ = q/ε0 = 2.0 × 10−6/8.854 × 10−12 ≈ 2.3 × 105 N m2/C (the size of the cube does not matter).

About 2.3 × 105 N m2/C.
