1.14 Consider a uniform electric field E = 3 × 103 î N/C. (a) What is the flux of this field through a square of 10 cm on a side whose plane is parallel to the yz plane? (b) What is the flux through the same square if the normal to its plane makes a 60° angle with the x-axis?

Given

  • E = 3 × 103 N/C along x; square of side 0.10 m.

Asked

The flux (a) facing x (b) tilted 60°.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) The normal is along x: Φ = EA = 3 × 103 × 0.01 = 30 N m2/C.
  2. (b) Φ = EA cos 60° = 15 N m2/C.

(a) 30 N m2/C (b) 15 N m2/C
