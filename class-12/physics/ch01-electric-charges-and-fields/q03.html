1.3 Check that the ratio ke2/G memp is dimensionless. Look up a Table of Physical Constants and determine the value of this ratio. What does the ratio signify?

Given

  • k = 9 × 109, e = 1.6 × 10−19 C, G = 6.67 × 10−11, me = 9.1 × 10−31 kg, mp = 1.67 × 10−27 kg.

Asked

Dimensions, value and meaning of the ratio.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. ke2/r2 and Gmemp/r2 are both forces, so their ratio (with the same r) is dimensionless.
  2. Value ≈ 9 × 109 × (1.6 × 10−19)2/(6.67 × 10−11 × 9.1 × 10−31 × 1.67 × 10−27) ≈ 2.3 × 1039.
  3. It is the ratio of the electric to the gravitational force between an electron and a proton: the electric force is about 1039 times stronger.

≈ 2.3 × 1039: electric force ≫ gravitational force.
