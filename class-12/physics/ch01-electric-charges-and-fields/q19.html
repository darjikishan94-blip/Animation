1.19 A point charge causes an electric flux of –1.0 × 103 Nm2/C to pass through a spherical Gaussian surface of 10.0 cm radius centred on the charge. (a) If the radius of the Gaussian surface were doubled, how much flux would pass through the surface? (b) What is the value of the point charge?

Given

  • Φ = −1.0 × 103 N m2/C through a 10.0 cm sphere.

Asked

(a) Flux through a 20 cm sphere (b) the charge.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) The flux depends only on the enclosed charge: still −1.0 × 103 N m2/C.
  2. (b) q = ε0Φ = 8.854 × 10−12 × (−1.0 × 103) ≈ −8.9 × 10−9 C.

(a) −1.0 × 103 N m2/C (b) ≈ −8.9 nC
