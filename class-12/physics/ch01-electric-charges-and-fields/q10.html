1.10 An electric dipole with dipole moment 4 × 10–9 C m is aligned at 30° with the direction of a uniform electric field of magnitude 5 × 104 NC–1. Calculate the magnitude of the torque acting on the dipole.

Given

  • p = 4 × 10−9 C m; θ = 30°; E = 5 × 104 N C−1.

Asked

The torque.

How the animation is drawn

  • Dipole length and charges illustrative.

Answer

  1. τ = pE sin θ = 4 × 10−9 × 5 × 104 × sin 30° = 10−4 N m.
  2. It turns the dipole towards the field direction.

10−4 N m.
