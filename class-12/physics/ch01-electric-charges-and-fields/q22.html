1.22 An infinite line charge produces a field of 9 × 104 N/C at a distance of 2 cm. Calculate the linear charge density.

Given

  • E = 9 × 104 N/C at r = 0.02 m.

Asked

The linear charge density λ.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. E = λ/(2πε0r) = 2kλ/r, so λ = Er/2k = 9 × 104 × 0.02/(2 × 9 × 109) = 10−7 C/m.

10−7 C/m.
