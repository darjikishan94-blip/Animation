1.6 Four point charges qA = 2 μC, qB = –5 μC, qC = 2 μC, and qD = –5 μC are located at the corners of a square ABCD of side 10 cm. What is the force on a charge of 1 μC placed at the centre of the square?

Given

  • qA = qC = 2 μC and qB = qD = −5 μC at the corners of a 10 cm square (A and C opposite, B and D opposite); 1 μC at the centre.

Asked

The net force on the centre charge.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force arrows to scale with each other.

Answer

  1. The centre is equidistant from all four corners.
  2. A and C have equal charges at opposite corners: their forces on the centre charge are equal and opposite and cancel.
  3. B and D likewise cancel.
  4. Net force = 0.

Zero.
