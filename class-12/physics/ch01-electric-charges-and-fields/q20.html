1.20 A conducting sphere of radius 10 cm has an unknown charge. If the electric field 20 cm from the centre of the sphere is 1.5 × 103 N/C and points radially inward, what is the net charge on the sphere?

Given

  • E = 1.5 × 103 N/C, inward, at r = 0.20 m. (k = 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N m2 C−2; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 C2 N−1 m−2.)

Asked

The charge on the sphere.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Outside, the sphere acts as a point charge: |q| = Er2/k = 1.5 × 103 × 0.04/9 × 109 ≈ 6.7 × 10−9 C.
  2. The field points inward, so the charge is negative.

About −6.7 nC.
