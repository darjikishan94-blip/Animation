1.23 Two large, thin metal plates are parallel and close to each other. On their inner faces, the plates have surface charge densities of opposite signs and of magnitude 17.0 × 10–22 C/m2. What is E: (a) in the outer region of the first plate, (b) in the outer region of the second plate, and (c) between the plates?

Given

  • σ = 17.0 × 10−22 C/m2 (+ on one, − on the other).

Asked

E outside each plate and between them.

How the animation is drawn

  • Field arrows show directions only (the field is tiny).

Answer

  1. Each sheet alone gives σ/2ε0 on both sides, pointing away from + and towards −.
  2. (a), (b) Outside the plates the two fields are opposite: E = 0.
  3. (c) Between them they add: E = σ/ε0 = 17.0 × 10−22/8.854 × 10−12 ≈ 1.9 × 10−10 N/C, from the positive to the negative plate.

(a) 0 (b) 0 (c) ≈ 1.9 × 10−10 N/C
