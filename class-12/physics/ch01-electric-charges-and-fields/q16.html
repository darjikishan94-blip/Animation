1.16 Careful measurement of the electric field at the surface of a black box indicates that the net outward flux through the surface of the box is 8.0 × 103 Nm2/C. (a) What is the net charge inside the box? (b) If the net outward flux through the surface of the box were zero, could you conclude that there were no charges inside the box? Why or Why not?

Given

  • Net outward flux 8.0 × 103 N m2/C; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12.

Asked

(a) The net charge (b) does zero flux mean no charges?

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Gauss’s law: q = ε0Φ = 8.854 × 10−12 × 8.0 × 103 ≈ 7.1 × 10−8 C.
  2. (b) No: zero flux means zero net charge; there could be equal positive and negative charges inside.

(a) ≈ 0.07 μC (b) No: only the net charge is zero.
