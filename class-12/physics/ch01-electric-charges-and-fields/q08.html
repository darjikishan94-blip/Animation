1.8 Two point charges qA = 3 μC and qB = –3 μC are located 20 cm apart in vacuum. (a) What is the electric field at the midpoint O of the line AB joining the two charges? (b) If a negative test charge of magnitude 1.5 × 10–9 C is placed at this point, what is the force experienced by the test charge?

Given

  • qA = 3 μC, qB = −3 μC, 20 cm apart; test charge −1.5 × 10−9 C. (k = 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N m2 C−2; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 C2 N−1 m−2.)

Asked

(a) E at the midpoint (b) the force on the test charge.

How the animation is drawn

  • Field arrows to scale with each other; 2 px per mm.

Answer

  1. Each charge is 0.10 m from O: E = kq/r2 = 9 × 109 × 3 × 10−6/0.01 = 2.7 × 106 N/C each.
  2. (a) Both fields point from A to B (away from +, towards −): E = 5.4 × 106 N/C along AB.
  3. (b) F = qE = 1.5 × 10−9 × 5.4 × 106 = 8.1 × 10−3 N; the charge is negative, so the force points from B to A.

(a) 5.4 × 106 N/C along AB (b) 8.1 × 10−3 N, along BA.
