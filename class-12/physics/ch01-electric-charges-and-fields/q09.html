1.9 A system has two charges qA = 2.5 × 10–7 C and qB = –2.5 × 10–7 C located at points A: (0, 0, –15 cm) and B: (0,0, +15 cm), respectively. What are the total charge and electric dipole moment of the system?

Given

  • +2.5 × 10−7 C at z = −15 cm, −2.5 × 10−7 C at z = +15 cm.

Asked

Total charge; dipole moment.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Total charge = 2.5 × 10−7 − 2.5 × 10−7 = 0.
  2. p = q × separation = 2.5 × 10−7 × 0.30 = 7.5 × 10−8 C m, pointing from the negative to the positive charge: along −z.

Zero; 7.5 × 10−8 C m along −z.
