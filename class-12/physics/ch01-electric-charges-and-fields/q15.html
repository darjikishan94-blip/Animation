1.15 What is the net flux of the uniform electric field of Exercise 1.14 through a cube of side 20 cm oriented so that its faces are parallel to the coordinate planes?

Given

  • Uniform E along x; cube of side 20 cm.

Asked

The net flux.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. What enters through one yz face leaves through the opposite one; the other faces are parallel to E. (And the cube encloses no charge.)

Zero.
