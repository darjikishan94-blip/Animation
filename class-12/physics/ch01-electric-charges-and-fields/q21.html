1.21 A uniformly charged conducting sphere of 2.4 m diameter has a surface charge density of 80.0 μC/m2. (a) Find the charge on the sphere. (b) What is the total electric flux leaving the surface of the sphere?

Given

  • Radius 1.2 m; σ = 80.0 μC/m2.

Asked

(a) The charge (b) the flux.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Q = σ × 4πr2 = 80.0 × 10−6 × 4π × 1.22 ≈ 1.45 × 10−3 C.
  2. (b) Φ = Q/ε0 ≈ 1.45 × 10−3/8.854 × 10−12 ≈ 1.6 × 108 N m2/C.

(a) ≈ 1.45 × 10−3 C (b) ≈ 1.6 × 108 N m2/C
