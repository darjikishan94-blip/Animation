1.4 (a) Explain the meaning of the statement ‘electric charge of a body is quantised’. (b) Why can one ignore quantisation of electric charge when dealing with macroscopic i.e., large scale charges?

Given

  • Electric charge.

Asked

Meaning of quantisation; why it can be ignored on a large scale.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for an explanation rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Any charge is a whole-number multiple of the basic charge e = 1.6 × 10−19 C: q = ne. It cannot take values in between.
  2. (b) Everyday charges contain an enormous number of e (1 μC ≈ 6 × 1012 e); a step of one e is far too small to notice, so charge behaves as continuous.

q = ne; the steps are too small to notice for large charges.
