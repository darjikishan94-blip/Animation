1.17 A point charge +10 μC is a distance 5 cm directly above the centre of a square of side 10 cm, as shown in Fig. 1.31. What is the magnitude of the electric flux through the square? (Hint: Think of the square as one face of a cube with edge 10 cm.)

Given

  • q = +10 μC, 5 cm above the centre of a 10 cm square.

Asked

The flux through the square.

How the animation is drawn

  • The cube is imagined (dashed).

Answer

  1. The charge is at the centre of a 10 cm cube of which the square is one face.
  2. By symmetry the total flux q/ε0 divides equally among the 6 faces.
  3. Φ = q/6ε0 = 10−5/(6 × 8.854 × 10−12) ≈ 1.9 × 105 N m2/C.

About 1.9 × 105 N m2/C.
