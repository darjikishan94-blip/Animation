11.1 Find the (a) maximum frequency, and (b) minimum wavelength of X-rays produced by 30 kV electrons.

Given

  • Accelerating voltage V = 30 kV. h = 6.63 × 10−34 J s; c = 3 × 108 m/s; e = 1.6 × 10−19 C; me = 9.1 × 10−31 kg; hc ≈ 1240 eV nm.

Asked

(a) νmax (b) λmin.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. An electron’s whole kinetic energy eV can go into one photon: hνmax = eV.
  2. (a) νmax = eV/h = 1.6 × 10−19 × 3 × 104/6.63 × 10−34 ≈ 7.24 × 1018 Hz.
  3. (b) λmin = c/νmax ≈ 3 × 108/7.24 × 1018 ≈ 4.1 × 10−11 m = 0.041 nm.

(a) 7.24 × 1018 Hz (b) 0.041 nm.
