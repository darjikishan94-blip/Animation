11.8 Light of frequency 7.21 × 1014 Hz is incident on a metal surface. Electrons with a maximum speed of 6.0 × 105 m/s are ejected from the surface. What is the threshold frequency for photoemission of electrons?

Given

  • ν = 7.21 × 1014 Hz; vmax = 6.0 × 105 m/s. h = 6.63 × 10−34 J s; c = 3 × 108 m/s; e = 1.6 × 10−19 C; me = 9.1 × 10−31 kg; hc ≈ 1240 eV nm.

Asked

ν₀.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Kmax = ½mv2 = ½ × 9.1 × 10−31 × (6.0 × 105)2 ≈ 1.64 × 10−19 J.
  2. hν₀ = hν − Kmax: ν₀ = 7.21 × 1014 − 1.64 × 10−19/6.63 × 10−34 ≈ 7.21 × 1014 − 2.47 × 1014 ≈ 4.7 × 1014 Hz.

≈ 4.7 × 1014 Hz.
