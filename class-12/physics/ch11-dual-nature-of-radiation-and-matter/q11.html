11.11 Show that the wavelength of electromagnetic radiation is equal to the de Broglie wavelength of its quantum (photon).

Given

  • A photon: E = hν, p = E/c.

Asked

Show λdB = λ.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short derivation; the page shows the question and the worked answer.

Answer

  1. A photon’s momentum is p = E/c = hν/c = h/λ.
  2. Its de Broglie wavelength is h/p = h/(h/λ) = λ, the wavelength of the radiation.

h/p = λ.
