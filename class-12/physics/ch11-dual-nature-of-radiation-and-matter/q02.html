11.2 The work function of caesium metal is 2.14 eV. When light of frequency 6 × 1014 Hz is incident on the metal surface, photoemission of electrons occurs. What is the (a) maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electrons, (b) Stopping potential, and (c) maximum speed of the emitted photoelectrons?

Given

  • φ₀ = 2.14 eV; ν = 6 × 1014 Hz. h = 6.63 × 10−34 J s; c = 3 × 108 m/s; e = 1.6 × 10−19 C; me = 9.1 × 10−31 kg; hc ≈ 1240 eV nm.

Asked

(a) Kmax (b) V0 (c) vmax.

How the animation is drawn

  • Energy bars to scale.

Answer

  1. Photon energy hν = 6.63 × 10−34 × 6 × 1014 ≈ 3.98 × 10−19 J ≈ 2.49 eV.
  2. (a) Kmax = hν − φ₀ ≈ 2.49 − 2.14 ≈ 0.35 eV (0.345 eV).
  3. (b) eV0 = Kmax: V0 ≈ 0.35 V.
  4. (c) ½mv2 = 0.345 × 1.6 × 10−19 J, so v = √(2 × 5.52 × 10−20/9.1 × 10−31) ≈ 3.5 × 105 m/s.

(a) ≈ 0.35 eV (b) ≈ 0.35 V (c) ≈ 3.5 × 105 m/s.
