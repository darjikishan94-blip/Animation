11.5 In an experiment on photoelectric effect, the slope of the cut-off voltage versus frequency of incident light is found to be 4.12 × 10–15 V s. Calculate the value of Planck’s constant.

Given

  • Slope of V0 against ν = 4.12 × 10−15 V s; e = 1.6 × 10−19 C.

Asked

h.

How the animation is drawn

  • The threshold frequency (where the line meets the axis) is not given; it is illustrative.

Answer

  1. Einstein’s equation: eV0 = hν − φ₀, so V0 = (h/e)ν − φ₀/e: a straight line of slope h/e.
  2. h = e × slope = 1.6 × 10−19 × 4.12 × 10−15 ≈ 6.59 × 10−34 J s.

≈ 6.59 × 10−34 J s.
