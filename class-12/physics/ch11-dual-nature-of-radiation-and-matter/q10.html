11.10 What is the de Broglie wavelength of (a) a bullet of mass 0.040 kg travelling at the speed of 1.0 km/s, (b) a ball of mass 0.060 kg moving at a speed of 1.0 m/s, and (c) a dust particle of mass 1.0 × 10–9 kg drifting with a speed of 2.2 m/s?

Given

  • (a) 0.040 kg, 1.0 × 103 m/s (b) 0.060 kg, 1.0 m/s (c) 1.0 × 10−9 kg, 2.2 m/s; h = 6.63 × 10−34 J s.

Asked

λ = h/mv for each.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) λ = 6.63 × 10−34/(0.040 × 1000) ≈ 1.7 × 10−35 m.
  2. (b) λ = 6.63 × 10−34/(0.060 × 1.0) ≈ 1.1 × 10−32 m.
  3. (c) λ = 6.63 × 10−34/(1.0 × 10−9 × 2.2) ≈ 3.0 × 10−25 m.
  4. All far too small to show wave behaviour.

(a) 1.7 × 10−35 m (b) 1.1 × 10−32 m (c) 3.0 × 10−25 m
