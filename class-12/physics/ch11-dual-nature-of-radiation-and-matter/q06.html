11.6 The threshold frequency for a certain metal is 3.3 × 1014 Hz. If light of frequency 8.2 × 1014 Hz is incident on the metal, predict the cut-off voltage for the photoelectric emission.

Given

  • ν₀ = 3.3 × 1014 Hz; ν = 8.2 × 1014 Hz. h = 6.63 × 10−34 J s; c = 3 × 108 m/s; e = 1.6 × 10−19 C; me = 9.1 × 10−31 kg; hc ≈ 1240 eV nm.

Asked

V0.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. eV0 = h(ν − ν₀), so V0 = 6.63 × 10−34 × 4.9 × 1014/1.6 × 10−19 ≈ 2.0 V.

≈ 2.0 V.
