8.8 Suppose that the electric field amplitude of an electromagnetic wave is E0 = 120 N/C and that its frequency is ν = 50.0 MHz. (a) Determine, B0, ω, k, and λ. (b) Find expressions for E and B.

Given

  • E0 = 120 N/C; ν = 50.0 MHz; c = 3 × 108 m/s.

Asked

(a) B0, ω, k, λ (b) expressions for E and B.

How the animation is drawn

  • Direction of travel (x) and of E (y) chosen for the picture.

Answer

  1. (a) B0 = E0/c = 120/3 × 108 = 4.0 × 10−7 T (400 nT).
  2. ω = 2πν = 2π × 50 × 106 ≈ 3.14 × 108 rad/s; k = ω/c ≈ 1.05 rad/m; λ = c/ν = 6.0 m.
  3. (b) For a wave along x with E along y: E = 120 sin(1.05 x − 3.14 × 108 t) ĵ N/C and B = 4.0 × 10−7 sin(1.05 x − 3.14 × 108 t) k̂ T.

(a) 400 nT; 3.14 × 108 rad/s; 1.05 rad/m; 6.0 m (b) E = 120 sin(1.05x − 3.14 × 108t) ĵ, B = 4 × 10−7 sin(1.05x − 3.14 × 108t) k̂.
