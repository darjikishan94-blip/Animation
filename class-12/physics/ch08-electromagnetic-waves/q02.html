8.2 A parallel plate capacitor (Fig. 8.6) made of circular plates each of radius R = 6.0 cm has a capacitance C = 100 pF. The capacitor is connected to a 230 V ac supply with a (angular) frequency of 300 rad s–1. (a) What is the rms value of the conduction current? (b) Is the conduction current equal to the displacement current? (c) Determine the amplitude of B at a point 3.0 cm from the axis between the plates.

Given

  • R = 0.060 m; C = 100 pF; Vrms = 230 V; ω = 300 rad s−1; point at r = 0.030 m; μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.

Asked

(a) Irms (b) is ic = id? (c) B0 at 3.0 cm.

How the animation is drawn

  • Face-on view of the plates, 2.5 px per mm; the graph is B0(r) computed.

Answer

  1. (a) Irms = VrmsωC = 230 × 300 × 100 × 10−12 = 6.9 × 10−6 A (6.9 μA).
  2. (b) Yes: the displacement current between the plates equals the conduction current in the wires at every instant.
  3. (c) Inside, only the fraction r2/R2 of id is enclosed: B = μ0ri/(2πR2). With the peak current I0 = √2 × 6.9 μA = 9.76 μA:
  4. B0 = 4π × 10−7 × 0.030 × 9.76 × 10−6/(2π × 0.0602) ≈ 1.63 × 10−11 T.

(a) 6.9 μA (b) Yes (c) 1.63 × 10−11 T.
