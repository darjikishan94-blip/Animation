8.6 A charged particle oscillates about its mean equilibrium position with a frequency of 109 Hz. What is the frequency of the electromagnetic waves produced by the oscillator?

Given

  • Oscillation frequency 109 Hz.

Asked

The frequency of the waves.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation or a table rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. An oscillating charge radiates at its own frequency of oscillation: 109 Hz.

109 Hz.
