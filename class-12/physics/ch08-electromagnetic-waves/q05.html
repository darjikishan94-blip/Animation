8.5 A radio can tune in to any station in the 7.5 MHz to 12 MHz band. What is the corresponding wavelength band?

Given

  • 7.5 MHz to 12 MHz.

Asked

The wavelength band.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. λ = c/ν: 3 × 108/7.5 × 106 = 40 m and 3 × 108/12 × 106 = 25 m.

40 m to 25 m.
