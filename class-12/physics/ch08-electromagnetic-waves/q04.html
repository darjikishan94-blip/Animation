8.4 A plane electromagnetic wave travels in vacuum along z-direction. What can you say about the directions of its electric and magnetic field vectors? If the frequency of the wave is 30 MHz, what is its wavelength?

Given

  • Propagation along z, in vacuum; ν = 30 MHz.

Asked

Directions of E and B; λ.

How the animation is drawn

  • E chosen along x; it could be any direction in the xy-plane, with B at right angles to it.

Answer

  1. E and B both lie in the xy-plane, perpendicular to z and to each other, with E × B along +z.
  2. λ = c/ν = 3 × 108/30 × 106 = 10 m.

E ⟂ B, both in the xy-plane; λ = 10 m.
