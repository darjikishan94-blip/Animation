8.3 What physical quantity is the same for X-rays of wavelength 10–10 m, red light of wavelength 6800 Å and radiowaves of wavelength 500m?

Given

  • Three electromagnetic waves of very different wavelengths.

Asked

The quantity they share.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation or a table rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. All electromagnetic waves travel in vacuum at the same speed, c = 3 × 108 m/s (their frequencies and wavelengths differ, with νλ = c).

Their speed in vacuum, c.
