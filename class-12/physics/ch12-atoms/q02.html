12.2 Suppose you are given a chance to repeat the alpha-particle scattering experiment using a thin sheet of solid hydrogen in place of the gold foil. (Hydrogen is a solid at temperatures below 14 K.) What results do you expect?

Given

  • Target nuclei are protons (mass about ¼ of an α-particle, charge +e).

Asked

The expected results.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for short reasoned answers rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. A heavy particle striking a lighter one at rest cannot bounce back: momentum and energy conservation limit the α-particle’s deflection to small angles (at most about 14°, since sin θmax = mp/mα ≈ ¼).
  2. So no large-angle or back scattering would be seen; the hydrogen nucleus would itself recoil. The experiment could not reveal a small massive nucleus the way gold does.

Only small-angle scattering; no back-scattering.
