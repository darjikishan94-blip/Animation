12.3 A difference of 2.3 eV separates two energy levels in an atom. What is the frequency of radiation emitted when the atom make a transition from the upper level to the lower level?

Given

  • ΔE = 2.3 eV; h = 6.63 × 10−34 J s.

Asked

ν.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. ν = ΔE/h = 2.3 × 1.6 × 10−19/6.63 × 10−34 ≈ 5.6 × 1014 Hz.

≈ 5.6 × 1014 Hz.
