12.5 A hydrogen atom initially in the ground level absorbs a photon, which excites it to the n = 4 level. Determine the wavelength and frequency of photon.

Given

  • En = −13.6/n2 eV; n: 1 → 4; hc ≈ 1240 eV nm.

Asked

λ and ν of the photon.

How the animation is drawn

  • Energy levels to scale (25 px per eV).

Answer

  1. ΔE = 13.6(1 − 1/16) = 12.75 eV.
  2. λ = hc/ΔE ≈ 1240/12.75 ≈ 97 nm.
  3. ν = ΔE/h = 12.75 × 1.6 × 10−19/6.63 × 10−34 ≈ 3.1 × 1015 Hz.

≈ 97 nm; ≈ 3.1 × 1015 Hz.
