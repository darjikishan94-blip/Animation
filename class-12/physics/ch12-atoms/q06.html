12.6 (a) Using the Bohr’s model calculate the speed of the electron in a hydrogen atom in the n = 1, 2, and 3 levels. (b) Calculate the orbital period in each of these levels.

Given

  • vn = e2/(2ε0nh); rn = n2 × 0.53 × 10−10 m.

Asked

(a) v for n = 1, 2, 3 (b) the periods.

How the animation is drawn

  • Radii to scale with each other; motion slowed by about 1016; speeds to scale.

Answer

  1. (a) v1 = e2/(2ε0h) ≈ 2.18 × 106 m/s; vn = v1/n: v2 ≈ 1.09 × 106 m/s, v3 ≈ 7.27 × 105 m/s.
  2. (b) T = 2πr/v. T1 = 2π × 0.53 × 10−10/2.18 × 106 ≈ 1.53 × 10−16 s; T ∝ n3: T2 ≈ 1.22 × 10−15 s, T3 ≈ 4.12 × 10−15 s.

(a) 2.18, 1.09, 0.727 × 106 m/s (b) 1.53 × 10−16, 1.22 × 10−15, 4.12 × 10−15 s.
