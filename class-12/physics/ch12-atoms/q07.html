12.7 The radius of the innermost electron orbit of a hydrogen atom is 5.3×10–11 m. What are the radii of the n = 2 and n =3 orbits?

Given

  • r1 = 5.3 × 10−11 m; rn = n2r1.

Asked

r2, r3.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. r2 = 4 × 5.3 × 10−11 = 2.12 × 10−10 m.
  2. r3 = 9 × 5.3 × 10−11 = 4.77 × 10−10 m.

2.12 × 10−10 m; 4.77 × 10−10 m.
