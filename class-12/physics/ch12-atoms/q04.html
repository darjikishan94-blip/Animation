12.4 The ground state energy of hydrogen atom is –13.6 eV. What are the kinetic and potential energies of the electron in this state?

Given

  • E = −13.6 eV.

Asked

K and U.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. For an inverse-square attraction in a circular orbit, K = −E and U = 2E.
  2. K = 13.6 eV; U = −27.2 eV.

K = 13.6 eV; U = −27.2 eV.
