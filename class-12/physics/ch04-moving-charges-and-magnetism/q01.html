4.1 A circular coil of wire consisting of 100 turns, each of radius 8.0 cm carries a current of 0.40 A. What is the magnitude of the magnetic field B at the centre of the coil?

Given

  • N = 100; R = 0.080 m; I = 0.40 A. (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.)

Asked

B at the centre.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. B = μ0NI/2R = 4π × 10−7 × 100 × 0.40/(2 × 0.080) ≈ 3.1 × 10−4 T.

3.1 × 10−4 T.
