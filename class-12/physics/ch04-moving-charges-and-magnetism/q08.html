4.8 A closely wound solenoid 80 cm long has 5 layers of windings of 400 turns each. The diameter of the solenoid is 1.8 cm. If the current carried is 8.0 A, estimate the magnitude of B inside the solenoid near its centre.

Given

  • l = 0.80 m; N = 5 × 400 = 2000; I = 8.0 A. (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.)

Asked

B near the centre.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. n = N/l = 2000/0.80 = 2500 m−1.
  2. B = μ0nI = 4π × 10−7 × 2500 × 8.0 ≈ 2.5 × 10−2 T (the diameter does not matter for a long solenoid).

≈ 2.5 × 10−2 T.
