4.6 A 3.0 cm wire carrying a current of 10 A is placed inside a solenoid perpendicular to its axis. The magnetic field inside the solenoid is given to be 0.27 T. What is the magnetic force on the wire?

Given

  • l = 0.030 m; I = 10 A; B = 0.27 T along the axis; wire ⟂ axis.

Asked

The force on the wire.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. F = BIl sin 90° = 0.27 × 10 × 0.030 = 8.1 × 10−2 N, perpendicular to both the wire and the solenoid axis.

8.1 × 10−2 N.
