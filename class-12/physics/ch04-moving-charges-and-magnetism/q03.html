4.3 A long straight wire in the horizontal plane carries a current of 50 A in north to south direction. Give the magnitude and direction of B at a point 2.5 m east of the wire.

Given

  • I = 50 A, north to south, in a horizontal plane; point 2.5 m east. (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.)

Asked

Magnitude and direction of B.

How the animation is drawn

  • Two views: from above, and end-on looking north.

Answer

  1. B = μ0I/2πr = 2 × 10−7 × 50/2.5 = 4 × 10−6 T.
  2. Right-hand rule: thumb along the current (south), the fingers curl upward on the east side.

4 × 10−6 T, vertically upward.
