4.5 What is the magnitude of magnetic force per unit length on a wire carrying a current of 8 A and making an angle of 30º with the direction of a uniform magnetic field of 0.15 T?

Given

  • I = 8 A; θ = 30°; B = 0.15 T.

Asked

F/l.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. F/l = BI sin θ = 0.15 × 8 × sin 30° = 0.6 N m−1.

0.6 N m−1.
