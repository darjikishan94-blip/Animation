4.12 In Exercise 4.11 obtain the frequency of revolution of the electron in its circular orbit. Does the answer depend on the speed of the electron? Explain.

Given

  • As in 4.11: B = 6.5 × 10−4 T, me = 9.1 × 10−31 kg, e = 1.6 × 10−19 C.

Asked

The frequency; does it depend on speed?

How the animation is drawn

  • A second electron at twice the speed is added to show the independence of speed.

Answer

  1. T = 2πr/v = 2πm/eB, so ν = eB/2πm = 1.6 × 10−19 × 6.5 × 10−4/(2π × 9.1 × 10−31) ≈ 1.8 × 107 Hz (18 MHz).
  2. No: r is proportional to v, so the time for one turn is the same at any (non-relativistic) speed. (With the printed e = 1.5 × 10−19 C, ν ≈ 1.7 × 107 Hz.)

≈ 18 MHz; independent of the speed.
