4.7 Two long and parallel straight wires A and B carrying currents of 8.0 A and 5.0 A in the same direction are separated by a distance of 4.0 cm. Estimate the force on a 10 cm section of wire A.

Given

  • IA = 8.0 A, IB = 5.0 A, same direction; d = 0.040 m; section L = 0.10 m. (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.)

Asked

The force on 10 cm of A.

How the animation is drawn

  • Separation and section length not to scale.

Answer

  1. Field of B at A: B = μ0IB/2πd.
  2. F = B IA L = μ0IAIBL/2πd = 2 × 10−7 × 8.0 × 5.0 × 0.10/0.040 = 2 × 10−5 N.
  3. Currents in the same direction attract: the force on A is towards B.

2 × 10−5 N, attractive (towards B).
