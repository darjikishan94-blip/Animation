4.2 A long straight wire carries a current of 35 A. What is the magnitude of the field B at a point 20 cm from the wire?

Given

  • I = 35 A; r = 0.20 m. (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.)

Asked

B at 20 cm.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. B = μ0I/2πr = 2 × 10−7 × 35/0.20 = 3.5 × 10−5 T.

3.5 × 10−5 T.
