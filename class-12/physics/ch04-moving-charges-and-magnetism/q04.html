4.4 A horizontal overhead power line carries a current of 90 A in east to west direction. What is the magnitude and direction of the magnetic field due to the current 1.5 m below the line?

Given

  • I = 90 A, east to west; point 1.5 m below. (μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.)

Asked

Magnitude and direction of B.

How the animation is drawn

  • Two views: from the side (looking north), and end-on looking east.

Answer

  1. B = 2 × 10−7 × 90/1.5 = 1.2 × 10−5 T.
  2. Right-hand rule: thumb pointing west, the fingers below the wire point south.

1.2 × 10−5 T, towards the south.
