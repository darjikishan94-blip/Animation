7.6 A charged 30 μF capacitor is connected to a 27 mH inductor. What is the angular frequency of free oscillations of the circuit?

Given

  • C = 30 × 10−6 F; L = 27 × 10−3 H.

Asked

ω of free oscillations.

How the animation is drawn

  • Slowed down; no resistance assumed.

Answer

  1. ω = 1/√(LC) = 1/√(27 × 10−3 × 30 × 10−6) = 1/√(8.1 × 10−7) = 1/(9 × 10−4) ≈ 1.1 × 103 rad/s.

≈ 1.1 × 103 rad s−1.
