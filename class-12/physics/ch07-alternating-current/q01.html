7.1 A 100 Ω resistor is connected to a 220 V, 50 Hz ac supply. (a) What is the rms value of current in the circuit? (b) What is the net power consumed over a full cycle?

Given

  • R = 100 Ω; Vrms = 220 V; 50 Hz.

Asked

(a) Irms (b) the average power.

How the animation is drawn

  • Graphs over two cycles (40 ms); curves to scale.

Answer

  1. (a) Irms = Vrms/R = 220/100 = 2.2 A.
  2. (b) For a resistor v and i are in phase, so p = vi ≥ 0 and its average is VrmsIrms = 220 × 2.2 = 484 W.

(a) 2.2 A (b) 484 W.
