7.7 A series LCR circuit with R = 20 Ω, L = 1.5 H and C = 35 μF is connected to a variable-frequency 200 V ac supply. When the frequency of the supply equals the natural frequency of the circuit, what is the average power transferred to the circuit in one complete cycle?

Given

  • R = 20 Ω; L = 1.5 H; C = 35 μF; Vrms = 200 V; at resonance.

Asked

The average power.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. At resonance XL = XC, so Z = R = 20 Ω.
  2. Irms = 200/20 = 10 A; P = I2R = 100 × 20 = 2000 W (L and C do not matter).

2000 W.
