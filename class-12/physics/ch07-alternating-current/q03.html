7.3 A 44 mH inductor is connected to 220 V, 50 Hz ac supply. Determine the rms value of the current in the circuit.

Given

  • L = 44 × 10−3 H; Vrms = 220 V; ν = 50 Hz.

Asked

Irms.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. XL = 2πνL = 2π × 50 × 0.044 ≈ 13.8 Ω.
  2. Irms = 220/13.8 ≈ 15.9 A.

≈ 15.9 A.
