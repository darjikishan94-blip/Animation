7.5 In Exercises 7.3 and 7.4, what is the net power absorbed by each circuit over a complete cycle. Explain your answer.

Given

  • The inductor of 7.3 (220 V, 50 Hz, Irms 15.9 A) and the capacitor of 7.4 (110 V, 60 Hz, Irms 2.49 A).

Asked

The average power in each, and why.

How the animation is drawn

  • Curves to scale within each panel, over two cycles.

Answer

  1. P = VrmsIrms cos φ. In a pure inductor the current lags the voltage by 90°; in a pure capacitor it leads by 90°. Either way cos φ = 0.
  2. Energy taken in during one quarter cycle is given back during the next: the net power is zero in both circuits.

Zero in both.
