7.2 (a) The peak voltage of an ac supply is 300 V. What is the rms voltage? (b) The rms value of current in an ac circuit is 10 A. What is the peak current?

Given

  • (a) V0 = 300 V (b) Irms = 10 A.

Asked

(a) Vrms (b) I0.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Vrms = V0/√2 = 300/1.414 ≈ 212 V.
  2. (b) I0 = √2 Irms = 1.414 × 10 ≈ 14.1 A.

(a) ≈ 212 V (b) ≈ 14.1 A.
