7.4 A 60 μF capacitor is connected to a 110 V, 60 Hz ac supply. Determine the rms value of the current in the circuit.

Given

  • C = 60 × 10−6 F; Vrms = 110 V; ν = 60 Hz.

Asked

Irms.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. XC = 1/(2πνC) = 1/(2π × 60 × 60 × 10−6) ≈ 44.2 Ω.
  2. Irms = 110/44.2 ≈ 2.49 A.

≈ 2.49 A.
