6.3 A long solenoid with 15 turns per cm has a small loop of area 2.0 cm2 placed inside the solenoid normal to its axis. If the current carried by the solenoid changes steadily from 2.0 A to 4.0 A in 0.1 s, what is the induced emf in the loop while the current is changing?

Given

  • n = 15 per cm = 1500 m−1; A = 2.0 × 10−4 m2; I: 2.0 A → 4.0 A in 0.1 s; μ0 = 4π × 10−7 T m A−1.

Asked

The induced emf.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. B = μ0nI, so dB/dt = μ0n dI/dt = 4π × 10−7 × 1500 × 20 ≈ 3.8 × 10−2 T/s.
  2. ε = A dB/dt = 2.0 × 10−4 × 3.8 × 10−2 ≈ 7.5 × 10−6 V.

≈ 7.5 × 10−6 V.
