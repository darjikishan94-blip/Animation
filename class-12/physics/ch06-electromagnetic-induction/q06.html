6.6 A horizontal straight wire 10 m long extending from east to west is falling with a speed of 5.0 m s–1, at right angles to the horizontal component of the earth’s magnetic field, 0.30 × 10–4 Wb m–2. (a) What is the instantaneous value of the emf induced in the wire? (b) What is the direction of the emf? (c) Which end of the wire is at the higher electrical potential?

Given

  • l = 10 m, east–west; v = 5.0 m/s downward; BH = 0.30 × 10−4 T (pointing north).

Asked

(a) The emf (b) its direction (c) the higher-potential end.

How the animation is drawn

  • View looking north: BH points into the page.

Answer

  1. (a) ε = Blv = 0.30 × 10−4 × 10 × 5.0 = 1.5 × 10−3 V.
  2. (b) Force on a positive charge: q(v × B) with v down and B north points east: the emf acts from west to east.
  3. (c) Positive charge collects at the east end: the east end is at the higher potential.

(a) 1.5 × 10−3 V (b) west to east (c) the eastern end.
