6.2 Use Lenz’s law to determine the direction of induced current in the situations described by Fig. 6.16: (a) A wire of irregular shape turning into a circular shape; (b) A circular loop being deformed into a narrow straight wire.

Given

  • Fig. 6.16(a): field into the page (×), irregular loop abcd opening into a circle. (b): field out of the page (·), circle a′b′c′d′ squashed into a narrow loop.

Asked

The induced current direction in each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Shapes illustrative; dots on the wire show the induced current.

Answer

  1. (a) The area, and so the flux into the page, increases. The induced current makes a field out of the page inside the loop: anticlockwise as seen, along adcb.
  2. (b) The area, and so the flux out of the page, decreases. The induced current makes a field out of the page to make up for it: anticlockwise as seen, along a′d′c′b′.

(a) along adcb (anticlockwise) (b) along a′d′c′b′ (anticlockwise).
