6.4 A rectangular wire loop of sides 8 cm and 2 cm with a small cut is moving out of a region of uniform magnetic field of magnitude 0.3 T directed normal to the loop. What is the emf developed across the cut if the velocity of the loop is 1 cm s–1 in a direction normal to the (a) longer side, (b) shorter side of the loop? For how long does the induced voltage last in each case?

Given

  • Loop 8 cm × 2 cm with a cut; B = 0.3 T normal to the loop; v = 1 cm/s.

Asked

The emf across the cut and how long it lasts, for motion normal to (a) the longer side (b) the shorter side.

How the animation is drawn

  • 20 px per cm; real time (1 cm/s).

Answer

  1. While the loop crosses the edge of the field, the flux changes at B l v, where l is the side lying along the edge.
  2. (a) Moving normal to the longer side, l = 8 cm: ε = 0.3 × 0.08 × 0.01 = 2.4 × 10−4 V, lasting (2 cm)/(1 cm/s) = 2 s.
  3. (b) Moving normal to the shorter side, l = 2 cm: ε = 0.3 × 0.02 × 0.01 = 6 × 10−5 V, lasting (8 cm)/(1 cm/s) = 8 s.

(a) 2.4 × 10−4 V for 2 s (b) 6 × 10−5 V for 8 s.
