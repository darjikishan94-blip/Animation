2.3 Two charges 2 μC and –2 μC are placed at points A and B 6 cm apart. (a) Identify an equipotential surface of the system. (b) What is the direction of the electric field at every point on this surface?

Given

  • +2 μC at A, −2 μC at B, AB = 6 cm.

Asked

(a) An equipotential surface (b) the field direction on it.

How the animation is drawn

  • Field lines computed from the two charges; field arrows on the plane to scale with each other.

Answer

  1. (a) Every point of the plane through the midpoint of AB, perpendicular to AB, is equally far from the two charges: V = kq/r − kq/r = 0. This plane is an equipotential surface.
  2. (b) The field is perpendicular to an equipotential surface: on this plane it points parallel to AB, from A (positive) towards B (negative).

(a) The perpendicular bisector plane of AB (V = 0) (b) normal to the plane, in the direction A → B.
