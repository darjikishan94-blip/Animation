2.6 Three capacitors each of capacitance 9 pF are connected in series. (a) What is the total capacitance of the combination? (b) What is the potential difference across each capacitor if the combination is connected to a 120 V supply?

Given

  • Three capacitors of 9 pF in series; supply 120 V.

Asked

(a) The total capacitance (b) the p.d. across each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circuit schematic; the + plate of the cell is marked.

Answer

  1. (a) 1/C = 1/9 + 1/9 + 1/9 = 1/3, so C = 3 pF.
  2. (b) Every capacitor in series carries the same charge Q = CV = 3 pF × 120 V = 360 pC.
  3. V across each = Q/9 pF = 40 V.

(a) 3 pF (b) 40 V each.
