2.9 Explain what would happen if in the capacitor given in Exercise 2.8, a 3 mm thick mica sheet (of dielectric constant = 6) were inserted between the plates, (a) while the voltage supply remained connected. (b) after the supply was disconnected.

Given

  • The capacitor of Exercise 2.8 (17.7 pF, 100 V); a mica sheet 3 mm thick, K = 6, filling the gap.

Asked

What happens (a) with the supply connected (b) after it is disconnected.

How the animation is drawn

  • Bars show the values in proportion within each panel.

Answer

  1. The sheet fills the gap, so C becomes KC₀ = 6 × 17.7 pF ≈ 106 pF in both cases.
  2. (a) The supply holds V = 100 V. Q = CV = 1.06 × 10−10 × 100 ≈ 1.06 × 10−8 C: six times the charge flows on.
  3. (b) No charge can flow: Q stays 1.77 × 10−9 C. V = Q/C = 100/6 ≈ 16.7 V (the field between the plates also falls six-fold).

(a) V = 100 V, Q rises to 1.06 × 10−8 C (b) Q = 1.77 × 10−9 C, V falls to 16.7 V.
