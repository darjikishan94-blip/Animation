2.4 A spherical conductor of radius 12 cm has a charge of 1.6 × 10–7C distributed uniformly on its surface. What is the electric field (a) inside the sphere (b) just outside the sphere (c) at a point 18 cm from the centre of the sphere?

Given

  • R = 0.12 m; Q = 1.6 × 10−7 C on the surface. (k = 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N m2 C−2; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 C2 N−1 m−2.)

Asked

E (a) inside (b) just outside (c) at 18 cm.

How the animation is drawn

  • Field arrows to scale with each other; the graph is E = kQ/r² outside.

Answer

  1. (a) Inside a conductor the electrostatic field is zero.
  2. (b) Outside, the sphere acts like a point charge at its centre: E = kQ/R2 = 9 × 109 × 1.6 × 10−7/0.122 = 105 N/C.
  3. (c) E = 9 × 109 × 1.6 × 10−7/0.182 ≈ 4.4 × 104 N/C.

(a) 0 (b) 105 N/C (c) 4.4 × 104 N/C
