2.11 A 600pF capacitor is charged by a 200V supply. It is then disconnected from the supply and is connected to another uncharged 600 pF capacitor. How much electrostatic energy is lost in the process?

Given

  • C₁ = C₂ = 600 pF; C₁ charged to 200 V; C₂ uncharged.

Asked

The energy lost.

How the animation is drawn

  • The number of + signs is in proportion to the charge; energy bars to scale.

Answer

  1. Q = C₁V = 600 × 10−12 × 200 = 1.2 × 10−7 C. Initial energy ½C₁V2 = 1.2 × 10−5 J.
  2. Joined, the charge is shared: common V = Q/(C₁ + C₂) = 100 V.
  3. Final energy ½(1200 × 10−12)(100)2 = 6 × 10−6 J.
  4. Energy lost = 6 × 10−6 J (as heat in the wires and as radiation).

6 × 10−6 J.
