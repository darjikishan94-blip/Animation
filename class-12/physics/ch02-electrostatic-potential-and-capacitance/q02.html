2.2 A regular hexagon of side 10 cm has a charge 5 μC at each of its vertices. Calculate the potential at the centre of the hexagon.

Given

  • Six charges of 5 μC at the corners of a regular hexagon of side 0.10 m. (k = 1/4πε0 = 9 × 109 N m2 C−2; ε0 = 8.854 × 10−12 C2 N−1 m−2.)

Asked

V at the centre.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. In a regular hexagon the distance from the centre to each corner equals the side: r = 0.10 m.
  2. Potentials add as numbers: V = 6kq/r = 6 × 9 × 109 × 5 × 10−6/0.10 = 2.7 × 106 V.

2.7 × 106 V.
