2.7 Three capacitors of capacitances 2 pF, 3 pF and 4 pF are connected in parallel. (a) What is the total capacitance of the combination? (b) Determine the charge on each capacitor if the combination is connected to a 100 V supply.

Given

  • 2 pF, 3 pF and 4 pF in parallel; supply 100 V.

Asked

(a) The total capacitance (b) the charge on each.

How the animation is drawn

  • The number of + signs on each plate is in proportion to its charge.

Answer

  1. (a) In parallel the capacitances add: C = 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 pF.
  2. (b) Each has the full 100 V across it: Q = CV gives 2 × 10−10 C, 3 × 10−10 C and 4 × 10−10 C.

(a) 9 pF (b) 200 pC, 300 pC, 400 pC.
