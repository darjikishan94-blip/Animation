14.4 In an unbiased p-n junction, holes diffuse from the p-region to n-region because (a) free electrons in the n-region attract them. (b) they move across the junction by the potential difference. (c) hole concentration in p-region is more as compared to n-region. (d) All the above.

Given

  • An unbiased p-n junction.

Asked

The reason for hole diffusion.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a multiple-choice question about ideas rather than one situation to follow; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. Diffusion is driven by a difference in concentration: there are far more holes on the p side than on the n side. (The junction potential actually opposes this flow.)
