14.3 Carbon, silicon and germanium have four valence electrons each. These are characterised by valence and conduction bands separated by energy band gap respectively equal to (Eg)C, (Eg)Si and (Eg)Ge. Which of the following statements is true? (a) (Eg)Si < (Eg)Ge < (Eg)C (b) (Eg)C < (Eg)Ge > (Eg)Si (c) (Eg)C > (Eg)Si > (Eg)Ge (d) (Eg)C = (Eg)Si = (Eg)Ge

Given

  • C, Si, Ge: four valence electrons each.

Asked

The correct order of band gaps.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a multiple-choice question about ideas rather than one situation to follow; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. The valence electrons are most tightly held in the smallest atom: carbon (diamond) ≈ 5.4 eV, silicon ≈ 1.1 eV, germanium ≈ 0.7 eV.
