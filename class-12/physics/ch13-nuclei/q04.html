13.4 Obtain approximately the ratio of the nuclear radii of the gold isotope 19779Au and the silver isotope 10747Ag.

Given

  • A = 197 and 107; R = R0A1/3.

Asked

RAu/RAg.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. RAu/RAg = (197/107)1/3 = (1.84)1/3 ≈ 1.23.

≈ 1.23.
