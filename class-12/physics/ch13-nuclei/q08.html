13.8 How long can an electric lamp of 100W be kept glowing by fusion of 2.0 kg of deuterium? Take the fusion reaction as 21H + 21H → 32He + n + 3.27 MeV

Given

  • 2.0 kg of deuterium (molar mass ≈ 2.014 g); 3.27 MeV per reaction (two deuterons); P = 100 W; 1 year = 3.154 × 107 s.

Asked

How long the lamp glows.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Deuterons = 2000/2.014 × 6.023 × 1023 ≈ 5.98 × 1026; reactions = half of that ≈ 2.99 × 1026.
  2. Energy ≈ 2.99 × 1026 × 3.27 MeV ≈ 9.78 × 1026 MeV ≈ 1.56 × 1014 J.
  3. t = E/P ≈ 1.56 × 1012 s ≈ 4.9 × 104 years.

≈ 4.9 × 104 years.
