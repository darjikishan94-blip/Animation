13.1 Obtain the binding energy (in MeV) of a nitrogen nucleus (147N), given m(147N) = 14.00307 u.

Given

  • m(14N) = 14.00307 u (atomic). mH = 1.007825 u; mn = 1.008665 u; 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c2; N = 6.023 × 1023 per mole; 1 MeV = 1.6 × 10−13 J.

Asked

The binding energy.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Δm = 7mH + 7mn − m(N) = 7.054775 + 7.060655 − 14.00307 = 0.11236 u (using hydrogen-atom masses balances the electrons).
  2. B = 0.11236 × 931.5 ≈ 104.7 MeV.

≈ 104.7 MeV.
