13.10 From the relation R = R0A1/3, where R0 is a constant and A is the mass number of a nucleus, show that the nuclear matter density is nearly constant (i.e. independent of A).

Given

  • R = R0A1/3; nuclear mass ≈ A × m (m ≈ 1 u).

Asked

Show ρ is independent of A.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short derivation; the page shows the question and the worked answer.

Answer

  1. Volume V = (4/3)πR3 = (4/3)πR03A.
  2. ρ = mA/V = mA/[(4/3)πR03A] = 3m/(4πR03): A cancels.
  3. With R0 ≈ 1.2 fm, ρ ≈ 2.3 × 1017 kg/m3 for every nucleus.

ρ = 3m/(4πR03), independent of A.
