13.3 A given coin has a mass of 3.0 g. Calculate the nuclear energy that would be required to separate all the neutrons and protons from each other. For simplicity assume that the coin is entirely made of 6329Cu atoms (of mass 62.92960 u).

Given

  • Coin 3.0 g of 63Cu, atomic mass 62.92960 u. mH = 1.007825 u; mn = 1.008665 u; 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c2; N = 6.023 × 1023 per mole; 1 MeV = 1.6 × 10−13 J.

Asked

The total binding energy.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Per atom: Δm = 29 × 1.007825 + 34 × 1.008665 − 62.92960 = 0.591935 u; B = 0.591935 × 931.5 ≈ 551.4 MeV.
  2. Atoms in 3.0 g: 3.0/62.9296 × 6.023 × 1023 ≈ 2.87 × 1022.
  3. Total ≈ 2.87 × 1022 × 551.4 ≈ 1.58 × 1025 MeV ≈ 2.5 × 1012 J.

≈ 1.58 × 1025 MeV ≈ 2.5 × 1012 J.
