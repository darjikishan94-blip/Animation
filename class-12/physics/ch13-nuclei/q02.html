13.2 Obtain the binding energy of the nuclei 5626Fe and 20983Bi in units of MeV from the following data: m(5626Fe) = 55.934939 u, m(20983Bi) = 208.980388 u.

Given

  • m(Fe) = 55.934939 u; m(Bi) = 208.980388 u. mH = 1.007825 u; mn = 1.008665 u; 1 u = 931.5 MeV/c2; N = 6.023 × 1023 per mole; 1 MeV = 1.6 × 10−13 J.

Asked

Binding energies of Fe-56 and Bi-209.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Fe: Δm = 26 × 1.007825 + 30 × 1.008665 − 55.934939 = 0.528461 u; B ≈ 492.3 MeV (8.79 MeV per nucleon).
  2. Bi: Δm = 83 × 1.007825 + 126 × 1.008665 − 208.980388 = 1.760877 u; B ≈ 1640.3 MeV (7.85 MeV per nucleon).
  3. Fe-56 is more tightly bound per nucleon.

Fe: ≈ 492 MeV (8.79 MeV/nucleon); Bi: ≈ 1640 MeV (7.85 MeV/nucleon).
