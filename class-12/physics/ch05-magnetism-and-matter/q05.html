5.5 A bar magnet of magnetic moment 1.5 J T–1 lies aligned with the direction of a uniform magnetic field of 0.22 T. (a) What is the amount of work required by an external torque to turn the magnet so as to align its magnetic moment: (i) normal to the field direction, (ii) opposite to the field direction? (b) What is the torque on the magnet in cases (i) and (ii)?

Given

  • m = 1.5 J T−1; B = 0.22 T; starts at θ = 0°.

Asked

(a) Work to reach (i) 90° (ii) 180° (b) torque at each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Bars show work done and torque to scale with each other.

Answer

  1. Work = increase in U = mB(cos θ₁ − cos θ₂).
  2. (a)(i) W = mB(1 − 0) = 1.5 × 0.22 = 0.33 J. (ii) W = mB(1 − (−1)) = 0.66 J.
  3. (b) τ = mB sin θ: (i) 0.33 N m (turning it back towards the field); (ii) sin 180° = 0, so τ = 0.

(a) (i) 0.33 J (ii) 0.66 J (b) (i) 0.33 N m (ii) 0.
