5.6 A closely wound solenoid of 2000 turns and area of cross-section 1.6 × 10–4 m2, carrying a current of 4.0 A, is suspended through its centre allowing it to turn in a horizontal plane. (a) What is the magnetic moment associated with the solenoid? (b) What is the force and torque on the solenoid if a uniform horizontal magnetic field of 7.5 × 10–2 T is set up at an angle of 30° with the axis of the solenoid?

Given

  • N = 2000; A = 1.6 × 10−4 m2; I = 4.0 A; B = 7.5 × 10−2 T at 30° to the axis.

Asked

(a) m (b) force and torque.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) m = NIA = 2000 × 4.0 × 1.6 × 10−4 = 1.28 A m2, along the axis.
  2. (b) In a uniform field the net force is zero. τ = mB sin θ = 1.28 × 7.5 × 10−2 × 0.5 = 4.8 × 10−2 N m, tending to align the axis with B.

(a) 1.28 A m2 (b) force 0; torque 4.8 × 10−2 N m.
