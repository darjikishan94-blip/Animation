5.7 A short bar magnet has a magnetic moment of 0.48 J T–1. Give the direction and magnitude of the magnetic field produced by the magnet at a distance of 10 cm from the centre of the magnet on (a) the axis, (b) the equatorial lines (normal bisector) of the magnet.

Given

  • m = 0.48 J T−1; r = 0.10 m; μ0/4π = 10−7 T m A−1.

Asked

B on the axis and on the equator at 10 cm.

How the animation is drawn

  • Field lines computed for a short magnet; arrows at the two points to scale with each other.

Answer

  1. (a) Axis: B = (μ0/4π) 2m/r3 = 10−7 × 2 × 0.48/0.001 = 9.6 × 10−5 T, along m (from the S end towards the N end, pointing away from N).
  2. (b) Equator: B = (μ0/4π) m/r3 = 4.8 × 10−5 T, opposite to m (parallel to the magnet, from N towards S).

(a) 9.6 × 10−5 T along m (b) 4.8 × 10−5 T opposite to m.
