5.1 A short bar magnet placed with its axis at 30° with a uniform external magnetic field of 0.25 T experiences a torque of magnitude equal to 4.5 × 10–2 J. What is the magnitude of magnetic moment of the magnet?

Given

  • θ = 30°; B = 0.25 T; τ = 4.5 × 10−2 J (N m).

Asked

The magnetic moment m.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. τ = mB sin θ, so m = τ/(B sin θ) = 4.5 × 10−2/(0.25 × 0.5) = 0.36 J T−1.

0.36 J T−1.
