5.3 A closely wound solenoid of 800 turns and area of cross section 2.5 × 10–4 m2 carries a current of 3.0 A. Explain the sense in which the solenoid acts like a bar magnet. What is its associated magnetic moment?

Given

  • N = 800; A = 2.5 × 10−4 m2; I = 3.0 A.

Asked

The analogy with a bar magnet; the magnetic moment.

How the animation is drawn

  • Outside field lines computed for a thin solenoid (equivalent to two poles at its ends).

Answer

  1. Each turn is a small current loop (a magnetic dipole). Together they give a field like a bar magnet’s: lines leave one end (north), loop round outside and re-enter the other end (south); inside they run along the axis. The solenoid also aligns itself north–south when free to turn.
  2. m = NIA = 800 × 3.0 × 2.5 × 10−4 = 0.6 J T−1, along the axis (direction by the right-hand rule).

Its field is that of a bar magnet; m = 0.6 J T−1 along the axis.
