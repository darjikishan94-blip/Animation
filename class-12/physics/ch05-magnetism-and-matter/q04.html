5.4 If the solenoid in Exercise 5.5 is free to turn about the vertical direction and a uniform horizontal magnetic field of 0.25 T is applied, what is the magnitude of torque on the solenoid when its axis makes an angle of 30° with the direction of applied field?

Given

  • The solenoid is that of Exercise 5.3 (the reprint says 5.5, a misprint): m = 0.6 J T−1. B = 0.25 T; θ = 30°.

Asked

The torque.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. τ = mB sin θ = 0.6 × 0.25 × 0.5 = 7.5 × 10−2 N m.

7.5 × 10−2 N m.
