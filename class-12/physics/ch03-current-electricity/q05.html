3.5 A silver wire has a resistance of 2.1 Ω at 27.5 °C, and a resistance of 2.7 Ω at 100 °C. Determine the temperature coefficient of resistivity of silver.

Given

  • R₁ = 2.1 Ω at 27.5 °C; R₂ = 2.7 Ω at 100 °C.

Asked

α for silver.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. α = (R₂ − R₁)/[R₁(T₂ − T₁)] = 0.6/(2.1 × 72.5) ≈ 3.9 × 10−3 °C−1.

About 0.0039 °C−1.
