3.4 A negligibly small current is passed through a wire of length 15 m and uniform cross-section 6.0 × 10–7 m2, and its resistance is measured to be 5.0 Ω. What is the resistivity of the material at the temperature of the experiment?

Given

  • l = 15 m; A = 6.0 × 10−7 m2; R = 5.0 Ω.

Asked

The resistivity ρ.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. R = ρl/A, so ρ = RA/l = 5.0 × 6.0 × 10−7/15 = 2.0 × 10−7 Ω m.

2.0 × 10−7 Ω m.
