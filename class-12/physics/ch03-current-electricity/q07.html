3.7 Determine the current in each branch of the network shown in Fig. 3.20.

Given

  • Fig. 3.20: AB = 10 Ω, BC = 5 Ω, AD = 5 Ω, DC = 10 Ω, BD = 5 Ω; a 10 V cell (negligible internal resistance) in series with 10 Ω between C and A.

Asked

The current in each branch.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circuit as in Fig. 3.20; dot speeds are in proportion to the currents.

Answer

  1. Take C as 0 V; the + side of the cell is then at 10 V. Call the unknown potentials VA, VB, VD.
  2. Junction rule (current = ΔV/R) at A: (10 − VA)/10 = (VA − VB)/10 + (VA − VD)/5.
  3. At B: (VA − VB)/10 = VB/5 + (VB − VD)/5. At D: (VA − VD)/5 + (VB − VD)/5 = VD/10.
  4. Solving: VA = 70/17 V, VB = 30/17 V, VD = 40/17 V.
  5. Currents: AB = 4/17 A, AD = 6/17 A, BC = 6/17 A, DC = 4/17 A, BD = 2/17 A flowing from D to B (opposite to the arrow in the figure), and 10/17 A through the cell and the 10 Ω resistor.

AB 4/17 A, BC 6/17 A, AD 6/17 A, DC 4/17 A, D→B 2/17 A; total 10/17 A.
