3.1 The storage battery of a car has an emf of 12 V. If the internal resistance of the battery is 0.4 Ω, what is the maximum current that can be drawn from the battery?

Given

  • ε = 12 V; r = 0.4 Ω.

Asked

The maximum current.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. The current I = ε/(R + r) is largest when the external resistance R → 0 (terminals shorted).
  2. Imax = ε/r = 12/0.4 = 30 A.

30 A.
