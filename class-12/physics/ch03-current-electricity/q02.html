3.2 A battery of emf 10 V and internal resistance 3 Ω is connected to a resistor. If the current in the circuit is 0.5 A, what is the resistance of the resistor? What is the terminal voltage of the battery when the circuit is closed?

Given

  • ε = 10 V; r = 3 Ω; I = 0.5 A.

Asked

R; the terminal voltage.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circuit schematic; the dashed box is the battery, with its internal resistance inside.

Answer

  1. I = ε/(R + r): 0.5 = 10/(R + 3), so R + 3 = 20 and R = 17 Ω.
  2. Terminal voltage V = ε − Ir = 10 − 0.5 × 3 = 8.5 V (= IR = 0.5 × 17).

R = 17 Ω; terminal voltage 8.5 V.
