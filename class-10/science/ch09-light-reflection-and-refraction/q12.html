9.12 An object is placed at a distance of 10 cm from a convex mirror of focal length 15 cm. Find the position and nature of the image.

Given

  • u = −10 cm; f = +15 cm. New Cartesian sign convention: distances from the pole/optical centre, positive along the incident light; heights positive upward.

Asked

Position and nature of the image.

How the animation is drawn

  • 12 px per cm; object height illustrative.

Answer

  1. 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/15 + 1/10 = 1/6, so v = +6 cm (behind the mirror).
  2. m = −v/u = 0.6: virtual, erect, diminished.

6 cm behind the mirror; virtual, erect, diminished.
