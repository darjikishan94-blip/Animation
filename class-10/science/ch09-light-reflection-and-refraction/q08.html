9.8 Name the type of mirror used in the following situations. (a) Headlights of a car. (b) Side/rear-view mirror of a vehicle. (c) Solar furnace. Support your answer with reason.

Given

  • Three uses.

Asked

Mirror type and reason for each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic; sizes illustrative.

Answer

  1. (a) Concave: with the bulb at its focus, it sends out a strong parallel beam.
  2. (b) Convex: it always gives an erect (diminished) image and shows a much wider field of view.
  3. (c) Concave: it concentrates parallel sunlight at its focus, producing a high temperature there.

(a) concave (b) convex (c) concave.
