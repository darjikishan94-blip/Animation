9.11 A concave lens of focal length 15 cm forms an image 10 cm from the lens. How far is the object placed from the lens? Draw the ray diagram.

Given

  • f = −15 cm; v = −10 cm (a concave lens gives a virtual image on the object’s side). New Cartesian sign convention: distances from the pole/optical centre, positive along the incident light; heights positive upward.

Asked

u; ray diagram.

How the animation is drawn

  • 9 px per cm; object height illustrative.

Answer

  1. 1/u = 1/v − 1/f = −1/10 + 1/15 = −1/30, so u = −30 cm.
  2. The object is 30 cm in front of the lens; the image is virtual, erect and one-third its size (m = v/u = 1/3).

30 cm from the lens.
