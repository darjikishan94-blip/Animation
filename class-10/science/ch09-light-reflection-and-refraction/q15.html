9.15 An object of size 7.0 cm is placed at 27 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 18 cm. At what distance from the mirror should a screen be placed, so that a sharp focussed image can be obtained? Find the size and the nature of the image.

Given

  • h = 7.0 cm; u = −27 cm; f = −18 cm. New Cartesian sign convention: distances from the pole/optical centre, positive along the incident light; heights positive upward.

Asked

Screen distance; size and nature of the image.

How the animation is drawn

  • Distances 6 px per cm; heights 8 px per cm (straight lines and meeting points are not affected).

Answer

  1. 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/18 + 1/27 = −1/54, so v = −54 cm: the screen goes 54 cm in front of the mirror.
  2. m = −v/u = −2, so h′ = −14 cm.
  3. Real, inverted, enlarged (14 cm).

54 cm in front; 14 cm; real, inverted, enlarged.
