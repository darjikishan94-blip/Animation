9.10 An object 5 cm in length is held 25 cm away from a converging lens of focal length 10 cm. Draw the ray diagram and find the position, size and the nature of the image formed.

Given

  • h = 5 cm; u = −25 cm; f = +10 cm. New Cartesian sign convention: distances from the pole/optical centre, positive along the incident light; heights positive upward.

Asked

Position, size and nature of the image.

How the animation is drawn

  • 10 px per cm in both directions.

Answer

  1. 1/v − 1/u = 1/f: 1/v = 1/10 − 1/25 = 3/50, so v = +16.7 cm (on the other side of the lens).
  2. m = v/u = 16.7/(−25) = −0.67, so h′ = −3.3 cm.
  3. Real, inverted, diminished: 3.3 cm tall, 16.7 cm behind the lens.

16.7 cm behind the lens; 3.3 cm; real, inverted, diminished.
