9.9 One-half of a convex lens is covered with a black paper. Will this lens produce a complete image of the object? Verify your answer experimentally. Explain your observations.

Given

  • Convex lens with its upper half covered.

Asked

Is the image complete? Why?

How the animation is drawn

  • Distances illustrative (object beyond 2F).

Answer

  1. Yes, the complete image is formed.
  2. Rays from every point of the object pass through every part of the lens. Covering half the lens blocks half of these rays, but the rest still converge to the same image points.
  3. The image is complete but less bright, since only about half the light reaches it. (Experiment: cover half a lens, focus a candle flame on a screen; the full flame is seen, dimmer.)

Yes: a complete but dimmer image.
