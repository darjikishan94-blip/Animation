9.7 We wish to obtain an erect image of an object, using a concave mirror of focal length 15 cm. What should be the range of distance of the object from the mirror? What is the nature of the image? Is the image larger or smaller than the object? Draw a ray diagram to show the image formation in this case.

Given

  • Concave mirror, f = 15 cm.

Asked

Range of object distance; nature and size of the image; ray diagram.

How the animation is drawn

  • 7 px per cm. The object is drawn at 10 cm (and then moved within the range); its height is illustrative.

Answer

  1. The image is erect only when the object is between the pole P and the focus F: 0 < distance < 15 cm.
  2. The image is then virtual (behind the mirror), erect and larger than the object.
  3. Ray diagram: a ray parallel to the axis reflects through F; a ray to the pole reflects symmetrically. The reflected rays diverge; extended backwards they meet behind the mirror.

Between P and F (less than 15 cm); virtual, erect, enlarged.
