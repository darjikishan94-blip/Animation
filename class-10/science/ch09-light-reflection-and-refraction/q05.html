9.5 No matter how far you stand from a mirror, your image appears erect. The mirror is likely to be (a) only plane. (b) only concave. (c) only convex. (d) either plane or convex.

Given

  • Image erect at every distance.

Asked

Type of mirror.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a multiple-choice question about ideas rather than one situation to follow; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. A plane mirror and a convex mirror always give virtual, erect images. A concave mirror gives inverted images for objects beyond F.

(d) either plane or convex.
