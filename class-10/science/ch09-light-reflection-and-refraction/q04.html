9.4 A spherical mirror and a thin spherical lens have each a focal length of –15 cm. The mirror and the lens are likely to be (a) both concave. (b) both convex. (c) the mirror is concave and the lens is convex. (d) the mirror is convex, but the lens is concave.

Given

  • f = −15 cm for both. New Cartesian sign convention: distances from the pole/optical centre, positive along the incident light; heights positive upward.

Asked

Their types.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a multiple-choice question about ideas rather than one situation to follow; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. In the sign convention a concave mirror has a negative focal length (F is in front), and a concave (diverging) lens also has a negative focal length.

(a) both concave.
