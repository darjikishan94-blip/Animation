9.13 The magnification produced by a plane mirror is +1. What does this mean?

Given

  • m = +1.

Asked

Its meaning.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. m = h′/h = 1: the image is the same size as the object.
  2. The + sign: the image is erect (and hence virtual).

Same size, erect (virtual).
