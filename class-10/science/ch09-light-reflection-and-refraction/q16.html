9.16 Find the focal length of a lens of power – 2.0 D. What type of lens is this?

Given

  • P = −2.0 D.

Asked

f; lens type.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. f = 1/P = 1/(−2.0) = −0.50 m = −50 cm.
  2. Negative focal length: a concave (diverging) lens.

−50 cm; concave lens.
