9.14 An object 5.0 cm in length is placed at a distance of 20 cm in front of a convex mirror of radius of curvature 30 cm. Find the position of the image, its nature and size.

Given

  • h = 5.0 cm; u = −20 cm; R = +30 cm, f = +15 cm. New Cartesian sign convention: distances from the pole/optical centre, positive along the incident light; heights positive upward.

Asked

Position, nature and size.

How the animation is drawn

  • 10 px per cm in both directions.

Answer

  1. 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = 1/15 + 1/20 = 7/60, so v = +8.6 cm (behind the mirror).
  2. m = −v/u = 8.6/20 = 0.43, so h′ = 2.1 cm.
  3. Virtual, erect, diminished.

8.6 cm behind; virtual, erect; 2.1 cm.
