9.17 A doctor has prescribed a corrective lens of power +1.5 D. Find the focal length of the lens. Is the prescribed lens diverging or converging?

Given

  • P = +1.5 D.

Asked

f; converging or diverging?

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. f = 1/P = 1/1.5 ≈ 0.67 m = +66.7 cm.
  2. Positive: a converging (convex) lens.

+66.7 cm; converging.
