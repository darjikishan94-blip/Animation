12.7 State the rule to determine the direction of a (i) magnetic field produced around a straight conductor-carrying current, (ii) force experienced by a current-carrying straight conductor placed in a magnetic field which is perpendicular to it, and (iii) current induced in a coil due to its rotation in a magnetic field.

Given

  • Three situations.

Asked

The rule for each.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. (i) Right-hand thumb rule: hold the conductor in the right hand with the thumb along the current; the fingers curl in the direction of the field.
  2. (ii) Fleming’s left-hand rule: forefinger along the field, middle finger along the current, thumb gives the force (motion), all mutually perpendicular.
  3. (iii) Fleming’s right-hand rule: forefinger along the field, thumb along the motion of the conductor, middle finger gives the induced current.

(i) right-hand thumb rule (ii) Fleming’s left-hand rule (iii) Fleming’s right-hand rule.
