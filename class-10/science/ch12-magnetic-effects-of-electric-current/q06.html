12.6 Imagine that you are sitting in a chamber with your back to one wall. An electron beam, moving horizontally from back wall towards the front wall, is deflected by a strong magnetic field to your right side. What is the direction of magnetic field?

Given

  • Electrons move from the back wall to the front wall (horizontal); force to the right.

Asked

Direction of B.

How the animation is drawn

  • View from above; the field (vertically down) points into the page.

Answer

  1. Electrons are negative, so the conventional current points the opposite way: from the front wall towards the back wall.
  2. Fleming’s left-hand rule: force (thumb) to the right, current (middle finger) towards the back wall; the forefinger (field) then points vertically downward.

Vertically downward.
