12.1 Which of the following correctly describes the magnetic field near a long straight wire? (a) The field consists of straight lines perpendicular to the wire. (b) The field consists of straight lines parallel to the wire. (c) The field consists of radial lines originating from the wire. (d) The field consists of concentric circles centred on the wire.

Given

  • A long straight current-carrying wire.

Asked

The shape of the field lines.

How the animation is drawn

  • Card seen in perspective; compass needles follow the field.

Answer

  1. The field lines are concentric circles centred on the wire, in planes perpendicular to it; their direction is given by the right-hand thumb rule (current up → anticlockwise seen from above).
