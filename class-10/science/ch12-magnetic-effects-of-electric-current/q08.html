12.8 When does an electric short circuit occur?

Given

  • Household wiring.

Asked

When a short circuit occurs.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. When the live wire and the neutral wire come into direct contact (for example through damaged insulation or a fault in an appliance). The resistance becomes very small and a very large current flows.
