12.9 What is the function of an earth wire? Why is it necessary to earth metallic appliances?

Given

  • A metal-bodied appliance with a fault.

Asked

The function of the earth wire; why earthing is needed.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic.

Answer

  1. The earth wire connects the metal body of an appliance to the earth through a low-resistance path.
  2. If the live wire touches the metal body by a fault, the leakage current flows to earth through this path instead of through a person, and the large current blows the fuse. The body stays at earth potential, so the user does not get a severe shock.

A low-resistance path to earth for leakage current; it protects the user from shocks.
