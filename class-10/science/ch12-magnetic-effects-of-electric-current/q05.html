12.5 When is the force experienced by a current–carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field largest?

Given

  • A straight conductor carrying current in a uniform field.

Asked

When the force is largest.

How the animation is drawn

  • The graph is F/Fmax = sin θ.

Answer

  1. F = BIL sin θ, where θ is the angle between the conductor (current) and the field.
  2. It is largest when θ = 90°: the current is perpendicular to the magnetic field.

When the current is perpendicular to the field.
