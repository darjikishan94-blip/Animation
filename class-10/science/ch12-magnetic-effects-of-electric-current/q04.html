12.4 List two methods of producing magnetic fields.

Given

  • —

Asked

Two methods.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. (1) Using a permanent magnet (a bar or horseshoe magnet).
  2. (2) Passing an electric current through a conductor — a straight wire, a circular loop or a solenoid (an electromagnet).

Permanent magnets; current-carrying conductors (electromagnets).
