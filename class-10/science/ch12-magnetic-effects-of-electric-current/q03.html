12.3 State whether the following statements are true or false. (a) The field at the centre of a long circular coil carrying current will be parallel straight lines. (b) A wire with a green insulation is usually the live wire of an electric supply.

Given

  • Two statements.

Asked

True or false.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a multiple-choice or true/false question about ideas; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. (a) True: inside a long coil (solenoid) the field lines are parallel straight lines — the field is uniform.
  2. (b) False: green insulation marks the earth wire; the live wire usually has red (or brown) insulation.

(a) True (b) False
