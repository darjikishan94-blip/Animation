11.9 A battery of 9 V is connected in series with resistors of 0.2 Ω, 0.3 Ω, 0.4 Ω, 0.5 Ω and 12 Ω, respectively. How much current would flow through the 12 Ω resistor?

Given

  • 9 V; 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5 and 12 Ω in series.

Asked

Current through the 12 Ω resistor.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circuit schematic.

Answer

  1. Total resistance = 0.2 + 0.3 + 0.4 + 0.5 + 12 = 13.4 Ω.
  2. I = 9/13.4 ≈ 0.67 A, the same through every resistor in series.

≈ 0.67 A.
