11.16 Which uses more energy, a 250 W TV set in 1 hr, or a 1200 W toaster in 10 minutes?

Given

  • TV: 250 W, 1 h. Toaster: 1200 W, 10 min.

Asked

Which uses more energy.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. TV: E = 250 × 3600 = 9.0 × 105 J (250 Wh).
  2. Toaster: E = 1200 × 600 = 7.2 × 105 J (200 Wh).
  3. The TV uses more.

The TV (9.0 × 105 J against 7.2 × 105 J).
