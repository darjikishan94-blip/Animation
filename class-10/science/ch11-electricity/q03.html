11.3 An electric bulb is rated 220 V and 100 W. When it is operated on 110 V, the power consumed will be – (a) 100 W (b) 75 W (c) 50 W (d) 25 W

Given

  • Rating 220 V, 100 W; used at 110 V.

Asked

Power at 110 V.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. R = V2/P = 2202/100 = 484 Ω (taken as constant).
  2. P = 1102/484 = 25 W: half the voltage gives a quarter of the power.

(d) 25 W
