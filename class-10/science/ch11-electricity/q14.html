11.14 Compare the power used in the 2 Ω resistor in each of the following circuits: (i) a 6 V battery in series with 1 Ω and 2 Ω resistors, and (ii) a 4 V battery in parallel with 12 Ω and 2 Ω resistors.

Given

  • (i) 6 V; 1 Ω and 2 Ω in series. (ii) 4 V; 12 Ω and 2 Ω in parallel.

Asked

Power in the 2 Ω resistor in each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circuit schematics.

Answer

  1. (i) I = 6/(1 + 2) = 2 A; P = I2R = 4 × 2 = 8 W.
  2. (ii) The 2 Ω resistor has the full 4 V across it: P = V2/R = 16/2 = 8 W.
  3. The power is the same, 8 W, in both.

8 W in both: equal.
