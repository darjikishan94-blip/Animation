11.15 Two lamps, one rated 100 W at 220 V, and the other 60 W at 220 V, are connected in parallel to electric mains supply. What current is drawn from the line if the supply voltage is 220 V?

Given

  • 100 W and 60 W lamps (220 V) in parallel on 220 V.

Asked

Total current.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Both get 220 V, so total power = 160 W.
  2. I = P/V = 160/220 ≈ 0.73 A.

≈ 0.73 A.
