11.7 The values of current I flowing in a given resistor for the corresponding values of potential difference V across the resistor are given below – I (amperes): 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0; V (volts): 1.6, 3.4, 6.7, 10.2, 13.2. Plot a graph between V and I and calculate the resistance of that resistor.

Given

  • I = 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0 A; V = 1.6, 3.4, 6.7, 10.2, 13.2 V.

Asked

The V–I graph; R.

How the animation is drawn

  • Points plotted to scale; the line is the best straight line through the origin.

Answer

  1. The points lie close to a straight line through the origin (Ohm’s law).
  2. R = slope = V/I: 3.2, 3.4, 3.35, 3.4, 3.3 Ω from the readings; the best line through the origin gives ≈ 3.3 Ω (the NCERT answer quotes 3.4 Ω).

R ≈ 3.3–3.4 Ω.
