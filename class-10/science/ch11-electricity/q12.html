11.12 Several electric bulbs designed to be used on a 220 V electric supply line, are rated 10 W. How many lamps can be connected in parallel with each other across the two wires of 220 V line if the maximum allowable current is 5 A?

Given

  • Lamps 10 W, 220 V; maximum 5 A.

Asked

Number of lamps.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Each lamp draws I = P/V = 10/220 = 1/22 A.
  2. n = 5/(1/22) = 110 lamps. (Equivalently, total power 220 × 5 = 1100 W = 110 × 10 W.)

110.
