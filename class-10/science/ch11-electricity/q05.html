11.5 How is a voltmeter connected in the circuit to measure the potential difference between two points?

Given

  • A voltmeter.

Asked

How it is connected.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for short explanations; the page shows the question and the answers.

Answer

  1. In parallel, across the two points (its + terminal to the point at higher potential). It has a very high resistance, so it draws very little current.

In parallel across the two points.
