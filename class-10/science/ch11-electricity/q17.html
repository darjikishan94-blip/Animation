11.17 An electric heater of resistance 44 Ω draws 5 A from the service mains for 2 hours. Calculate the rate at which heat is developed in the heater.

Given

  • R = 44 Ω; I = 5 A; 2 h.

Asked

The rate of heating (power).

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. P = I2R = 25 × 44 = 1100 W (the 2 hours do not affect the rate; total heat = 1100 × 7200 ≈ 7.9 × 106 J).

1100 W.
