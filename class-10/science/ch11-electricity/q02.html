11.2 Which of the following terms does not represent electrical power in a circuit? (a) I2R (b) IR2 (c) VI (d) V2/R

Given

  • P = VI with V = IR.

Asked

The expression that is not power.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a multiple-choice question; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. P = VI = I2R = V2/R. IR2 has the wrong units (V Ω), so it is not power.

(b) IR2
