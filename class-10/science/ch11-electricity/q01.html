11.1 A piece of wire of resistance R is cut into five equal parts. These parts are then connected in parallel. If the equivalent resistance of this combination is R′, then the ratio R/R′ is – (a) 1/25 (b) 1/5 (c) 5 (d) 25

Given

  • Wire of resistance R cut into 5 equal parts, joined in parallel.

Asked

R/R′.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic.

Answer

  1. Each piece is one-fifth as long, so its resistance is R/5.
  2. Five equal resistors in parallel: R′ = (R/5)/5 = R/25.
  3. R/R′ = 25.

(d) 25
