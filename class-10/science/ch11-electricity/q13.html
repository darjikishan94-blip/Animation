11.13 A hot plate of an electric oven connected to a 220 V line has two resistance coils A and B, each of 24 Ω resistance, which may be used separately, in series, or in parallel. What are the currents in the three cases?

Given

  • Coils A and B, 24 Ω each; 220 V.

Asked

Current used separately, in series, in parallel.

How the animation is drawn

  • Dot speeds in proportion to the currents.

Answer

  1. Separately: I = 220/24 ≈ 9.2 A.
  2. In series (48 Ω): I = 220/48 ≈ 4.6 A.
  3. In parallel (12 Ω): I = 220/12 ≈ 18.3 A.

9.2 A; 4.6 A; 18.3 A.
