11.11 Show how you would connect three resistors, each of resistance 6 Ω, so that the combination has a resistance of (i) 9 Ω, (ii) 4 Ω.

Given

  • Three resistors of 6 Ω.

Asked

Arrangements giving 9 Ω and 4 Ω.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic; sizes illustrative.

Answer

  1. (i) Two in parallel: 6 × 6/(6 + 6) = 3 Ω; in series with the third: 3 + 6 = 9 Ω.
  2. (ii) Two in series: 12 Ω; in parallel with the third: 12 × 6/(12 + 6) = 4 Ω.

(i) (6 ∥ 6) + 6 (ii) (6 + 6) ∥ 6
