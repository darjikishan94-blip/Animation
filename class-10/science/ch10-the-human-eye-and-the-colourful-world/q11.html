10.11 Explain why the planets do not twinkle.

Given

  • Planets seen through the atmosphere.

Asked

Why no twinkling.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. Planets are much closer and look like small discs: extended sources made of many point sources.
  2. The fluctuations from the different points average out, so the total light reaching the eye stays steady.

They are extended sources; the fluctuations average out.
