10.7 Make a diagram to show how hypermetropia is corrected. The near point of a hypermetropic eye is 1 m. What is the power of the lens required to correct this defect? Assume that the near point of the normal eye is 25 cm.

Given

  • Near point of the defective eye 100 cm; normal near point 25 cm.

Asked

Correction diagram; power of the lens.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic: the eye is enlarged and distances are not to scale.

Answer

  1. An object at 25 cm must give a virtual image at the eye’s near point: u = −25 cm, v = −100 cm.
  2. 1/f = 1/v − 1/u = −1/100 + 1/25 = 3/100, so f = +33.3 cm: a convex lens.
  3. P = 1/0.333 m = +3.0 D.

Convex lens, +3.0 D.
