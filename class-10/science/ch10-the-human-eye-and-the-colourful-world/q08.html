10.8 Why is a normal eye not able to see clearly the objects placed closer than 25 cm?

Given

  • A normal eye.

Asked

The reason.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. The ciliary muscles can make the eye lens only so thick: its focal length cannot be reduced below a certain minimum. For an object closer than about 25 cm the image would form behind the retina, so it looks blurred.

The eye lens cannot be made any more powerful (limited accommodation).
