10.10 Why do stars twinkle?

Given

  • Starlight passing through the atmosphere.

Asked

The reason for twinkling.

How the animation is drawn

  • Bending greatly exaggerated.

Answer

  1. Starlight is refracted continuously as it passes through the atmosphere, whose refractive index varies from place to place and moment to moment (moving air, changing temperature and density).
  2. A star is so far away that it is practically a point source; the slightly changing path makes its apparent position and the amount of light reaching the eye fluctuate. The star twinkles.

Changing atmospheric refraction of light from a point-like source.
