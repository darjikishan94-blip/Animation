10.6 The far point of a myopic person is 80 cm in front of the eye. What is the nature and power of the lens required to correct the problem?

Given

  • Far point 80 cm.

Asked

Nature and power of the lens.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic: the eye is enlarged and distances are not to scale.

Answer

  1. The lens must make parallel rays from a distant object appear to come from the far point: an object at infinity must give a virtual image at v = −80 cm.
  2. 1/f = 1/v − 1/u = 1/(−80) − 0, so f = −80 cm: a concave (diverging) lens.
  3. P = 1/f = 1/(−0.80 m) = −1.25 D.

Concave lens, −1.25 D.
