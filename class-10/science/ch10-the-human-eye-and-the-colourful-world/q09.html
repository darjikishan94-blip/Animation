10.9 What happens to the image distance in the eye when we increase the distance of an object from the eye?

Given

  • An object moved away from the eye.

Asked

What happens to the image distance.

How the animation is drawn

  • Schematic; the lens thickness shows its changing focal length.

Answer

  1. Nothing: the image is always formed on the retina, so the image distance (lens to retina) stays the same.
  2. Instead the ciliary muscles relax and the eye lens becomes thinner, increasing its focal length (accommodation).

It stays the same; the focal length of the eye lens increases.
