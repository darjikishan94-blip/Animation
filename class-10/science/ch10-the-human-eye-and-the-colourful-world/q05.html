10.5 A person needs a lens of power –5.5 dioptres for correcting his distant vision. For correcting his near vision he needs a lens of power +1.5 dioptre. What is the focal length of the lens required for correcting (i) distant vision, and (ii) near vision?

Given

  • P₁ = −5.5 D (distant vision); P₂ = +1.5 D (near vision).

Asked

The two focal lengths.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (i) f = 1/P = 1/(−5.5) ≈ −0.182 m = −18.2 cm (concave lens).
  2. (ii) f = 1/1.5 ≈ 0.667 m = +66.7 cm (convex lens).

(i) −18.2 cm (ii) +66.7 cm.
