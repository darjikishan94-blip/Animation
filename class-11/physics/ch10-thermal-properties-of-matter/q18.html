10.18 A brass boiler has a base area of 0.15 m2 and thickness 1.0 cm. It boils water at the rate of 6.0 kg/min when placed on a gas stove. Estimate the temperature of the part of the flame in contact with the boiler. Thermal conductivity of brass = 109 J s–1 m–1 K–1 ; Heat of vaporisation of water = 2256 × 103 J kg–1.

Given

  • A = 0.15 m2, d = 1.0 cm; 6.0 kg/min boiled; k = 109 W m−1 K−1; Lv = 2256 × 103 J kg−1.

Asked

The flame temperature at the base.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Heat flow H = (6.0/60) × 2256 × 103 ≈ 2.26 × 105 W.
  2. H = kA(T − 100)/d: T − 100 = 2.26 × 105 × 0.010/(109 × 0.15) ≈ 138 °C.
  3. T ≈ 238 °C.
