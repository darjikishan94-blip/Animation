10.8 A hole is drilled in a copper sheet. The diameter of the hole is 4.24 cm at 27.0 °C. What is the change in the diameter of the hole when the sheet is heated to 227 °C? Coefficient of linear expansion of copper = 1.70 × 10–5 K–1.

Given

  • Hole 4.24 cm at 27.0 °C; heated to 227 °C; αCu = 1.70 × 10−5 K−1.

Asked

The change in the hole’s diameter.

How the animation is drawn

  • Expansion exaggerated about 100 times.

Answer

  1. A hole expands exactly as a disc of the same material would: every length in the sheet grows by the factor (1 + αΔT).
  2. Δd = 4.24 × 1.70 × 10−5 × 200 ≈ 1.44 × 10−2 cm (an increase).

It increases by about 0.0144 cm.
