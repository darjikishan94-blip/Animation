10.3 The electrical resistance in ohms of a certain thermometer varies with temperature according to the approximate law : R = Ro [1 + α (T – To)] The resistance is 101.6 Ω at the triple-point of water 273.16 K, and 165.5 Ω at the normal melting point of lead (600.5 K). What is the temperature when the resistance is 123.4 Ω ?

Given

  • R = 101.6 Ω at 273.16 K; 165.5 Ω at 600.5 K.

Asked

T when R = 123.4 Ω.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. 165.5 = 101.6[1 + α(600.5 − 273.16)] gives α ≈ 1.92 × 10−3 K−1.
  2. 123.4 = 101.6[1 + α(T − 273.16)] gives T − 273.16 = (123.4/101.6 − 1)/α ≈ 111.7 K.
  3. T ≈ 384.8 K.
