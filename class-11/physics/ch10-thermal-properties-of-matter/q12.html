10.12 A 10 kW drilling machine is used to drill a bore in a small aluminium block of mass 8.0 kg. How much is the rise in temperature of the block in 2.5 minutes, assuming 50% of power is used up in heating the machine itself or lost to the surroundings. Specific heat of aluminium = 0.91 J g–1 K–1.

Given

  • Power 10 kW; 50% into the block; 2.5 min; m = 8.0 kg; c = 0.91 J g−1 K−1.

Asked

The temperature rise.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Heat into the block = 0.5 × 104 × 150 = 7.5 × 105 J.
  2. ΔT = Q/(mc) = 7.5 × 105/(8000 × 0.91) ≈ 103 °C.

About 103 °C.
