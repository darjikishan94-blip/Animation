10.20 A body cools from 80 °C to 50 °C in 5 minutes. Calculate the time it takes to cool from 60 °C to 30 °C. The temperature of the surroundings is 20 °C.

Given

  • 80 °C → 50 °C in 5 min; surroundings 20 °C.

Asked

The time to cool from 60 °C to 30 °C.

How the animation is drawn

  • Curves drawn with the exact exponential cooling law.

Answer

  1. Newton’s law (average form, as in NCERT): (rate of fall) = K × (average temperature − 20 °C).
  2. First: 30/5 = K(65 − 20), so K = 2/15 per min.
  3. Second: 30/t = (2/15)(45 − 20) = 10/3 per min, so t = 9 min.
  4. (Solving the law exactly, T − 20 falls exponentially: 60 → 30 halves the excess, 40 → 10 quarters it, so it takes 2 × 5 = 10 min. The average form is an approximation.)

9 min by NCERT’s method (about 10 min by the exact exponential law).
