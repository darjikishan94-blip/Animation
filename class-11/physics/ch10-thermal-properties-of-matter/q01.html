10.1 The triple points of neon and carbon dioxide are 24.57 K and 216.55 K respectively. Express these temperatures on the Celsius and Fahrenheit scales.

Given

  • Neon 24.57 K; carbon dioxide 216.55 K.

Asked

Both temperatures in °C and °F.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. tC = T − 273.15; tF = (9/5) tC + 32.
  2. Neon: 24.57 − 273.15 = −248.58 °C; (9/5)(−248.58) + 32 = −415.44 °F.
  3. CO2: 216.55 − 273.15 = −56.60 °C; (9/5)(−56.60) + 32 = −69.88 °F.

Neon: −248.58 °C, −415.44 °F. CO2: −56.60 °C, −69.88 °F.
