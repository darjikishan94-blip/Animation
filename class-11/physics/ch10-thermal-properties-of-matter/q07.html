10.7 A large steel wheel is to be fitted on to a shaft of the same material. At 27 °C, the outer diameter of the shaft is 8.70 cm and the diameter of the central hole in the wheel is 8.69 cm. The shaft is cooled using ‘dry ice’. At what temperature of the shaft does the wheel slip on the shaft? Assume coefficient of linear expansion of the steel to be constant over the required temperature range : αsteel = 1.20 × 10–5 K–1.

Given

  • Shaft 8.70 cm, hole 8.69 cm at 27 °C; α = 1.20 × 10−5 K−1.

Asked

The temperature at which the shaft fits.

How the animation is drawn

  • The shrinkage is exaggerated.

Answer

  1. The shaft must shrink by 0.01 cm: Δd = dαΔT, so ΔT = −0.01/(8.70 × 1.20 × 10−5) ≈ −96 K.
  2. T = 27 − 96 ≈ −69 °C.

About −69 °C.
