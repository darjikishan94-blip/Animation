10.13 A copper block of mass 2.5 kg is heated in a furnace to a temperature of 500 °C and then placed on a large ice block. What is the maximum amount of ice that can melt? (Specific heat of copper = 0.39 J g–1 K–1; heat of fusion of water = 335 J g–1 ).

Given

  • Copper 2.5 kg at 500 °C; c = 0.39 J g−1 K−1; Lf = 335 J g−1.

Asked

The greatest mass of ice that can melt.

How the animation is drawn

  • Ice block not to scale.

Answer

  1. The copper can give out at most mcΔT, cooling to 0 °C: 2500 × 0.39 × 500 = 4.875 × 105 J.
  2. Ice melted = 4.875 × 105/335 ≈ 1.46 × 103 g ≈ 1.5 kg.

About 1.5 kg.
