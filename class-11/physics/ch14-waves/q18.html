14.18 Two sitar strings A and B playing the note ‘Ga’ are slightly out of tune and produce beats of frequency 6 Hz. The tension in the string A is slightly reduced and the beat frequency is found to reduce to 3 Hz. If the original frequency of A is 324 Hz, what is the frequency of B?

Given

  • Beats 6 Hz; fA = 324 Hz; reducing A’s tension makes the beats 3 Hz.

Asked

The frequency of B.

How the animation is drawn

  • Beat envelopes slowed 4 times; the waves inside are not to scale.

Answer

  1. fB = 324 ± 6 = 318 Hz or 330 Hz.
  2. Less tension lowers fA. If fB were 330 Hz, the beats would increase; they decrease, so fB is below fA.
  3. fB = 318 Hz (A drops to 321 Hz).

318 Hz.
