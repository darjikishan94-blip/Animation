14.12 (i) For the wave on a string described in Exercise 14.11, do all the points on the string oscillate with the same (a) frequency, (b) phase, (c) amplitude? Explain your answers. (ii) What is the amplitude of a point 0.375 m away from one end?

Given

  • y = 0.06 sin(2πx/3) cos(120πt), 1.5 m string.

Asked

(i) frequency, phase, amplitude of the points (ii) the amplitude at 0.375 m.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for explanations or a classification rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (i) (a) Yes: every point (except the nodes at the ends, which do not move) oscillates at 60 Hz.
  2. (b) Yes: the string is one loop (1.5 m = λ/2), and all points in one loop move in phase.
  3. (c) No: the amplitude is 0.06 sin(2πx/3), zero at the ends and greatest (0.06 m) at the middle.
  4. (ii) x = 0.375 m: amplitude = 0.06 × sin(π/4) ≈ 0.042 m.

(i) same frequency, same phase, different amplitudes (ii) ≈ 0.042 m
