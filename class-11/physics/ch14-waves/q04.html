14.4 Use the formula v = √(γP/ρ) to explain why the speed of sound in air (a) is independent of pressure, (b) increases with temperature, (c) increases with humidity.

Given

  • v = √(γP/ρ); for an ideal gas P/ρ = RT/M.

Asked

Explanations.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for explanations or a classification rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) At constant temperature, P/ρ = RT/M is constant: raising P raises ρ in proportion, so v is unchanged.
  2. (b) v = √(γRT/M) ∝ √T.
  3. (c) Water vapour (M = 18) is lighter than air (M ≈ 29); moist air has a smaller average molar mass, so a smaller density at the same P and T, and v increases.

v = √(γRT/M): independent of P, grows with T, grows as M falls.
