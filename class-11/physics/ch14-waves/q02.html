14.2 A stone dropped from the top of a tower of height 300 m splashes into the water of a pond near the base of the tower. When is the splash heard at the top given that the speed of sound in air is 340 m s–1 ? (g = 9.8 m s–2)

Given

  • Height 300 m; vsound = 340 m s−1; g = 9.8 m s−2.

Asked

When the splash is heard at the top.

How the animation is drawn

  • Real time; 1.2 px per metre.

Answer

  1. Fall: t₁ = √(2h/g) = √(2 × 300/9.8) ≈ 7.82 s.
  2. Sound coming up: t₂ = 300/340 ≈ 0.88 s.
  3. Total ≈ 8.7 s after the stone is dropped.

About 8.7 s.
