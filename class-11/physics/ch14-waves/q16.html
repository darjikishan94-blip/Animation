14.16 A steel rod 100 cm long is clamped at its middle. The fundamental frequency of longitudinal vibrations of the rod are given to be 2.53 kHz. What is the speed of sound in steel?

Given

  • L = 100 cm, clamped at the middle; fundamental 2.53 kHz.

Asked

The speed of sound in steel.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Clamped at the middle: a node there and antinodes at the free ends, so L = λ/2 and λ = 2.0 m.
  2. v = fλ = 2.53 × 103 × 2.0 ≈ 5.06 × 103 m s−1.

About 5.06 km s−1.
