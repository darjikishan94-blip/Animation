14.10 For the travelling harmonic wave y(x, t) = 2.0 cos 2π (10t – 0.0080 x + 0.35) where x and y are in cm and t in s. Calculate the phase difference between oscillatory motion of two points separated by a distance of (a) 4 m, (b) 0.5 m, (c) λ/2, (d) 3λ/4

Given

  • y = 2.0 cos 2π(10t − 0.0080x + 0.35), x in cm.

Asked

Phase differences for the four separations.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. k = 2π × 0.0080 rad cm−1, so Δφ = 2π × 0.0080 × Δx (Δx in cm); λ = 1/0.0080 = 125 cm.
  2. (a) 400 cm: Δφ = 2π × 3.2 = 6.4π rad.
  3. (b) 50 cm: Δφ = 2π × 0.4 = 0.8π rad.
  4. (c) λ/2: π rad.
  5. (d) 3λ/4: 1.5π rad.

(a) 6.4π (b) 0.8π (c) π (d) 1.5π rad
