14.15 A metre-long tube open at one end, with a movable piston at the other end, shows resonance with a fixed frequency source (a tuning fork of frequency 340 Hz) when the tube length is 25.5 cm or 79.3 cm. Estimate the speed of sound in air at the temperature of the experiment. The edge effects may be neglected.

Given

  • f = 340 Hz; resonance at 25.5 cm and 79.3 cm; edge effects neglected.

Asked

The speed of sound.

How the animation is drawn

  • Standing-wave pattern shown as displacement amplitude; 4 px per cm.

Answer

  1. A tube closed at one end resonates at L = λ/4, 3λ/4, …: successive lengths differ by λ/2.
  2. λ/2 = 79.3 − 25.5 = 53.8 cm, so λ = 107.6 cm.
  3. v = fλ = 340 × 1.076 ≈ 366 m s−1.

About 366 m s−1.
