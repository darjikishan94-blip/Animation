14.3 A steel wire has a length of 12.0 m and a mass of 2.10 kg. What should be the tension in the wire so that speed of a transverse wave on the wire equals the speed of sound in dry air at 20 °C = 343 m s–1.

Given

  • L = 12.0 m; m = 2.10 kg; v = 343 m s−1.

Asked

The tension.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. μ = 2.10/12.0 = 0.175 kg m−1.
  2. T = μv2 = 0.175 × 3432 ≈ 2.06 × 104 N.

About 2.06 × 104 N.
