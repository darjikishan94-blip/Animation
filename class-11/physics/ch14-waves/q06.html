14.6 A bat emits ultrasonic sound of frequency 1000 kHz in air. If the sound meets a water surface, what is the wavelength of (a) the reflected sound, (b) the transmitted sound? Speed of sound in air is 340 m s–1 and in water 1486 m s–1.

Given

  • f = 1000 kHz = 106 Hz; vair = 340 m s−1; vwater = 1486 m s−1.

Asked

The wavelengths of the reflected and transmitted sound.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. The frequency does not change at the surface.
  2. (a) Reflected (in air): λ = 340/106 = 3.4 × 10−4 m.
  3. (b) Transmitted (in water): λ = 1486/106 ≈ 1.49 × 10−3 m.

(a) 3.4 × 10−4 m (b) 1.49 × 10−3 m
