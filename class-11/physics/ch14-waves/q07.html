14.7 A hospital uses an ultrasonic scanner to locate tumours in a tissue. What is the wavelength of sound in the tissue in which the speed of sound is 1.7 km s–1 ? The operating frequency of the scanner is 4.2 MHz.

Given

  • v = 1.7 km s−1; f = 4.2 MHz.

Asked

The wavelength.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. λ = v/f = 1700/4.2 × 106 ≈ 4.0 × 10−4 m.

About 4.0 × 10−4 m.
