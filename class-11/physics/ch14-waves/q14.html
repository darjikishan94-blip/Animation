14.14 A wire stretched between two rigid supports vibrates in its fundamental mode with a frequency of 45 Hz. The mass of the wire is 3.5 × 10–2 kg and its linear mass density is 4.0 × 10–2 kg m–1. What is (a) the speed of a transverse wave on the string, and (b) the tension in the string?

Given

  • Fundamental 45 Hz; m = 3.5 × 10−2 kg; μ = 4.0 × 10−2 kg m−1.

Asked

(a) wave speed (b) tension.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Length L = m/μ = 0.875 m; fundamental: λ = 2L = 1.75 m.
  2. (a) v = νλ = 45 × 1.75 ≈ 78.8 m s−1.
  3. (b) T = μv2 = 0.040 × 78.82 ≈ 248 N.

(a) ≈ 79 m s−1 (b) ≈ 248 N
