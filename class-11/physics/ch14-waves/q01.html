14.1 A string of mass 2.50 kg is under a tension of 200 N. The length of the stretched string is 20.0 m. If the transverse jerk is struck at one end of the string, how long does the disturbance take to reach the other end?

Given

  • Mass 2.50 kg; tension 200 N; length 20.0 m.

Asked

The time for the pulse to travel the length of the string.

How the animation is drawn

  • Shown 10 times slower; 25 px per metre.

Answer

  1. Mass per unit length μ = 2.50/20.0 = 0.125 kg m−1.
  2. v = √(T/μ) = √(200/0.125) = 40 m s−1.
  3. t = 20.0/40 = 0.50 s.

0.50 s.
