14.11 The transverse displacement of a string (clamped at its both ends) is given by y(x, t) = 0.06 sin (2π x/3) cos (120 πt) where x and y are in m and t in s. The length of the string is 1.5 m and its mass is 3.0 ×10–2 kg. Answer the following : (a) Does the function represent a travelling wave or a stationary wave? (b) Interpret the wave as a superposition of two waves travelling in opposite directions. What is the wavelength, frequency, and speed of each wave ? (c) Determine the tension in the string.

Given

  • y = 0.06 sin(2πx/3) cos(120πt); L = 1.5 m; m = 3.0 × 10−2 kg.

Asked

(a) type (b) the two component waves (c) the tension.

How the animation is drawn

  • Time slowed 100 times; displacement exaggerated.

Answer

  1. (a) Stationary: a product of a function of x and a function of t.
  2. (b) y = 0.03 sin(2πx/3 − 120πt) + 0.03 sin(2πx/3 + 120πt). Each: λ = 2π/(2π/3) = 3 m, ν = 120π/2π = 60 Hz, v = νλ = 180 m s−1.
  3. (c) μ = 3.0 × 10−2/1.5 = 0.020 kg m−1; T = μv2 = 0.020 × 1802 = 648 N.

(a) stationary (b) 3 m, 60 Hz, 180 m s−1 each (c) 648 N
