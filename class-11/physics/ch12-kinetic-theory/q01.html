12.1 Estimate the fraction of molecular volume to the actual volume occupied by oxygen gas at STP. Take the diameter of an oxygen molecule to be 3 Å.

Given

  • Diameter of an O2 molecule 3 Å; one mole at STP occupies 22.4 L.

Asked

Molecular volume / actual volume.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Volume of one molecule = (4/3)π(1.5 × 10−10)3 ≈ 1.41 × 10−29 m3; for a mole: × 6.02 × 1023 ≈ 8.5 × 10−6 m3.
  2. Fraction = 8.5 × 10−6/22.4 × 10−3 ≈ 3.8 × 10−4.

About 4 × 10−4.
