12.10 Estimate the mean free path and collision frequency of a nitrogen molecule in a cylinder containing nitrogen at 2.0 atm and temperature 17 °C. Take the radius of a nitrogen molecule to be roughly 1.0 Å. Compare the collision time with the time the molecule moves freely between two successive collisions (Molecular mass of N2 = 28.0 u).

Given

  • P = 2.0 atm; T = 290 K; molecular radius 1.0 Å (diameter d = 2.0 Å); M = 28.0 u.

Asked

Mean free path, collision frequency; collision time vs free time.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Number density n = P/kT = 2.0 × 1.013 × 105/(1.38 × 10−23 × 290) ≈ 5.1 × 1025 m−3.
  2. Mean free path λ = 1/(√2 πd2n) ≈ 1.1 × 10−7 m.
  3. vrms = √(3RT/M) ≈ 5.1 × 102 m s−1; collision frequency ≈ v/λ ≈ 4.6 × 109 s−1.
  4. Collision time ≈ d/v ≈ 4 × 10−13 s; free time between collisions ≈ 1/(4.6 × 109) ≈ 2 × 10−10 s: about 500 times longer.

λ ≈ 1 × 10−7 m; ≈ 5 × 109 collisions per second; free time ≈ 500 × collision time.
