12.2 Molar volume is the volume occupied by 1 mol of any (ideal) gas at standard temperature and pressure (STP : 1 atmospheric pressure, 0 °C). Show that it is 22.4 litres.

Given

  • n = 1 mol; P = 1.013 × 105 Pa; T = 273 K; R = 8.31 J mol−1 K−1.

Asked

Show V = 22.4 L.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. V = nRT/P = 1 × 8.31 × 273/1.013 × 105 ≈ 2.24 × 10−2 m3 = 22.4 L.

22.4 L.
