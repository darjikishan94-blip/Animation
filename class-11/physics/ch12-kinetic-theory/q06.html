12.6 Estimate the total number of air molecules (inclusive of oxygen, nitrogen, water vapour and other constituents) in a room of capacity 25.0 m3 at a temperature of 27 °C and 1 atm pressure.

Given

  • V = 25.0 m3; T = 300 K; P = 1.013 × 105 Pa; k = 1.38 × 10−23 J K−1.

Asked

The number of molecules.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. N = PV/kT = 1.013 × 105 × 25.0/(1.38 × 10−23 × 300) ≈ 6.1 × 1026.

About 6.1 × 1026.
