12.5 An air bubble of volume 1.0 cm3 rises from the bottom of a lake 40 m deep at a temperature of 12 °C. To what volume does it grow when it reaches the surface, which is at a temperature of 35 °C ?

Given

  • V₁ = 1.0 cm3 at 40 m depth, 12 °C; surface at 35 °C; Patm = 1.013 × 105 Pa; water 103 kg m−3.

Asked

The volume at the surface.

How the animation is drawn

  • Bubble size grows in proportion to its volume; depth to scale (8 px per metre).

Answer

  1. P₁ = 1.013 × 105 + 103 × 9.8 × 40 ≈ 4.93 × 105 Pa; T₁ = 285 K. P₂ = 1.013 × 105 Pa; T₂ = 308 K.
  2. P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂: V₂ = 1.0 × (4.93/1.013) × (308/285) ≈ 5.3 cm3.

About 5.3 cm3.
