12.4 An oxygen cylinder of volume 30 litre has an initial gauge pressure of 15 atm and a temperature of 27 °C. After some oxygen is withdrawn from the cylinder, the gauge pressure drops to 11 atm and its temperature drops to 17 °C. Estimate the mass of oxygen taken out of the cylinder (R = 8.31 J mol–1 K–1, molecular mass of O2 = 32 u).

Given

  • V = 30 L; gauge 15 atm at 27 °C, then 11 atm at 17 °C; R = 8.31; M = 32 g/mol.

Asked

The mass of oxygen withdrawn.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Absolute pressures: 16 atm and 12 atm (add 1 atm).
  2. n₁ = PV/RT = 16 × 1.013 × 105 × 0.030/(8.31 × 300) ≈ 19.5 mol; n₂ = 12 × 1.013 × 105 × 0.030/(8.31 × 290) ≈ 15.1 mol.
  3. Removed ≈ 4.4 mol × 32 g ≈ 0.14 kg.

About 0.14 kg.
