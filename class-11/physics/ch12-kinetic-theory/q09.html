12.9 At what temperature is the root mean square speed of an atom in an argon gas cylinder equal to the rms speed of a helium gas atom at – 20 °C ? (atomic mass of Ar = 39.9 u, of He = 4.0 u).

Given

  • He at −20 °C = 253 K; M(Ar) = 39.9 u, M(He) = 4.0 u.

Asked

The temperature of argon with the same vrms.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. vrms = √(3RT/M): equal speeds need T/M equal.
  2. TAr = 253 × 39.9/4.0 ≈ 2.52 × 103 K.

About 2.52 × 103 K.
