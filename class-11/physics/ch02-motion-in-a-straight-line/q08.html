2.8 A ball is dropped from a height of 90 m on a floor. At each collision with the floor, the ball loses one tenth of its speed. Plot the speed-time graph of its motion between t = 0 to 12 s.

Given

  • Dropped from 90 m; loses one tenth of its speed at each bounce.
  • Graph wanted for 0 to 12 s. (g = 9.8 m s−2, as in NCERT’s solution.)

Asked

The speed–time graph from 0 to 12 s.

How the animation is drawn

  • Real time; heights at 4.4 px per metre.

Answer

  1. Fall: t = √(2 × 90/9.8) ≈ 4.29 s; speed at impact = 9.8 × 4.29 ≈ 42 m s−1.
  2. After the bounce: 9/10 × 42 = 37.8 m s−1 upward.
  3. It rises for 37.8/9.8 ≈ 3.86 s (speed falling to 0 at ≈ 8.14 s) and falls back for 3.86 s, hitting the floor again at ≈ 12 s with 37.8 m s−1.
  4. Speed–time graph: a straight rise from 0 to 42 m s−1 (0–4.29 s), a drop to 37.8, a straight fall to 0 at 8.14 s, and a straight rise to 37.8 m s−1 at 12 s.

Peaks of 42 m s−1 at 4.29 s and 37.8 m s−1 at 12 s; zero at 8.14 s.
