2.14 A police van moving on a highway with a speed of 30 km h–1 fires a bullet at a thief’s car speeding away in the same direction with a speed of 192 km h–1. If the muzzle speed of the bullet is 150 m s–1, with what speed does the bullet hit the thief’s car ? (Note: Obtain that speed which is relevant for damaging the thief’s car).

Given

  • Van 30 km h−1; thief’s car 192 km h−1, same direction; muzzle speed 150 m s−1 (relative to the van).

Asked

The speed of the bullet relative to the car (the one that matters for damage).

How the animation is drawn

  • Motion slowed down greatly; distances illustrative.

Answer

  1. Van: 30 km h−1 = 8.33 m s−1; car: 192 km h−1 = 53.3 m s−1.
  2. Bullet’s speed over the ground = 150 + 8.33 = 158.3 m s−1.
  3. Relative to the car: 158.3 − 53.3 = 105 m s−1.

105 m s−1.
