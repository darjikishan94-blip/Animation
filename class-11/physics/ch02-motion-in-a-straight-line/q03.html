2.3 A woman starts from her home at 9.00 am, walks with a speed of 5 km h–1 on a straight road up to her office 2.5 km away, stays at the office up to 5.00 pm, and returns home by an auto with a speed of 25 km h–1. Choose suitable scales and plot the x-t graph of her motion.

Given

  • Leaves home at 9.00 am; walks at 5 km h−1; office 2.5 km away.
  • Stays till 5.00 pm; returns by auto at 25 km h−1.

Asked

The x–t graph of her motion.

How the animation is drawn

  • The day is compressed: the walk, the stay and the ride are shown at different speeds; the graph is to scale.

Answer

  1. Walk: time = 2.5 km / 5 km h−1 = 0.5 h, so she reaches the office at 9.30 am.
  2. At the office x = 2.5 km from 9.30 am to 5.00 pm: a horizontal line.
  3. Ride: time = 2.5 / 25 = 0.1 h = 6 min, so she is home at 5.06 pm.
  4. Graph: a line rising from (9.00, 0) to (9.30, 2.5 km), flat to (5.00 pm, 2.5 km), then falling steeply to (5.06 pm, 0).

Office at 9.30 am; home again at 5.06 pm.
