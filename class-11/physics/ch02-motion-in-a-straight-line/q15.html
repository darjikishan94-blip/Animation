2.15 Suggest a suitable physical situation for each of the following graphs (Fig 2.12):

Given

  • Fig. 2.12: (a) an x–t graph rising to A, falling past B to a constant negative value; (b) a v–t graph of falling lines with jumps, getting shorter; (c) an a–t graph that is zero except for one short spike.

Asked

A physical situation for each graph.

How the animation is drawn

  • Speeds, times and distances illustrative; the shapes follow Fig. 2.12.

Answer

  1. (a) A ball resting on a smooth floor is kicked; it hits a wall (A), rebounds with reduced speed past its start (B), and is stopped by the opposite wall.
  2. (b) A ball thrown up that falls back and rebounds from the floor with reduced speed after each hit.
  3. (c) A uniformly moving cricket ball turned back by a bat in a very short time.

(a) kicked ball between two walls (b) bouncing ball (c) ball hit back by a bat
