2.5 A car moving along a straight highway with speed of 126 km h–1 is brought to a stop within a distance of 200 m. What is the retardation of the car (assumed uniform), and how long does it take for the car to stop ?

Given

  • Initial speed 126 km h−1; stopping distance 200 m; uniform retardation.

Asked

The retardation and the stopping time.

How the animation is drawn

  • Real time; 2.1 px per metre.

Answer

  1. 126 km h−1 = 126 × 1000 / 3600 = 35 m s−1.
  2. v2 = u2 − 2as: 0 = 352 − 2a × 200, so a = 1225/400 ≈ 3.06 m s−2.
  3. t = u/a = 35/3.06 ≈ 11.4 s.

Retardation ≈ 3.06 m s−2; time ≈ 11.4 s.
