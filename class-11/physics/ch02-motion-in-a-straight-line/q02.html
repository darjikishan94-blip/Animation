2.2 The position-time (x-t) graphs for two children A and B returning from their school O to their homes P and Q respectively are shown in Fig. 2.9. Choose the correct entries in the brackets below ; (a) (A/B) lives closer to the school than (B/A) (b) (A/B) starts from the school earlier than (B/A) (c) (A/B) walks faster than (B/A) (d) A and B reach home at the (same/different) time (e) (A/B) overtakes (B/A) on the road (once/twice).

Given

  • Fig. 2.9: straight-line x–t graphs of A (from O at t = 0 to P) and B (starting later, steeper, to Q, farther than P).

Asked

The correct entries in (a) to (e).

How the animation is drawn

  • No numbers are given: the graph is drawn to match the shape of Fig. 2.9, with both children reaching home together as in the NCERT answer.

Answer

  1. (a) A lives closer: P is lower on the x-axis than Q.
  2. (b) A starts earlier: A’s graph begins at t = 0, B’s later.
  3. (c) B walks faster: B’s graph is steeper (greater slope = greater speed).
  4. (d) The same time (NCERT answer): both graphs end at the same instant.
  5. (e) B overtakes A once: the graphs cross at one point.

(a) A, B (b) A, B (c) B, A (d) same (e) B, A, once
