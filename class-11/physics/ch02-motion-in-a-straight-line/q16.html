2.16 Figure 2.13 gives the x-t plot of a particle executing one-dimensional simple harmonic motion. (You will learn about this motion in more detail in Chapter13). Give the signs of position, velocity and acceleration variables of the particle at t = 0.3 s, 1.2 s, – 1.2 s.

Given

  • Fig. 2.13: x–t graph of simple harmonic motion crossing zero at t = −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3 s.

Asked

Signs of x, v and a at t = 0.3 s, 1.2 s and −1.2 s.

How the animation is drawn

  • The amplitude is not given; the graph is drawn as x = −A sin(πt), matching Fig. 2.13.

Answer

  1. In SHM, a = −ω2x: the acceleration always has the opposite sign to x. The velocity sign is the slope of the x–t graph.
  2. t = 0.3 s: x < 0, v < 0, a > 0.
  3. t = 1.2 s: x > 0, v > 0, a < 0.
  4. t = −1.2 s: x < 0, v > 0, a > 0.

0.3 s: −, −, + ; 1.2 s: +, +, − ; −1.2 s: −, +, +
