11.3 Explain why (a) Two bodies at different temperatures T1 and T2 if brought in thermal contact do not necessarily settle to the mean temperature (T1 + T2 )/2. (b) The coolant in a chemical or a nuclear plant (i.e., the liquid used to prevent the different parts of a plant from getting too hot) should have high specific heat. (c) Air pressure in a car tyre increases during driving. (d) The climate of a harbour town is more temperate than that of a town in a desert at the same latitude.

Given

  • Four observations.

Asked

Explanations.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for explanations rather than describing one physical situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) The heat lost by one equals the heat gained by the other: m₁c₁ΔT₁ = m₂c₂ΔT₂. Only if the heat capacities m₁c₁ and m₂c₂ are equal is the final temperature the mean.
  2. (b) A high specific heat lets the coolant carry away a lot of heat with only a small rise in its own temperature.
  3. (c) Friction and the flexing of the tyre warm the air inside; at nearly constant volume, the pressure rises with temperature.
  4. (d) Near the sea, the large heat capacity of water and sea breezes keep temperatures from swinging much; a desert’s sand heats and cools quickly.

Heat capacities; high c absorbs heat with little temperature rise; P ∝ T at fixed V; the sea moderates temperature.
