11.4 A cylinder with a movable piston contains 3 moles of hydrogen at standard temperature and pressure. The walls of the cylinder are made of a heat insulator, and the piston is insulated by having a pile of sand on it. By what factor does the pressure of the gas increase if the gas is compressed to half its original volume ?

Given

  • 3 mol of H2 at STP; insulated (adiabatic); volume halved; H2 diatomic, γ = 1.4.

Asked

The factor by which the pressure increases.

How the animation is drawn

  • P–V graph to scale in units of the starting pressure and volume.

Answer

  1. No heat can enter or leave: the compression is adiabatic, PVγ = constant, with γ = 7/5 = 1.4 for a diatomic gas.
  2. P₂/P₁ = (V₁/V₂)γ = 21.4 ≈ 2.64.
