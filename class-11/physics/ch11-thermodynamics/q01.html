11.1 A geyser heats water flowing at the rate of 3.0 litres per minute from 27 °C to 77 °C. If the geyser operates on a gas burner, what is the rate of consumption of the fuel if its heat of combustion is 4.0 × 104 J/g ?

Given

  • 3.0 L of water per minute heated from 27 °C to 77 °C; heat of combustion 4.0 × 104 J/g; cwater ≈ 4.2 J g−1 K−1.

Asked

The rate of fuel consumption.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Heat needed per minute = 3000 g × 4.2 × 50 ≈ 6.3 × 105 J.
  2. Fuel per minute = 6.3 × 105/4.0 × 104 ≈ 15.7 g.

About 15.7 g per minute.
