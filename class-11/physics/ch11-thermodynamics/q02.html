11.2 What amount of heat must be supplied to 2.0 × 10–2 kg of nitrogen (at room temperature) to raise its temperature by 45 °C at constant pressure ? (Molecular mass of N2 = 28; R = 8.3 J mol–1 K–1.)

Given

  • m = 20 g of N2 (M = 28); ΔT = 45 K at constant pressure; R = 8.3 J mol−1 K−1.

Asked

The heat supplied.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. n = 20/28 ≈ 0.714 mol. For a diatomic gas Cp = (7/2)R ≈ 29.05 J mol−1 K−1.
  2. Q = nCpΔT = 0.714 × 29.05 × 45 ≈ 933 J.
