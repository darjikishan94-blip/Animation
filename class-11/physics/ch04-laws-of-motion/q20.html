4.20 A batsman deflects a ball by an angle of 45° without changing its initial speed which is equal to 54 km/h. What is the impulse imparted to the ball? (Mass of the ball is 0.15 kg.)

Given

  • Speed 54 km/h, unchanged; deflection 45°; mass 0.15 kg.

Asked

The impulse imparted to the ball.

How the animation is drawn

  • Following the NCERT solution, the incoming and outgoing paths make 45°, each at 22.5° to the bat’s normal.

Answer

  1. 54 km/h = 15 m s−1; momentum = 0.15 × 15 = 2.25 kg m s−1.
  2. Along the bat the momentum is unchanged; perpendicular to it, 2.25 cos 22.5° reverses.
  3. Impulse = 2 × 2.25 × cos 22.5° ≈ 4.16 kg m s−1, perpendicular to the bat.
  4. If “deflects by 45°” is read as a 45° change of direction instead, the impulse is 2 × 2.25 × sin 22.5° ≈ 1.72 kg m s−1.

About 4.16 kg m s⁻¹ (the NCERT answer).
