4.1 Give the magnitude and direction of the net force acting on (a) a drop of rain falling down with a constant speed, (b) a cork of mass 10 g floating on water, (c) a kite skillfully held stationary in the sky, (d) a car moving with a constant velocity of 30 km/h on a rough road, (e) a high-speed electron in space far from all material objects, and free of electric and magnetic fields.

Given

  • Five objects, each either at rest or moving with constant velocity (the electron: no forces of any kind).
  • Cork mass 10 g; car speed 30 km/h; g = 10 m s−2.

Asked

The magnitude and direction of the net force on each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force arrows are drawn only in the solution run; within each scene the balancing forces are drawn equal.
  • The motion in each scene is illustrative.

Answer

  1. (a) Zero: constant velocity, so the air resistance balances the weight.
  2. (b) Zero: the cork is at rest; the buoyant force balances its weight of 0.1 N.
  3. (c) Zero: the kite is at rest; weight, tension and wind force balance.
  4. (d) Zero: constant velocity; the forward force balances friction and air drag.
  5. (e) Zero: no force acts on it.

The net force is zero in all five cases (no acceleration).
