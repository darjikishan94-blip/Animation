4.5 A constant retarding force of 50 N is applied to a body of mass 20 kg moving initially with a speed of 15 m s−1. How long does the body take to stop?

Given

  • Retarding force = 50 N; mass = 20 kg; initial speed = 15 m s−1.

Asked

The time taken to stop.

How the animation is drawn

  • Real time; 11 px per metre.

Answer

  1. a = F/m = 50/20 = 2.5 m s−2, opposite to the motion.
  2. v = u − at: 0 = 15 − 2.5t, so t = 6 s.

6 s.
