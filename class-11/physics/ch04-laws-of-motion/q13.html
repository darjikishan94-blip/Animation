4.13 A man of mass 70 kg stands on a weighing scale in a lift which is moving (a) upwards with a uniform speed of 10 m s−1, (b) downwards with a uniform acceleration of 5 m s−2, (c) upwards with a uniform acceleration of 5 m s−2. What would be the readings on the scale in each case? (d) What would be the reading if the lift mechanism failed and it hurtled down freely under gravity?

Given

  • Mass of the man = 70 kg; g = 10 m s−2.
  • Lift: up at uniform 10 m s−1; down with a = 5 m s−2; up with a = 5 m s−2; free fall.

Asked

The scale reading in each case.

How the animation is drawn

  • The scale reads the normal force N it exerts on the man. Lift motion is illustrative.

Answer

  1. (a) a = 0: N = mg = 700 N (70 kg wt).
  2. (b) N = m(g − a) = 70 × 5 = 350 N (35 kg wt).
  3. (c) N = m(g + a) = 70 × 15 = 1050 N (105 kg wt).
  4. (d) a = g: N = 0; the scale reads zero.

700 N, 350 N, 1050 N and 0.
