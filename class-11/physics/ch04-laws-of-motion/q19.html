4.19 A shell of mass 0.020 kg is fired by a gun of mass 100 kg. If the muzzle speed of the shell is 80 m s−1, what is the recoil speed of the gun?

Given

  • Shell 0.020 kg at 80 m s−1; gun 100 kg; both initially at rest.

Asked

The recoil speed of the gun.

How the animation is drawn

  • The recoil (0.016 m s−1) is far too slow to see and is exaggerated.

Answer

  1. Momentum before = 0, so after: 0.020 × 80 + 100 × v = 0.
  2. v = −1.6/100 = −0.016 m s−1.

0.016 m s⁻¹, opposite to the shell.
