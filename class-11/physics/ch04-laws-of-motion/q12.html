4.12 A bob of mass 0.1 kg hung from the ceiling of a room by a string 2 m long is set into oscillation. The speed of the bob at its mean position is 1 m s−1. What is the trajectory of the bob if the string is cut when the bob is (a) at one of its extreme positions, (b) at its mean position.

Given

  • Bob mass 0.1 kg; string 2 m; speed at the mean position 1 m s−1.

Asked

The trajectory after the string is cut at an extreme position and at the mean position.

How the animation is drawn

  • The amplitude (about 13°) follows from the speed at the mean position. The floor is not given, so the fall is shown for 0.75 s, at half speed.

Answer

  1. (a) At an extreme position the bob is momentarily at rest; with only gravity acting it falls vertically downward in a straight line.
  2. (b) At the mean position it moves horizontally at 1 m s−1; with only gravity acting it moves like a horizontally projected body, along a parabola.

(a) a vertical straight line (b) a parabola.
