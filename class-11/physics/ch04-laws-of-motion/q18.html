4.18 Two billiard balls each of mass 0.05 kg moving in opposite directions with speed 6 m s−1 collide and rebound with the same speed. What is the impulse imparted to each ball due to the other?

Given

  • Each ball 0.05 kg; 6 m s−1 in opposite directions; they rebound with the same speed.

Asked

The impulse imparted to each ball.

How the animation is drawn

  • Right is taken as positive.

Answer

  1. Ball A: momentum changes from +0.3 to −0.3 kg m s−1, so impulse = −0.6 kg m s−1.
  2. Ball B: from −0.3 to +0.3, impulse = +0.6 kg m s−1.

0.6 kg m s⁻¹ on each ball, in opposite directions.
