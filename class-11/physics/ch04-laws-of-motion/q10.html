4.10 A body of mass 0.40 kg moving initially with a constant speed of 10 m s−1 to the north is subject to a constant force of 8.0 N directed towards the south for 30 s. Take the instant the force is applied to be t = 0, the position of the body at that time to be x = 0, and predict its position at t = −5 s, 25 s, 100 s.

Given

  • Mass 0.40 kg; initial velocity 10 m s−1 north; force 8.0 N south for 0 ≤ t ≤ 30 s.
  • x = 0 at t = 0 (north positive).

Asked

The position at t = −5 s, 25 s and 100 s.

How the animation is drawn

  • North is positive. The x–t graph and the north–south line share one scale; 8 s of motion per second of animation.

Answer

  1. t = −5 s: no force yet, so x = 10 × (−5) = −50 m.
  2. a = −8.0/0.40 = −20 m s−2 for 0 ≤ t ≤ 30 s. At t = 25 s: x = 10 × 25 − ½ × 20 × 25² = −6000 m.
  3. At t = 30 s: x = 300 − 9000 = −8700 m, v = 10 − 600 = −590 m s−1. After that no force: x(100) = −8700 − 590 × 70 = −50,000 m.

−50 m, −6 km and −50 km (i.e. 50 m, 6 km and 50 km south of x = 0).
