4.8 The driver of a three-wheeler moving with a speed of 36 km/h sees a child standing in the middle of the road and brings his vehicle to rest in 4.0 s just in time to save the child. What is the average retarding force on the vehicle? The mass of the three-wheeler is 400 kg and the mass of the driver is 65 kg.

Given

  • Speed = 36 km/h; stops in 4.0 s.
  • Mass of the three-wheeler 400 kg; driver 65 kg.

Asked

The average retarding force on the vehicle.

How the animation is drawn

  • Uniform retardation assumed (the question asks for the average force); 24 px per metre.

Answer

  1. u = 36 km/h = 10 m s−1; total mass = 400 + 65 = 465 kg.
  2. a = (0 − 10)/4.0 = −2.5 m s−2.
  3. F = 465 × 2.5 ≈ 1.16 × 103 N, opposite to the motion.

About 1.16 × 10³ N, opposing the motion.
