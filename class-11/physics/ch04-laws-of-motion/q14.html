4.14 Figure 4.16 shows the position-time graph of a particle of mass 4 kg. What is the (a) force on the particle for t < 0, t > 4 s, 0 < t < 4 s? (b) impulse at t = 0 and t = 4 s? (Consider one-dimensional motion only).

Given

  • Mass 4 kg. Fig. 4.16: x = 0 for t < 0; x rises uniformly to 3 m at t = 4 s; x = 3 m for t > 4 s.

Asked

The force in each interval and the impulse at t = 0 and t = 4 s.

How the animation is drawn

  • The graph is redrawn from Fig. 4.16; the particle’s line uses its own scale.

Answer

  1. (a) t < 0: at rest; 0 < t < 4 s: v = 3/4 = 0.75 m s−1, constant; t > 4 s: at rest. Constant velocity in each, so the force is zero in all three.
  2. (b) Impulse at t = 0: 4 × (0.75 − 0) = +3 kg m s−1. At t = 4 s: 4 × (0 − 0.75) = −3 kg m s−1.

(a) zero in all three intervals (b) +3 kg m s⁻¹ at t = 0 and −3 kg m s⁻¹ at t = 4 s.
