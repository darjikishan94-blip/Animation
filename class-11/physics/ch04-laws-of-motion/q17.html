4.17 A nucleus is at rest in the laboratory frame of reference. Show that if it disintegrates into two smaller nuclei the products must move in opposite directions.

Given

  • A nucleus at rest; it splits into two smaller nuclei.

Asked

Show that the products move in opposite directions.

How the animation is drawn

  • The fragment masses are not given; one is drawn twice the other.

Answer

  1. Before: momentum = 0. No external force acts, so momentum is conserved.
  2. After: m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = 0, so v₁ = −(m₂/m₁) v₂.
  3. The two velocities are along one line in opposite directions.

Momentum conservation makes the two momenta equal and opposite, so the products move in opposite directions.
