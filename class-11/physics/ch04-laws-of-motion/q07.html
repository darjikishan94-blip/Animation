4.7 A body of mass 5 kg is acted upon by two perpendicular forces 8 N and 6 N. Give the magnitude and direction of the acceleration of the body.

Given

  • Mass = 5 kg; two perpendicular forces of 8 N and 6 N.

Asked

The magnitude and direction of the acceleration.

How the animation is drawn

  • Seen from above; force arrows to scale; the body starts from rest in the picture.

Answer

  1. Resultant = √(8² + 6²) = 10 N, at tan−1(6/8) ≈ 37° to the 8 N force.
  2. a = F/m = 10/5 = 2 m s−2, along the resultant.

2 m s⁻², at about 37° to the 8 N force (towards the 6 N force).
