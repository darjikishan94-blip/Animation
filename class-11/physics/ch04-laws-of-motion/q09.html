4.9 A rocket with a lift-off mass 20,000 kg is blasted upwards with an initial acceleration of 5.0 m s−2. Calculate the initial thrust (force) of the blast. (Take g = 10 m s−2.)

Given

  • Lift-off mass = 20,000 kg; initial acceleration = 5.0 m s−2, upward; g = 10 m s−2.

Asked

The initial thrust of the blast.

How the animation is drawn

  • The rise is shown for 3.2 s at constant acceleration (the change of mass is ignored, as only the initial thrust is asked).

Answer

  1. Thrust − mg = ma.
  2. Thrust = m(g + a) = 20,000 × (10 + 5) = 3.0 × 105 N.

3.0 × 10⁵ N, upward.
