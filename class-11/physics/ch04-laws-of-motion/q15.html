4.15 Two bodies of masses 10 kg and 20 kg respectively kept on a smooth, horizontal surface are tied to the ends of a light string. A horizontal force F = 600 N is applied to (i) A, (ii) B along the direction of string. What is the tension in the string in each case?

Given

  • A = 10 kg, B = 20 kg; smooth horizontal surface; light string; F = 600 N along the string.

Asked

The tension in the string when F is applied to A, and when it is applied to B.

How the animation is drawn

  • Motion shown much slower than real (a = 20 m s−2).

Answer

  1. a = 600/(10 + 20) = 20 m s−2 in both cases.
  2. (i) Force on A: the string pulls B, T = 20 × 20 = 400 N.
  3. (ii) Force on B: the string pulls A, T = 10 × 20 = 200 N.

(i) 400 N (ii) 200 N.
