4.23 Explain why (a) a horse cannot pull a cart and run in empty space, (b) passengers are thrown forward from their seats when a speeding bus stops suddenly, (c) it is easier to pull a lawn mower than to push it, (d) a cricketer moves his hands backwards while holding a catch.

Given

  • Four everyday situations.

Asked

An explanation for each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Illustrative scenes; force arrows only in the solution run.

Answer

  1. (a) To move forward the horse pushes the ground backward and the ground pushes it forward (Newton’s third law). In empty space there is nothing to push on, and the horse–cart forces are internal and cancel.
  2. (b) The bus stops, but the passengers’ bodies keep moving forward by inertia (Newton’s first law).
  3. (c) Pulling at an angle has an upward component that reduces the normal force and so the friction; pushing adds a downward component that increases them.
  4. (d) Moving the hands back increases the time over which the ball stops. For the same change in momentum, a longer time means a smaller force (F = Δp/Δt).

Third law, inertia, normal force and friction, and impulse respectively.
