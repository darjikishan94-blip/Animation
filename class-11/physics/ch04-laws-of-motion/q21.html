4.21 A stone of mass 0.25 kg tied to the end of a string is whirled round in a circle of radius 1.5 m with a speed of 40 rev./min in a horizontal plane. What is the tension in the string? What is the maximum speed with which the stone can be whirled around if the string can withstand a maximum tension of 200 N?

Given

  • Mass 0.25 kg; radius 1.5 m; 40 rev/min in a horizontal plane; maximum tension 200 N.

Asked

The tension at 40 rev/min, and the maximum speed.

How the animation is drawn

  • Seen from above; gravity ignored (horizontal circle). The fast case is shown slowed down.

Answer

  1. v = 2πrn = 2π × 1.5 × (40/60) ≈ 6.28 m s−1.
  2. T = mv²/r = 0.25 × 6.28²/1.5 ≈ 6.6 N.
  3. vmax = √(Tmax r/m) = √(200 × 1.5/0.25) = √1200 ≈ 35 m s−1.

T ≈ 6.6 N; maximum speed ≈ 35 m s⁻¹.
