4.11 A truck starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2.0 m s−2. At t = 10 s, a stone is dropped by a person standing on the top of the truck (6 m high from the ground). What are the (a) velocity, and (b) acceleration of the stone at t = 11 s? (Neglect air resistance.)

Given

  • Truck: from rest, a = 2.0 m s−2.
  • Stone dropped at t = 10 s from 6 m; air resistance neglected; g = 10 m s−2.

Asked

The velocity and acceleration of the stone at t = 11 s.

How the animation is drawn

  • The view covers 60 m to 140 m from the truck’s start; 7 px per metre; the fall is shown at half speed.

Answer

  1. At t = 10 s the truck (and stone) move at 2 × 10 = 20 m s−1.
  2. The stone lands after √(2 × 6/10) ≈ 1.1 s, so at t = 11 s it is still in the air.
  3. (a) vx = 20 m s−1, vy = 10 × 1 = 10 m s−1 down: v = √(20² + 10²) ≈ 22.4 m s−1, at tan−1(10/20) ≈ 27° below the horizontal.
  4. (b) a = g = 10 m s−2, vertically downward: no horizontal force acts on the stone once it is dropped.

(a) ≈ 22.4 m s⁻¹ at ≈ 27° below the horizontal (b) 10 m s⁻², vertically down.
