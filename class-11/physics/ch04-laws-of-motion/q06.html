4.6 A constant force acting on a body of mass 3.0 kg changes its speed from 2.0 m s−1 to 3.5 m s−1 in 25 s. The direction of the motion of the body remains unchanged. What is the magnitude and direction of the force?

Given

  • Mass = 3.0 kg; speed 2.0 → 3.5 m s−1 in 25 s; direction unchanged.

Asked

The magnitude and direction of the force.

How the animation is drawn

  • 25 s is shown in 5 s; 7.4 px per metre.

Answer

  1. a = (3.5 − 2.0)/25 = 0.06 m s−2.
  2. F = ma = 3.0 × 0.06 = 0.18 N.
  3. The speed increases with no change of direction, so the force is along the direction of motion.

0.18 N, along the direction of motion.
