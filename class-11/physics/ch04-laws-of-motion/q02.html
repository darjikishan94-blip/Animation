4.2 A pebble of mass 0.05 kg is thrown vertically upwards. Give the direction and magnitude of the net force on the pebble, (a) during its upward motion, (b) during its downward motion, (c) at the highest point where it is momentarily at rest. Do your answers change if the pebble was thrown at an angle of 45° with the horizontal direction? Ignore air resistance.

Given

  • Mass of the pebble = 0.05 kg; thrown vertically upwards, then at 45°.
  • Air resistance ignored; g = 10 m s−2.

Asked

The net force during the upward motion, the downward motion and at the highest point; and whether a 45° throw changes the answers.

How the animation is drawn

  • No launch speed is given: heights and timing are illustrative.
  • The flight is shown at half speed.

Answer

  1. Once released, the only force is the weight: mg = 0.05 × 10 = 0.5 N.
  2. (a), (b) and (c): 0.5 N, vertically downward, in all three.
  3. At 45°: no change; the net force is still 0.5 N, vertically downward, throughout the flight.

0.5 N vertically downward in every case, including the 45° throw.
