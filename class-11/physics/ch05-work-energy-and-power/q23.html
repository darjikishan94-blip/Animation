5.23 A family uses 8 kW of power. (a) Direct solar energy is incident on the horizontal surface at an average rate of 200 W per square meter. If 20% of this energy can be converted to useful electrical energy, how large an area is needed to supply 8 kW? (b) Compare this area to that of the roof of a typical house.

Given

  • Power needed 8 kW; sunlight 200 W m−2; 20% conversion.

Asked

(a) The area needed (b) compared with a typical roof.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a short calculation with no motion to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Useful power per m2 = 0.20 × 200 = 40 W. Area = 8000/40 = 200 m2.
  2. (b) About the roof of a large house (roughly 14 m × 14 m): larger than most typical roofs.

(a) 200 m2 (b) comparable to a large house’s roof.
