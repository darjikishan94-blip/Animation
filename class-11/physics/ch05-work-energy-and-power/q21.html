5.21 The blades of a windmill sweep out a circle of area A. (a) If the wind flows at a velocity v perpendicular to the circle, what is the mass of the air passing through it in time t ? (b) What is the kinetic energy of the air ? (c) Assume that the windmill converts 25% of the wind’s energy into electrical energy, and that A = 30 m2, v = 36 km/h and the density of air is 1.2 kg m–3. What is the electrical power produced ?

Given

  • Swept area A; wind speed v perpendicular to it; 25% conversion; A = 30 m2, v = 36 km/h, air density 1.2 kg m−3.

Asked

(a) Mass of air through it in time t (b) its kinetic energy (c) the electrical power.

How the animation is drawn

  • The air column is drawn for a time t of 1 s; wind shown slowed.

Answer

  1. (a) In time t, a column of length vt passes: volume Avt, mass m = ρAvt.
  2. (b) K = ½mv2 = ½ρAv3t.
  3. (c) v = 10 m s−1. Wind power = ½ × 1.2 × 30 × 103 = 1.8 × 104 W; electrical = 25% = 4.5 × 103 W.

(a) ρAvt (b) ½ρAv3t (c) 4.5 kW
