5.19 A trolley of mass 300 kg carrying a sandbag of 25 kg is moving uniformly with a speed of 27 km/h on a frictionless track. After a while, sand starts leaking out of a hole on the floor of the trolley at the rate of 0.05 kg s–1. What is the speed of the trolley after the entire sand bag is empty ?

Given

  • Trolley 300 kg; sandbag 25 kg; 27 km/h; frictionless track; sand leaks at 0.05 kg s−1.

Asked

The trolley’s speed once the bag is empty.

How the animation is drawn

  • The 500 s of leaking is shown in 12 s.

Answer

  1. The sand falls straight down relative to the trolley, so as it leaves it still has the trolley’s horizontal velocity.
  2. No horizontal force acts on the trolley (frictionless, and the leaking sand pushes nothing back).
  3. So its speed does not change.

27 km/h (unchanged).
