5.10 A body is moving unidirectionally under the influence of a source of constant power. Its displacement in time t is proportional to (i) t1/2 (ii) t (iii) t3/2 (iv) t2

Given

  • Constant power P; motion in one direction (from rest).

Asked

How the displacement depends on t.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a short calculation with no motion to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Work done = Pt = ½mv2, so v ∝ t1/2.
  2. dx/dt ∝ t1/2 gives x ∝ t3/2.

(iii) t3/2
