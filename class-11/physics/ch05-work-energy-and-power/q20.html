5.20 A body of mass 0.5 kg travels in a straight line with velocity v = a x3/2 where a = 5 m–1/2 s–1. What is the work done by the net force during its displacement from x = 0 to x = 2 m ?

Given

  • m = 0.5 kg; v = a x3/2, a = 5 m−1/2 s−1; from x = 0 to 2 m.

Asked

The work done by the net force.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a short calculation with no motion to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. At x = 0: v = 0. At x = 2 m: v = 5 × 23/2 ≈ 14.1 m s−1, v2 = 25 × 8 = 200.
  2. Work = ΔK = ½ × 0.5 × 200 − 0 = 50 J.

50 J
