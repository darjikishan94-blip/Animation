5.12 An electron and a proton are detected in a cosmic ray experiment, the first with kinetic energy 10 keV, and the second with 100 keV. Which is faster, the electron or the proton ? Obtain the ratio of their speeds. (electron mass = 9.11×10-31 kg, proton mass = 1.67×10–27 kg, 1 eV = 1.60 ×10–19 J).

Given

  • Electron: K = 10 keV, m = 9.11 × 10−31 kg. Proton: K = 100 keV, m = 1.67 × 10−27 kg. 1 eV = 1.60 × 10−19 J.

Asked

Which is faster, and the ratio of speeds.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a short calculation with no motion to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. v = √(2K/m).
  2. Electron: K = 1.6 × 10−15 J, ve = √(3.2 × 10−15 / 9.11 × 10−31) ≈ 5.9 × 107 m s−1.
  3. Proton: K = 1.6 × 10−14 J, vp = √(3.2 × 10−14 / 1.67 × 10−27) ≈ 4.4 × 106 m s−1.
  4. ve/vp ≈ 13.5.

The electron; ve : vp ≈ 13.5 : 1.
