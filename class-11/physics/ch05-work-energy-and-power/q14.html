5.14 A molecule in a gas container hits a horizontal wall with speed 200 m s–1 and angle 30° with the normal, and rebounds with the same speed. Is momentum conserved in the collision ? Is the collision elastic or inelastic ?

Given

  • Speed 200 m s−1 before and after; angle 30° with the normal.

Asked

Is momentum conserved? Elastic or inelastic?

How the animation is drawn

  • Slowed down enormously.

Answer

  1. The molecule’s own momentum is not conserved: its normal component reverses, from the wall’s push. The momentum of molecule + wall (+ container) together is conserved.
  2. The speed (so the kinetic energy) is the same before and after: the collision is elastic.

Total momentum (molecule + wall) is conserved; the collision is elastic.
