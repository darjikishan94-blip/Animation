5.17 The bob A of a pendulum released from 30° to the vertical hits another bob B of the same mass at rest on a table as shown in Fig. 5.15. How high does the bob A rise after the collision ? Neglect the size of the bobs and assume the collision to be elastic.

Given

  • Bob A released from 30° to the vertical; B of equal mass at rest at the lowest point; elastic collision.

Asked

How high A rises after the collision.

How the animation is drawn

  • The string length is not given; B slides on the frictionless table.

Answer

  1. In an elastic head-on collision of equal masses the velocities are exchanged.
  2. A stops at the lowest point and B moves off with A’s speed.
  3. So A does not rise at all.

A does not rise: it comes to rest (height 0).
