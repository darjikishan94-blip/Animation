8.11 A 14.5 kg mass, fastened to the end of a steel wire of unstretched length 1.0 m, is whirled in a vertical circle with an angular velocity of 2 rev/s at the bottom of the circle. The cross-sectional area of the wire is 0.065 cm2. Calculate the elongation of the wire when the mass is at the lowest point of its path.

Given

  • m = 14.5 kg; L = 1.0 m; ω = 2 rev/s at the bottom; A = 0.065 cm2; Y (steel) = 2.0 × 1011 N m−2.

Asked

The elongation at the lowest point.

How the animation is drawn

  • Shown 4 times slower than real; tension arrows not to scale.

Answer

  1. At the bottom: T − mg = mω2r, with ω = 2 × 2π = 4π rad s−1, r = 1.0 m.
  2. T = 14.5 × 9.8 + 14.5 × (4π)2 × 1.0 ≈ 142 + 2290 ≈ 2432 N.
  3. ΔL = TL/(AY) = 2432 × 1.0/(0.065 × 10−4 × 2.0 × 1011) ≈ 1.87 × 10−3 m.
