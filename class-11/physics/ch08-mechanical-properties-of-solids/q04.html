8.4 Read the following two statements below carefully and state, with reasons, if it is true or false. (a) The Young’s modulus of rubber is greater than that of steel; (b) The stretching of a coil is determined by its shear modulus.

Given

  • Two statements.

Asked

True or false, with reasons.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a judgement with reasons; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) False: for the same stress, rubber stretches far more than steel, so its Young’s modulus (stress/strain) is much smaller.
  2. (b) True: when a coil spring is stretched, the wire is twisted, not lengthened: its shape changes at constant volume, which is governed by the shear modulus.

(a) False (b) True
