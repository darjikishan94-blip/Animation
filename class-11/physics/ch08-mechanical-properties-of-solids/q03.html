8.3 The stress-strain graphs for materials A and B are shown in Fig. 8.10. The graphs are drawn to the same scale. (a) Which of the materials has the greater Young’s modulus? (b) Which of the two is the stronger material?

Given

  • Fig. 8.10: stress–strain curves of A and B, same scale.

Asked

(a) Greater Young’s modulus (b) stronger material.

How the animation is drawn

  • Curves follow the shapes in Fig. 8.10, overlaid on one set of axes.

Answer

  1. (a) A: its straight initial part is steeper (larger stress for the same strain).
  2. (b) A: it withstands a larger stress before breaking (higher ultimate strength).

(a) A (b) A
