8.10 A rigid bar of mass 15 kg is supported symmetrically by three wires each 2.0 m long. Those at each end are of copper and the middle one is of iron. Determine the ratios of their diameters if each is to have the same tension.

Given

  • Bar 15 kg; three wires of 2.0 m (copper, iron, copper), symmetric; equal tensions.
  • Y (copper) = 1.1 × 1011, Y (iron) = 1.9 × 1011 N m−2 (Table 8.1).

Asked

The ratio of the diameters.

How the animation is drawn

  • Wire thicknesses exaggerated but in the right ratio.

Answer

  1. Each tension = 15 × 9.8/3 = 49 N. The bar is rigid and level, so all wires stretch by the same ΔL; their lengths are equal too.
  2. T = YAΔL/L is the same for all, so Y × A is the same: ACu/AFe = YFe/YCu = 190/110.
  3. dCu/dFe = √(190/110) ≈ 1.31.

dcopper : diron ≈ 1.31 : 1.
