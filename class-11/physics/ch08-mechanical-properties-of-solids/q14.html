8.14 Compute the fractional change in volume of a glass slab, when subjected to a hydraulic pressure of 10 atm.

Given

  • p = 10 atm = 1.013 × 106 Pa; B (glass) = 37 × 109 Pa (Table 8.3).

Asked

ΔV/V.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. ΔV/V = p/B = 1.013 × 106/3.7 × 1010 ≈ 2.7 × 10−5 (a decrease).

About 2.7 × 10−5.
