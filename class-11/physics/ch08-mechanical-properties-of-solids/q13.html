8.13 What is the density of water at a depth where pressure is 80.0 atm, given that its density at the surface is 1.03 × 103 kg m–3?

Given

  • Pressure 80.0 atm (taking the increase as 80.0 atm = 8.10 × 106 Pa); surface density 1.03 × 103 kg m−3; B (water) = 2.2 × 109 Pa (Table 8.3).

Asked

The density at that depth.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. ΔV/V = Δp/B = 8.10 × 106/2.2 × 109 ≈ 3.68 × 10−3.
  2. Density ∝ 1/V: ρ′ = ρ/(1 − ΔV/V) = 1.03 × 103/(1 − 0.00368) ≈ 1.034 × 103 kg m−3.

About 1.034 × 103 kg m−3.
