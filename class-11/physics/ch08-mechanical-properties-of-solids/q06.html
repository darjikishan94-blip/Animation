8.6 The edge of an aluminium cube is 10 cm long. One face of the cube is firmly fixed to a vertical wall. A mass of 100 kg is then attached to the opposite face of the cube. The shear modulus of aluminium is 25 GPa. What is the vertical deflection of this face?

Given

  • Edge 10 cm; mass 100 kg; G = 25 GPa.

Asked

The vertical deflection of the free face.

How the animation is drawn

  • Cube drawn 200 px for 10 cm; the deflection is exaggerated about 40 000 times.

Answer

  1. Shear stress = F/A = 100 × 9.8 / (0.10)2 = 9.8 × 104 N m−2.
  2. Shear strain = stress/G = 9.8 × 104/25 × 109 = 3.92 × 10−6.
  3. Deflection = strain × edge = 3.92 × 10−6 × 0.10 = 3.92 × 10−7 m.

3.92 × 10−7 m.
