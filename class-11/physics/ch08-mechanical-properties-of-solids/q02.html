8.2 Figure 8.9 shows the strain-stress curve for a given material. What are (a) Young’s modulus and (b) approximate yield strength for this material?

Given

  • Fig. 8.9: stress (106 N m−2) against strain; straight up to about 300 at strain 0.004.

Asked

(a) Young’s modulus (b) approximate yield strength.

How the animation is drawn

  • The curve follows Fig. 8.9.

Answer

  1. (a) Y = slope of the straight part = 150 × 106 / 0.002 = 7.5 × 1010 N m−2.
  2. (b) The curve stops being straight at about 300 × 106 N m−2: yield strength ≈ 3 × 108 N m−2.

(a) 7.5 × 1010 N m−2 (b) ≈ 3 × 108 N m−2
