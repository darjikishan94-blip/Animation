8.8 A piece of copper having a rectangular cross-section of 15.2 mm × 19.1 mm is pulled in tension with 44,500 N force, producing only elastic deformation. Calculate the resulting strain?

Given

  • Cross-section 15.2 mm × 19.1 mm; tension 44 500 N; Y (copper) = 1.1 × 1011 N m−2 (Table 8.1).

Asked

The strain.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. A = 15.2 × 10−3 × 19.1 × 10−3 ≈ 2.90 × 10−4 m2; stress = 44 500/2.90 × 10−4 ≈ 1.53 × 108 N m−2.
  2. Strain = stress/Y ≈ 1.53 × 108/1.1 × 1011 ≈ 1.4 × 10−3.
  3. (Some solution books divide by copper’s shear modulus, 42 GPa, and get 3.65 × 10−3; for a pull, Young’s modulus is the right one.)

About 1.4 × 10−3.
