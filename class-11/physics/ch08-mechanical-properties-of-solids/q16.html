8.16 How much should the pressure on a litre of water be changed to compress it by 0.10%?

Given

  • ΔV/V = 0.10% = 1.0 × 10−3; B (water) = 2.2 × 109 Pa (Table 8.3).

Asked

The pressure change.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Δp = B × ΔV/V = 2.2 × 109 × 1.0 × 10−3 = 2.2 × 106 Pa (about 22 atm).

2.2 × 106 Pa.
