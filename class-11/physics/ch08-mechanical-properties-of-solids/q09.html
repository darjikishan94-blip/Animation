8.9 A steel cable with a radius of 1.5 cm supports a chairlift at a ski area. If the maximum stress is not to exceed 108 N m–2, what is the maximum load the cable can support ?

Given

  • Radius 1.5 cm; maximum stress 108 N m−2.

Asked

The maximum load.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Area = π(0.015)2 ≈ 7.07 × 10−4 m2.
  2. Maximum load = stress × area = 108 × 7.07 × 10−4 ≈ 7.07 × 104 N.

About 7.07 × 104 N.
