8.5 Two wires of diameter 0.25 cm, one made of steel and the other made of brass are loaded as shown in Fig. 8.11. The unloaded length of steel wire is 1.5 m and that of brass wire is 1.0 m. Compute the elongations of the steel and the brass wires.

Given

  • Diameter 0.25 cm (both); steel 1.5 m with 4.0 kg below it; brass 1.0 m with 6.0 kg (Fig. 8.11).
  • Ysteel = 2.0 × 1011 N m−2 (Table 8.1); Ybrass = 0.91 × 1011 N m−2 (standard value).

Asked

The elongation of each wire.

How the animation is drawn

  • Elongations exaggerated about 2000 times.

Answer

  1. Area A = π(0.125 × 10−2)2 ≈ 4.91 × 10−6 m2.
  2. Steel carries both masses: F = (4.0 + 6.0) × 9.8 = 98 N. ΔL = FL/(AY) = 98 × 1.5/(4.91 × 10−6 × 2.0 × 1011) ≈ 1.5 × 10−4 m.
  3. Brass carries only 6.0 kg: F = 58.8 N. ΔL = 58.8 × 1.0/(4.91 × 10−6 × 0.91 × 1011) ≈ 1.3 × 10−4 m.

Steel ≈ 1.5 × 10−4 m; brass ≈ 1.3 × 10−4 m.
