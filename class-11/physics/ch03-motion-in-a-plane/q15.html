3.15 An aircraft executes a horizontal loop of radius 1.00 km with a steady speed of 900 km/h. Compare its centripetal acceleration with the acceleration due to gravity.

Given

  • Radius 1.00 km; speed 900 km/h, steady.

Asked

The ratio of centripetal acceleration to g.

How the animation is drawn

  • Seen from above; the loop takes about 25 s in reality and is shown in 10 s.

Answer

  1. v = 900 km/h = 250 m s−1.
  2. a = v2/r = 2502/1000 = 62.5 m s−2.
  3. a/g = 62.5/9.8 ≈ 6.4.

The centripetal acceleration is about 6.4 g.
