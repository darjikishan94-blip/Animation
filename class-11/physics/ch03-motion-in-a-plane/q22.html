3.22 An aircraft is flying at a height of 3400 m above the ground. If the angle subtended at a ground observation point by the aircraft positions 10.0 s apart is 30°, what is the speed of the aircraft ?

Given

  • Height 3400 m; positions 10.0 s apart subtend 30° at the observer.

Asked

The speed of the aircraft.

How the animation is drawn

  • As in the NCERT solution, the two positions are taken symmetric about the observer’s vertical. 0.09 px per metre.

Answer

  1. Each half-angle is 15°: half the distance = 3400 tan 15° ≈ 911 m.
  2. Distance in 10.0 s = 2 × 911 ≈ 1822 m.
  3. Speed = 1822 / 10.0 ≈ 182 m s−1.

About 182 m s−1.
