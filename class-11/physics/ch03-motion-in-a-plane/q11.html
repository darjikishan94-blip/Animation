3.11 A passenger arriving in a new town wishes to go from the station to a hotel located 10 km away on a straight road from the station. A dishonest cabman takes him along a circuitous path 23 km long and reaches the hotel in 28 min. What is (a) the average speed of the taxi, (b) the magnitude of average velocity ? Are the two equal ?

Given

  • Straight-line distance station to hotel = 10 km; path taken = 23 km; time = 28 min.

Asked

(a) Average speed (b) magnitude of average velocity; are they equal?

How the animation is drawn

  • The winding route is illustrative; only its length is given.

Answer

  1. 28 min = 28/60 h.
  2. (a) Average speed = 23 / (28/60) ≈ 49.3 km h−1.
  3. (b) |average velocity| = 10 / (28/60) ≈ 21.4 km h−1.
  4. Not equal: the path (23 km) is longer than the displacement (10 km).

(a) 49.3 km h−1 (b) 21.4 km h−1; not equal.
