3.19 î and ĵ are unit vectors along x- and y- axis respectively. What is the magnitude and direction of the vectors î + ĵ , and î − ĵ ? What are the components of a vector A = 2 î + 3ĵ along the directions of î + ĵ and î − ĵ ? [You may use graphical method]

Given

  • A = 2î + 3ĵ.

Asked

Magnitude and direction of î + ĵ and î − ĵ; components of A along them.

How the animation is drawn

  • Grid of unit squares, 70 px per unit.

Answer

  1. |î + ĵ| = √(1 + 1) = √2, at 45° above the x-axis. |î − ĵ| = √2, at 45° below the x-axis.
  2. Component of A along î + ĵ: A · (î + ĵ)/√2 = (2 + 3)/√2 = 5/√2 ≈ 3.54.
  3. Component of A along î − ĵ: A · (î − ĵ)/√2 = (2 − 3)/√2 = −1/√2 ≈ −0.71.

√2 at ±45°; components 5/√2 and −1/√2.
