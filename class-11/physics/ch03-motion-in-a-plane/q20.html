3.20 For any arbitrary motion in space, which of the following relations are true : (a) vaverage = (1/2) (v(t1) + v(t2)) (b) vaverage = [r(t2) − r(t1)] /(t2 – t1) (c) v(t) = v(0) + a t (d) r(t) = r(0) + v(0) t + (1/2) a t2 (e) aaverage = [v(t2) − v(t1)] /(t2 – t1) (The ‘average’ stands for average of the quantity over the time interval t1 to t2)

Given

  • Five relations.

Asked

Which are true for any motion.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a classification or a judgement rather than describing one physical situation; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (b) and (e) are the definitions of average velocity and average acceleration: always true.
  2. (a), (c) and (d) hold only for uniform (constant) acceleration, not for arbitrary motion.

(b) and (e).
