3.8 Three girls skating on a circular ice ground of radius 200 m start from a point P on the edge of the ground and reach a point Q diametrically opposite to P following different paths as shown in Fig. 3.19. What is the magnitude of the displacement vector for each ? For which girl is this equal to the actual length of path skate ?

Given

  • Radius of the ground = 200 m; P and Q diametrically opposite.
  • Paths as in Fig. 3.19 (B goes straight along the diameter).

Asked

The magnitude of each girl’s displacement; for whom it equals the path length.

How the animation is drawn

  • The curved paths are drawn to match Fig. 3.19; their lengths are not given.

Answer

  1. Displacement depends only on the start P and end Q: |PQ| = diameter = 2 × 200 = 400 m for all three.
  2. Only B skates along the straight line PQ, so only for B is the path length equal to 400 m.

400 m for each; equal to the path length only for girl B.
