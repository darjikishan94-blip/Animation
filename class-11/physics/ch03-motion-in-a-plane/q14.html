3.14 A stone tied to the end of a string 80 cm long is whirled in a horizontal circle with a constant speed. If the stone makes 14 revolutions in 25 s, what is the magnitude and direction of acceleration of the stone ?

Given

  • Radius r = 80 cm = 0.80 m; 14 revolutions in 25 s; constant speed.

Asked

The magnitude and direction of the acceleration.

How the animation is drawn

  • Real time, seen from above.

Answer

  1. ω = 2π × 14/25 ≈ 3.52 rad s−1.
  2. a = ω2r = 3.522 × 0.80 ≈ 9.9 m s−2.
  3. Direction: along the string, towards the centre, at every instant (centripetal).

≈ 9.9 m s−2, towards the centre.
