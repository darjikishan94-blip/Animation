3.18 A particle starts from the origin at t = 0 s with a velocity of 10.0 ĵ m/s and moves in the x-y plane with a constant acceleration of (8.0î + 2.0ĵ) m s-2. (a) At what time is the x- coordinate of the particle 16 m? What is the y-coordinate of the particle at that time? (b) What is the speed of the particle at the time ?

Given

  • Starts at the origin; v(0) = 10.0 ĵ m s−1; a = 8.0 î + 2.0 ĵ m s−2.

Asked

(a) The time when x = 16 m and y then (b) the speed then.

How the animation is drawn

  • Real time; 12 px per metre.

Answer

  1. x = ½ × 8.0 × t2 = 4t2 = 16, so t = 2.0 s.
  2. y = 10.0t + ½ × 2.0 × t2 = 20 + 4 = 24 m.
  3. v = (8.0t) î + (10.0 + 2.0t) ĵ = 16 î + 14 ĵ at 2 s; speed = √(162 + 142) ≈ 21.3 m s−1.

(a) t = 2.0 s, y = 24 m (b) ≈ 21.3 m s−1
