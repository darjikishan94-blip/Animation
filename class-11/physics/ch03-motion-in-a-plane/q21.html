3.21 Read each statement below carefully and state, with reasons and examples, if it is true or false : A scalar quantity is one that (a) is conserved in a process (b) can never take negative values (c) must be dimensionless (d) does not vary from one point to another in space (e) has the same value for observers with different orientations of axes.

Given

  • Five statements about scalars.

Asked

True or false, with reasons and examples.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a classification or a judgement rather than describing one physical situation; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) False: kinetic energy is a scalar but is not conserved in an inelastic collision.
  2. (b) False: temperature in °C or potential energy can be negative.
  3. (c) False: mass and speed are scalars with dimensions.
  4. (d) False: gravitational potential varies from point to point.
  5. (e) True: a scalar has no direction, so rotating the axes does not change it.

(a) F (b) F (c) F (d) F (e) T
