3.13 A cricketer can throw a ball to a maximum horizontal distance of 100 m. How much high above the ground can the cricketer throw the same ball ?

Given

  • Maximum horizontal range = 100 m.

Asked

The greatest height the same ball can be thrown.

How the animation is drawn

  • 3.6 px per metre; flight shown at 2 × speed.

Answer

  1. Maximum range is at 45°: Rmax = u2/g = 100 m.
  2. Thrown straight up with the same speed: H = u2/2g = Rmax/2 = 50 m.

50 m.
