3.9 A cyclist starts from the centre O of a circular park of radius 1 km, reaches the edge P of the park, then cycles along the circumference, and returns to the centre along QO as shown in Fig. 3.20. If the round trip takes 10 min, what is the (a) net displacement, (b) average velocity, and (c) average speed of the cyclist ?

Given

  • Radius = 1 km; route O → P → (quarter circle) → Q → O (Fig. 3.20); round trip 10 min.

Asked

(a) Net displacement (b) average velocity (c) average speed.

How the animation is drawn

  • 10 minutes shown in 12 s.

Answer

  1. (a) He ends where he started: net displacement = 0.
  2. (b) Average velocity = displacement / time = 0.
  3. (c) Path = OP + arc PQ + QO = 1 + (π/2)(1) + 1 ≈ 3.57 km. Time = 10 min = 1/6 h. Average speed = 3.57 × 6 ≈ 21.4 km h−1.

(a) 0 (b) 0 (c) ≈ 21.4 km h−1
