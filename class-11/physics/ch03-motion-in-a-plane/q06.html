3.6 Establish the following vector inequalities geometrically or otherwise : (a) |a+b| ≤ |a| + |b| (b) |a+b| ≥ ||a| − |b|| (c) |a−b| ≤ |a| + |b| (d) |a−b| ≥ ||a| − |b||. When does the equality sign above apply?

Given

  • Any two vectors a and b.

Asked

Prove the four inequalities and say when equality holds.

How the animation is drawn

  • The lengths of a and b are illustrative (none given).

Answer

  1. (a) a, b and a + b form a triangle; one side of a triangle is less than the sum of the other two. Equality when a and b point the same way (the triangle flattens).
  2. (b) Likewise, a side is greater than the difference of the other two. Equality when a and b point opposite ways.
  3. (c) a, −b and a − b form a triangle: |a − b| ≤ |a| + |−b| = |a| + |b|. Equality when a and b are opposite.
  4. (d) |a − b| ≥ ||a| − |b||, with equality when a and b point the same way.

Triangle inequality; equality when the vectors are parallel or antiparallel.
