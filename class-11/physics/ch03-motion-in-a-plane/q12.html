3.12 The ceiling of a long hall is 25 m high. What is the maximum horizontal distance that a ball thrown with a speed of 40 m s-1 can go without hitting the ceiling of the hall ?

Given

  • Ceiling height 25 m; launch speed 40 m s−1. (g = 9.8 m s−2.)

Asked

The greatest horizontal range without touching the ceiling.

How the animation is drawn

  • Thrown from floor level; 3.4 px per metre; flight shown at 2 × speed.

Answer

  1. Range grows with angle up to 45°, but so does height. The best throw just reaches 25 m.
  2. H = u2 sin2θ / 2g = 25: sin2θ = 25 × 2 × 9.8 / 402 ≈ 0.306, so θ ≈ 33.6°.
  3. R = u2 sin 2θ / g = 1600 × sin 67.2° / 9.8 ≈ 150.5 m.

About 150.5 m (at θ ≈ 33.6°).
