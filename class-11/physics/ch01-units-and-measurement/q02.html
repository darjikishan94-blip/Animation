1.2 Fill in the blanks by suitable conversion of units (a) 1 kg m2 s–2 = ....g cm2 s–2 (b) 1 m = ..... ly (c) 3.0 m s–2 = .... km h–2 (d) G = 6.67 × 10–11 N m2 (kg)–2 = .... (cm)3 s–2 g–1.

Given

  • The four quantities in their original units.

Asked

Each quantity in the new units.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation (or an explanation) with no physical situation to animate; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) 1 kg = 103 g and 1 m2 = 104 cm2, so 1 kg m2 s−2 = 107 g cm2 s−2.
  2. (b) 1 ly = 9.46 × 1015 m, so 1 m = 1/(9.46 × 1015) ly ≈ 1.06 × 10−16 ly.
  3. (c) 1 m = 10−3 km and 1 s = 1/3600 h, so 3.0 m s−2 = 3.0 × 10−3 × (3600)2 km h−2 ≈ 3.9 × 104 km h−2.
  4. (d) 1 N = 1 kg m s−2, so G = 6.67 × 10−11 m3 s−2 kg−1 = 6.67 × 10−11 × 106 cm3 s−2 × 10−3 g−1 = 6.67 × 10−8 cm3 s−2 g−1.

(a) 107 (b) 10−16 (c) 3.9 × 104 (d) 6.67 × 10−8
