1.6 Which of the following is the most precise device for measuring length : (a) a vernier callipers with 20 divisions on the sliding scale (b) a screw gauge of pitch 1 mm and 100 divisions on the circular scale (c) an optical instrument that can measure length to within a wavelength of light ?

Given

  • Vernier: 20 divisions on the sliding scale; screw gauge: pitch 1 mm, 100 divisions; optical instrument: within one wavelength of light.

Asked

The most precise of the three.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation (or an explanation) with no physical situation to animate; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Vernier callipers (main scale in mm): least count = 1 mm / 20 = 0.05 mm.
  2. Screw gauge: least count = 1 mm / 100 = 0.01 mm.
  3. Optical instrument: about one wavelength of visible light ≈ 5 × 10−7 m = 0.0005 mm.

(c) the optical instrument is the most precise.
