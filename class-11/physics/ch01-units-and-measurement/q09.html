1.9 The photograph of a house occupies an area of 1.75 cm2 on a 35 mm slide. The slide is projected on to a screen, and the area of the house on the screen is 1.55 m2. What is the linear magnification of the projector-screen arrangement.

Given

  • Area of the house on the slide = 1.75 cm2; area on the screen = 1.55 m2.

Asked

The linear magnification.

How the animation is drawn

  • Not to scale: a linear magnification of about 94 cannot be drawn on one screen.

Answer

  1. Areal magnification = 1.55 m2 / 1.75 cm2 = 1.55 × 104 cm2 / 1.75 cm2 ≈ 8.86 × 103.
  2. Areas grow as the square of lengths, so linear magnification = √(8.86 × 103) ≈ 94.1.

Linear magnification ≈ 94.1.
