1.1 Fill in the blanks (a) The volume of a cube of side 1 cm is equal to .....m3 (b) The surface area of a solid cylinder of radius 2.0 cm and height 10.0 cm is equal to ...(mm)2 (c) A vehicle moving with a speed of 18 km h–1 covers....m in 1 s (d) The relative density of lead is 11.3. Its density is ....g cm–3 or ....kg m–3.

Given

  • Cube side 1 cm; cylinder radius 2.0 cm, height 10.0 cm; speed 18 km h−1; relative density of lead 11.3.

Asked

The four missing values.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation (or an explanation) with no physical situation to animate; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) 1 cm3 = (10−2 m)3 = 10−6 m3.
  2. (b) Area = 2πr(r + h) = 2π × 20 mm × (20 + 100) mm ≈ 1.5 × 104 mm2 (2 significant figures, as in 2.0 cm).
  3. (c) 18 km h−1 = 18 × 1000 m / 3600 s = 5 m s−1, so 5 m in 1 s.
  4. (d) Density = 11.3 × density of water = 11.3 g cm−3 = 1.13 × 104 kg m−3.

(a) 10−6 (b) 1.5 × 104 (c) 5 (d) 11.3; 1.13 × 104
