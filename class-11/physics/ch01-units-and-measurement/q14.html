1.14 The unit of length convenient on the atomic scale is known as an angstrom and is denoted by Å: 1 Å = 10–10 m. The size of a hydrogen atom is about 0.5 Å. What is the total atomic volume in m3 of a mole of hydrogen atoms ?

Given

  • 1 Å = 10−10 m; size (radius) of a hydrogen atom ≈ 0.5 Å; one mole = 6.023 × 1023 atoms.

Asked

The total atomic volume of a mole of hydrogen atoms.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation (or an explanation) with no physical situation to animate; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Volume of one atom = (4/3)πr3 = (4/3) × 3.14 × (0.5 × 10−10 m)3 ≈ 5.23 × 10−31 m3.
  2. For a mole: 6.023 × 1023 × 5.23 × 10−31 m3 ≈ 3.15 × 10−7 m3.
