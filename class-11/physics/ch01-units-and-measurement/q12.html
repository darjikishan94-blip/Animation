1.12 The mass of a box measured by a grocer’s balance is 2.30 kg. Two gold pieces of masses 20.15 g and 20.17 g are added to the box. What is (a) the total mass of the box, (b) the difference in the masses of the pieces to correct significant figures ?

Given

  • Box: 2.30 kg (grocer’s balance). Gold pieces: 20.15 g and 20.17 g.

Asked

(a) The total mass; (b) the difference in the masses of the pieces.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation (or an explanation) with no physical situation to animate; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Total = 2.30 kg + 0.02015 kg + 0.02017 kg = 2.34032 kg. A sum keeps the decimal places of the least precise term (2.30 kg: two places), giving 2.34 kg. (The NCERT answer key rounds further, to 2.3 kg, since the box was weighed only on a grocer’s balance.)
  2. (b) Difference = 20.17 g − 20.15 g = 0.02 g (two decimal places, as in both masses).

(a) 2.34 kg (NCERT key: 2.3 kg) (b) 0.02 g
