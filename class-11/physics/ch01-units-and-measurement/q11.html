1.11 The length, breadth and thickness of a rectangular sheet of metal are 4.234 m, 1.005 m, and 2.01 cm respectively. Give the area and volume of the sheet to correct significant figures.

Given

  • Length 4.234 m, breadth 1.005 m, thickness 2.01 cm = 0.0201 m.

Asked

The (surface) area and the volume, to correct significant figures.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation (or an explanation) with no physical situation to animate; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Surface area = 2(lb + bt + tl) = 2(4.234 × 1.005 + 1.005 × 0.0201 + 0.0201 × 4.234) m2 = 2 × 4.3604 m2 = 8.7209 m2.
  2. Volume = lbt = 4.234 × 1.005 × 0.0201 m3 = 0.085529 m3.
  3. The thickness has the fewest significant figures (3), so both results keep 3.

Area ≈ 8.72 m2; volume ≈ 0.0855 m3.
