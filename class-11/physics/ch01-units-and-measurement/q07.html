1.7 A student measures the thickness of a human hair by looking at it through a microscope of magnification 100. He makes 20 observations and finds that the average width of the hair in the field of view of the microscope is 3.5 mm. What is the estimate on the thickness of hair ?

Given

  • Magnification = 100.
  • 20 observations; average width in the field of view = 3.5 mm.

Asked

The estimated thickness of the hair.

How the animation is drawn

  • The image width is drawn at 30 px per mm; the hair itself is then about one pixel wide.

Answer

  1. Magnification = width of the image / actual width.
  2. Actual thickness = 3.5 mm / 100 = 0.035 mm.

About 0.035 mm (3.5 × 10−5 m).
