7.7 Does the escape speed of a body from the earth depend on (a) the mass of the body, (b) the location from where it is projected, (c) the direction of projection, (d) the height of the location from where the body is launched?

Given

  • ve = √(2GM/r).

Asked

Which factors it depends on.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for reasoning or a choice rather than describing one physical situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) No: the body’s mass cancels.
  2. (b) Yes: it depends on the gravitational potential at the launch point (latitude, local terrain), though only slightly.
  3. (c) No: energy is a scalar; direction does not matter (ignoring the Earth’s rotation).
  4. (d) Yes: from a greater height r is larger, so ve is smaller.

(a) no (b) yes (c) no (d) yes
