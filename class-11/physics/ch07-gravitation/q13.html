7.13 How will you ‘weigh the sun’, that is estimate its mass? The mean orbital radius of the earth around the sun is 1.5 × 108 km.

Given

  • r = 1.5 × 1011 m; T = 1 year = 3.15 × 107 s; G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m2 kg−2.

Asked

The mass of the Sun.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation with no motion worth animating; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. The Sun’s gravity provides the Earth’s centripetal force: GMSm/r2 = m(2π/T)2r.
  2. MS = 4π2r3/(GT2) = 4π2 × (1.5 × 1011)3 / (6.67 × 10−11 × (3.15 × 107)2) ≈ 2 × 1030 kg.
