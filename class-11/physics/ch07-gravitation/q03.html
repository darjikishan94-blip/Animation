7.3 Suppose there existed a planet that went around the Sun twice as fast as the earth. What would be its orbital size as compared to that of the earth ?

Given

  • The planet’s period is half the Earth’s: Tp = Te/2.

Asked

The planet’s orbital radius compared with the Earth’s.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circular orbits; 1 year shown in 8 s.

Answer

  1. Kepler’s third law: T2 ∝ r3, so rp/re = (Tp/Te)2/3 = (1/2)2/3 ≈ 0.63.

About 0.63 times the Earth’s orbital radius.
