7.14 A saturn year is 29.5 times the earth year. How far is the saturn from the sun if the earth is 1.50 × 108 km away from the sun ?

Given

  • TSaturn = 29.5 TEarth; rEarth = 1.50 × 108 km.

Asked

Saturn’s distance from the Sun.

How the animation is drawn

  • Circular orbits to scale with each other; Earth’s year shown in 1.5 s.

Answer

  1. T2 ∝ r3: rS = rE × (29.5)2/3 = 1.50 × 108 × 9.55 ≈ 1.43 × 109 km.

About 1.43 × 109 km.
