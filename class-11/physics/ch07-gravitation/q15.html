7.15 A body weighs 63 N on the surface of the earth. What is the gravitational force on it due to the earth at a height equal to half the radius of the earth ?

Given

  • Weight at the surface 63 N; height h = R/2.

Asked

The gravitational force at h = R/2.

How the animation is drawn

  • Earth drawn with R = 130 px; force arrow length to scale.

Answer

  1. F ∝ 1/r2; r goes from R to 1.5R.
  2. F = 63 × (R/1.5R)2 = 63 × 4/9 = 28 N.

28 N.
