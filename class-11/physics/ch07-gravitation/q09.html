7.9 Which of the following symptoms is likely to afflict an astronaut in space (a) swollen feet, (b) swollen face, (c) headache, (d) orientational problem.

Given

  • Weightlessness in orbit.

Asked

The likely symptoms.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for reasoning or a choice rather than describing one physical situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Without the pull of gravity, body fluids shift towards the head: a swollen face (b) and headache (c).
  2. With no sense of “down”, the balance organs are confused: orientation problems (d).
  3. Swollen feet (a) do not occur: fluids are not pulled down to the legs.

(b), (c) and (d).
