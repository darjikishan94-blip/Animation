7.18 The escape speed of a projectile on the earth’s surface is 11.2 km s–1. A body is projected out with thrice this speed. What is the speed of the body far away from the earth? Ignore the presence of the sun and other planets.

Given

  • ve = 11.2 km s−1; launch speed 3ve.

Asked

The speed far from the Earth.

How the animation is drawn

  • Distances in Earth radii; speed graph to scale.

Answer

  1. Energy: ½v2 − GM/R = ½v∞2, and GM/R = ½ve2.
  2. v∞2 = (3ve)2 − ve2 = 8ve2, so v∞ = √8 × 11.2 ≈ 31.7 km s−1.

About 31.7 km s−1.
