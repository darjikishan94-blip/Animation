7.17 A rocket is fired vertically with a speed of 5 km s-1 from the earth’s surface. How far from the earth does the rocket go before returning to the earth ? Mass of the earth = 6.0 × 1024 kg; mean radius of the earth = 6.4 × 106 m; G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m2 kg−2.

Given

  • Launch speed 5 km s−1, vertical; M = 6.0 × 1024 kg; R = 6.4 × 106 m; G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m2 kg−2.

Asked

The greatest height above the surface.

How the animation is drawn

  • Earth and height to scale (1 px = 32 km); flight much faster than real.

Answer

  1. Energy conservation: ½v2 − GM/R = −GM/(R + h).
  2. GM/R = 6.67 × 10−11 × 6.0 × 1024/6.4 × 106 ≈ 6.25 × 107 J/kg; ½v2 = 1.25 × 107 J/kg.
  3. GM/(R + h) = 5.0 × 107, so R + h = 8.0 × 106 m and h ≈ 1.6 × 106 m.

About 1.6 × 106 m above the surface.
