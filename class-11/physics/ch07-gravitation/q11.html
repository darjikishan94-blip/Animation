7.11 For the above problem, the direction of the gravitational intensity at an arbitrary point P is indicated by the arrow (i) d, (ii) e, (iii) f, (iv) g.

Given

  • P is any point of the flat face (rim plane) of the hemispherical shell; arrows d, e, f, g.

Asked

The direction of the field at P.

How the animation is drawn

  • A side section; the missing upper half is shown dashed.

Answer

  1. Inside a complete uniform spherical shell the field is zero everywhere.
  2. Fields of the lower and upper halves at P must therefore be equal and opposite.
  3. By symmetry (mirror image in the rim plane) their horizontal parts are equal and their vertical parts opposite; equal and opposite overall means the horizontal parts are zero.
  4. So the lower half’s field at P is vertical, pointing down: arrow e.

(ii) e
