7.16 Assuming the earth to be a sphere of uniform mass density, how much would a body weigh half way down to the centre of the earth if it weighed 250 N on the surface ?

Given

  • Weight at the surface 250 N; depth d = R/2; uniform density.

Asked

The weight at d = R/2.

How the animation is drawn

  • Earth drawn with R = 170 px; weight arrow to scale.

Answer

  1. Inside a uniform Earth, g = g₀(1 − d/R): only the mass inside radius (R − d) attracts the body.
  2. At d = R/2: weight = 250 × (1 − 1/2) = 125 N.

125 N.
