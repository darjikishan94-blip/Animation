7.5 Let us assume that our galaxy consists of 2.5 × 1011 stars each of one solar mass. How long will a star at a distance of 50,000 ly from the galactic centre take to complete one revolution ? Take the diameter of the Milky Way to be 105 ly.

Given

  • 2.5 × 1011 stars of 2 × 1030 kg; r = 50 000 ly = 4.73 × 1020 m.

Asked

The period of revolution.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation with no motion worth animating; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. M = 2.5 × 1011 × 2 × 1030 = 5 × 1041 kg (all treated as at the centre).
  2. T = 2π√(r3/GM) = 2π√((4.73 × 1020)3 / (6.67 × 10−11 × 5 × 1041)) ≈ 1.12 × 1016 s.
  3. ≈ 3.5 × 108 years.
