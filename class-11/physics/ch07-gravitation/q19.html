7.19 A satellite orbits the earth at a height of 400 km above the surface. How much energy must be expended to rocket the satellite out of the earth’s gravitational influence? Mass of the satellite = 200 kg; mass of the earth = 6.0×1024 kg; radius of the earth = 6.4 × 106 m; G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m2 kg−2.

Given

  • h = 400 km, so r = 6.8 × 106 m; m = 200 kg; M = 6.0 × 1024 kg; G = 6.67 × 10−11 N m2 kg−2.

Asked

The energy needed to free it from the Earth’s gravity.

How the animation is drawn

  • Orbit height exaggerated for visibility.

Answer

  1. In orbit, E = −GMm/2r = −6.67 × 10−11 × 6.0 × 1024 × 200 / (2 × 6.8 × 106) ≈ −5.9 × 109 J.
  2. To escape, its total energy must be raised to 0: energy needed ≈ 5.9 × 109 J.

About 5.9 × 109 J.
