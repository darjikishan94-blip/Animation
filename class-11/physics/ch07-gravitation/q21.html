7.21 Two heavy spheres each of mass 100 kg and radius 0.10 m are placed 1.0 m apart on a horizontal table. What is the gravitational force and potential at the mid point of the line joining the centres of the spheres ? Is an object placed at that point in equilibrium? If so, is the equilibrium stable or unstable ?

Given

  • Two spheres, 100 kg each, radius 0.10 m, centres 1.0 m apart.

Asked

Force and potential at the midpoint; equilibrium and its kind.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force arrows exaggerated (the real forces are tiny).

Answer

  1. Force: the two pulls are equal and opposite: net force zero.
  2. Potential: V = −GM/0.5 − GM/0.5 = −4 × 6.67 × 10−11 × 100 ≈ −2.7 × 10−8 J/kg.
  3. An object there is in equilibrium. Moved slightly towards one sphere, that sphere pulls harder: it moves further away from the midpoint, so the equilibrium is unstable.

Force 0; potential ≈ −2.7 × 10−8 J/kg; equilibrium, unstable.
