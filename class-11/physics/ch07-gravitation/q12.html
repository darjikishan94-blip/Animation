7.12 A rocket is fired from the earth towards the sun. At what distance from the earth’s centre is the gravitational force on the rocket zero ? Mass of the sun = 2×1030 kg, mass of the earth = 6×1024 kg. Neglect the effect of other planets etc. (orbital radius = 1.5 × 1011 m).

Given

  • MS = 2 × 1030 kg, ME = 6 × 1024 kg; Earth–Sun distance r = 1.5 × 1011 m.

Asked

The distance x from the Earth where the net force is zero.

How the animation is drawn

  • A logarithmic distance scale (the point is very close to the Earth on the Sun–Earth scale).

Answer

  1. GMEm/x2 = GMSm/(r − x)2, so (r − x)/x = √(MS/ME) = √(3.33 × 105) ≈ 577.
  2. x = r/578 ≈ 1.5 × 1011/578 ≈ 2.6 × 108 m.

About 2.6 × 108 m from the Earth’s centre.
