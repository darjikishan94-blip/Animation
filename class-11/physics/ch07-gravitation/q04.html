7.4 Io, one of the satellites of Jupiter, has an orbital period of 1.769 days and the radius of the orbit is 4.22 × 108 m. Show that the mass of Jupiter is about one-thousandth that of the sun.

Given

  • Io: T = 1.769 days, r = 4.22 × 108 m. Earth: T = 365.25 days, r = 1.496 × 1011 m (known values).

Asked

Show MJ ≈ MS/1000.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a calculation with no motion worth animating; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. For any orbit, M = 4π2r3/(GT2), so MJ/MS = (rIo/rE)3 × (TE/TIo)2.
  2. = (4.22 × 108 / 1.496 × 1011)3 × (365.25/1.769)2 ≈ 2.24 × 10−8 × 4.26 × 104 ≈ 9.6 × 10−4.
  3. That is about 1/1000.

MJ/MS ≈ 10−3.
