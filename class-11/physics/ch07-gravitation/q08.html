7.8 A comet orbits the sun in a highly elliptical orbit. Does the comet have a constant (a) linear speed, (b) angular speed, (c) angular momentum, (d) kinetic energy, (e) potential energy, (f) total energy throughout its orbit? Neglect any mass loss of the comet when it comes very close to the Sun.

Given

  • A comet in a highly elliptical orbit.

Asked

Which quantities are constant.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for reasoning or a choice rather than describing one physical situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Gravity is a central, conservative force.
  2. (c) Angular momentum: constant (central force, no torque). (f) Total energy: constant (conservative force).
  3. (a), (b), (d), (e) change: it moves fastest near the Sun and slowest far away, and its potential energy rises and falls.

Only (c) angular momentum and (f) total energy.
