13.5 A particle is in linear simple harmonic motion between two points, A and B, 10 cm apart. Take the direction from A to B as the positive direction and give the signs of velocity, acceleration and force on the particle when it is (a) at the end A, (b) at the end B, (c) at the mid-point of AB going towards A, (d) at 2 cm away from B going towards A, (e) at 3 cm away from A going towards B, and (f) at 4 cm away from B going towards A.

Given

  • SHM between A and B, 10 cm apart (amplitude 5 cm); A → B positive.

Asked

The signs of v, a and F in six situations.

How the animation is drawn

  • Period illustrative.

Answer

  1. The acceleration (and force) always points towards the midpoint O: a = −ω2x. The velocity points the way the particle is moving.
  2. (a) At A: v = 0, a +, F +.
  3. (b) At B: v = 0, a −, F −.
  4. (c) At O going to A: v −, a = 0, F = 0.
  5. (d) 2 cm from B going to A (x = +3 cm): v −, a −, F −.
  6. (e) 3 cm from A going to B (x = −2 cm): v +, a +, F +.
  7. (f) 4 cm from B going to A (x = +1 cm): v −, a −, F −.

(a) 0, +, + (b) 0, −, − (c) −, 0, 0 (d) −, −, − (e) +, +, + (f) −, −, −
