13.4 Which of the following functions of time represent (a) simple harmonic, (b) periodic but not simple harmonic, and (c) non-periodic motion? Give period for each case of periodic motion (ω is any positive constant): (a) sin ωt – cos ωt (b) sin3 ωt (c) 3 cos (π/4 – 2ωt) (d) cos ωt + cos 3ωt + cos 5ωt (e) exp (–ω2t2) (f) 1 + ωt + ω2t2

Given

  • Six functions of time.

Asked

Classify each; give the periods.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a classification or a short derivation rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) √2 sin(ωt − π/4): SHM, T = 2π/ω.
  2. (b) sin3ωt = (3 sin ωt − sin 3ωt)/4: periodic, not SHM, T = 2π/ω.
  3. (c) 3 cos(2ωt − π/4): SHM, T = π/ω.
  4. (d) Sum of three SHMs: periodic, not SHM, T = 2π/ω (the period of the slowest term).
  5. (e) exp(−ω2t2): non-periodic.
  6. (f) 1 + ωt + ω2t2: non-periodic.

SHM: (a), (c). Periodic not SHM: (b), (d). Non-periodic: (e), (f).
