13.10 In Exercise 13.9, let us take the position of mass when the spring is unstreched as x = 0, and the direction from left to right as the positive direction of x-axis. Give x as a function of time t for the oscillating mass if at the moment we start the stopwatch (t = 0), the mass is (a) at the mean position, (b) at the maximum stretched position, and (c) at the maximum compressed position. In what way do these functions for SHM differ from each other, in frequency, in amplitude or the initial phase?

Given

  • As in 13.9: A = 2 cm, ω = 20 rad s−1; x = 0 at the unstretched position; right is positive.

Asked

x(t) in the three cases; how they differ.

How the animation is drawn

  • Graphs to scale (time slowed 5 times in the animation).

Answer

  1. (a) Starting at the mean position (moving right, as it would when released from the stretched side half a cycle earlier): x = 2 sin 20t cm.
  2. (b) Starting at maximum stretch: x = 2 cos 20t cm.
  3. (c) Starting at maximum compression: x = −2 cos 20t cm.
  4. Same amplitude and frequency; only the initial phase differs.

(a) 2 sin 20t (b) 2 cos 20t (c) −2 cos 20t (cm): they differ only in initial phase.
