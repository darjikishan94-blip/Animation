13.11 Figures 13.20 correspond to two circular motions. The radius of the circle, the period of revolution, the initial position, and the sense of revolution (i.e. clockwise or anti-clockwise) are indicated on each figure. Obtain the corresponding simple harmonic motions of the x-projection of the radius vector of the revolving particle P, in each case.

Given

  • (a) r = 3 cm, T = 2 s, P starts on the −y axis, clockwise. (b) r = 2 m, T = 4 s, P starts on the −x axis.

Asked

The SHM of the x-projection in each case.

How the animation is drawn

  • Shown at real speed; the two circles are not on the same scale.

Answer

  1. (a) ω = 2π/2 = π rad s−1. At t = 0, x = 0; going clockwise from the bottom, P moves to the left, so x becomes negative: x = −3 sin πt (cm).
  2. (b) ω = 2π/4 = π/2 rad s−1. At t = 0, x = −2 m: x = −2 cos(πt/2) (m).

(a) x = −3 sin πt cm (b) x = −2 cos(πt/2) m
