13.14 The piston in the cylinder head of a locomotive has a stroke (twice the amplitude) of 1.0 m. If the piston moves with simple harmonic motion with an angular frequency of 200 rad/min, what is its maximum speed ?

Given

  • Stroke 1.0 m (amplitude 0.50 m); ω = 200 rad/min.

Asked

The maximum speed.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. vmax = ωA = 200 × 0.50 = 100 m/min.

100 m/min.
