13.18 One end of a U-tube containing mercury is connected to a suction pump and the other end to atmosphere. A small pressure difference is maintained between the two columns. Show that, when the suction pump is removed, the column of mercury in the U-tube executes simple harmonic motion.

Given

  • A mercury column of total length L and cross-section A in a U-tube; one side displaced by y.

Asked

Show that the motion is SHM.

How the animation is drawn

  • Column length and amplitude illustrative; no damping.

Answer

  1. If one surface is up by y, the other is down by y: an unbalanced column of height 2y.
  2. Restoring force = (pressure of 2y of mercury) × A = −2ρgAy, proportional to y.
  3. The whole column, mass ρAL, moves together: ρAL a = −2ρgA y, so a = −(2g/L) y: SHM with T = 2π√(L/2g).

a = −(2g/L)y: SHM with T = 2π√(L/2g).
