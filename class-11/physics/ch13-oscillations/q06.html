13.6 Which of the following relationships between the acceleration a and the displacement x of a particle involve simple harmonic motion? (a) a = 0.7x (b) a = –200x2 (c) a = –10x (d) a = 100x3

Given

  • Four relations.

Asked

Which give SHM.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a classification or a short derivation rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. SHM needs a = −(positive constant) × x: proportional to x and directed towards the mean position.
  2. Only (c) a = −10x qualifies (ω = √10 rad/s).

(c) only.
