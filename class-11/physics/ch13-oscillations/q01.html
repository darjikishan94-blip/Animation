13.1 Which of the following examples represent periodic motion? (a) A swimmer completing one (return) trip from one bank of a river to the other and back. (b) A freely suspended bar magnet displaced from its N-S direction and released. (c) A hydrogen molecule rotating about its centre of mass. (d) An arrow released from a bow.

Given

  • Four motions.

Asked

Which are periodic.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a classification or a short derivation rather than describing one situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. (a) Not periodic: a single return trip is not repeated.
  2. (b) Periodic: the magnet oscillates about the N–S direction.
  3. (c) Periodic: the rotation repeats.
  4. (d) Not periodic.

(b) and (c).
