13.17 A cylindrical piece of cork of density of base area A and height h floats in a liquid of density ρl. The cork is depressed slightly and then released. Show that the cork oscillates up and down simple harmonically with a period T = 2π √(hρ/(ρlg)) where ρ is the density of cork. (Ignore damping due to viscosity of the liquid).

Given

  • Cork: base area A, height h, density ρ; liquid density ρl.

Asked

Show the motion is SHM with T = 2π√(hρ/ρlg).

How the animation is drawn

  • Sizes and densities illustrative; no damping.

Answer

  1. Floating: the weight ρAhg is balanced by the buoyant force.
  2. Pushed down a further x, the extra submerged volume Ax gives an extra upward force ρlAgx: a restoring force F = −(ρlAg)x, proportional to x.
  3. So the motion is SHM with k = ρlAg and mass m = ρAh: T = 2π√(m/k) = 2π√(hρ/(ρlg)).

Restoring force −ρlAgx ⇒ SHM, T = 2π√(hρ/ρlg).
