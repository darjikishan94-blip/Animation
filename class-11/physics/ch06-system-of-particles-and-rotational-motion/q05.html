6.5 Show that a.(b × c) is equal in magnitude to the volume of the parallelepiped formed on the three vectors, a, b and c.

Given

  • Three vectors forming a parallelepiped.

Asked

Show |a·(b × c)| = volume.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a proof, an explanation or a short calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. |b × c| is the area of the base (the parallelogram on b and c); its direction is normal to the base.
  2. a·(b × c) = |a||b × c| cos φ, where |a| cos φ is the height of the solid above the base.
  3. So its magnitude = base area × height = volume.

|a·(b × c)| = base area × height = volume.
