6.4 Show that the area of the triangle contained between the vectors a and b is one half of the magnitude of a × b.

Given

  • Vectors a and b with angle θ between them.

Asked

Show area = ½|a × b|.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a proof, an explanation or a short calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Take a as the base. The height of the triangle is |b| sin θ.
  2. Area = ½ × base × height = ½ |a||b| sin θ = ½ |a × b|.

Area = ½ |a||b| sin θ = ½ |a × b|.
