6.16 A metre stick is balanced on a knife edge at its centre. When two coins, each of mass 5 g are put one on top of the other at the 12.0 cm mark, the stick is found to be balanced at 45.0 cm. What is the mass of the metre stick?

Given

  • Balances at 50.0 cm alone; two 5 g coins at 12.0 cm; new balance point 45.0 cm.

Asked

The mass of the metre stick.

How the animation is drawn

  • 5 px per cm.

Answer

  1. The stick’s weight acts at its centre of gravity, the 50.0 cm mark.
  2. Torques about 45.0 cm: 10 g × (45.0 − 12.0) = m × (50.0 − 45.0).
  3. 330 = 5m, so m = 66 g.

66 g.
