6.10 Torques of equal magnitude are applied to a hollow cylinder and a solid sphere, both having the same mass and radius. The cylinder is free to rotate about its standard axis of symmetry, and the sphere is free to rotate about an axis passing through its centre. Which of the two will acquire a greater angular speed after a given time.

Given

  • Same torque, mass and radius; hollow cylinder about its axis, solid sphere about a diameter.

Asked

Which reaches the greater angular speed in a given time.

How the animation is drawn

  • Torque and time illustrative; spins shown slowed.

Answer

  1. α = τ/I. Hollow cylinder: I = MR2. Solid sphere: I = (2/5)MR2.
  2. The sphere has the smaller I, so the larger α: 2.5 times the cylinder’s.
  3. Starting from rest, ω = αt: the sphere spins faster.

The solid sphere (2.5 times the angular speed).
