6.9 A car weighs 1800 kg. The distance between its front and back axles is 1.8 m. Its centre of gravity is 1.05 m behind the front axle. Determine the force exerted by the level ground on each front wheel and each back wheel.

Given

  • Mass 1800 kg; axles 1.8 m apart; centre of gravity 1.05 m behind the front axle (0.75 m in front of the back axle). (g = 9.8 m s−2.)

Asked

The normal force on each front wheel and each back wheel.

How the animation is drawn

  • Side view; 170 px per metre; forces to scale (1 px = 160 N).

Answer

  1. Weight W = 1800 × 9.8 = 17640 N.
  2. Torques about the back axle: Rf × 1.8 = W × 0.75, so Rf = 7350 N (both front wheels).
  3. Rf + Rb = W gives Rb = 10290 N (both back wheels).
  4. Each front wheel: 3675 N; each back wheel: 5145 N.

Front wheel 3675 N each; back wheel 5145 N each.
