6.3 A child sits stationary at one end of a long trolley moving uniformly with a speed V on a smooth horizontal floor. If the child gets up and runs about on the trolley in any manner, what is the speed of the CM of the (trolley + child) system ?

Given

  • Trolley + child moving uniformly at V; smooth floor; the child runs about.

Asked

The speed of the centre of mass of the system.

How the animation is drawn

  • Masses and the child’s running are illustrative.

Answer

  1. The forces between the child and the trolley are internal; the smooth floor exerts no horizontal force.
  2. With no external horizontal force, the CM velocity cannot change.
  3. So the CM keeps moving at speed V, however the child runs.

V (unchanged).
