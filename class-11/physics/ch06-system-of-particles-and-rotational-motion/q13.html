6.13 A rope of negligible mass is wound round a hollow cylinder of mass 3 kg and radius 40 cm. What is the angular acceleration of the cylinder if the rope is pulled with a force of 30 N ? What is the linear acceleration of the rope ? Assume that there is no slipping.

Given

  • Hollow cylinder: M = 3 kg, R = 0.40 m; pull 30 N; no slipping.

Asked

The angular acceleration of the cylinder and the linear acceleration of the rope.

How the animation is drawn

  • Shown slowed; 250 px per metre.

Answer

  1. I = MR2 = 3 × 0.402 = 0.48 kg m2; τ = FR = 30 × 0.40 = 12 N m.
  2. α = τ/I = 12/0.48 = 25 rad s−2.
  3. a = Rα = 0.40 × 25 = 10 m s−2.

α = 25 rad s−2; a = 10 m s−2.
