6.7 Two particles, each of mass m and speed v, travel in opposite directions along parallel lines separated by a distance d. Show that the angular momentum vector of the two particle system is the same whatever be the point about which the angular momentum is taken.

Given

  • Masses m, speeds v, opposite directions, lines a distance d apart.

Asked

Show the total angular momentum is the same about any point.

How the animation is drawn

  • The reference point moves around to test different choices; values illustrative.

Answer

  1. About a point O, L = r₁ × p + r₂ × (−p) = (r₁ − r₂) × p.
  2. r₁ − r₂ joins one particle to the other and does not depend on O; its component perpendicular to p is d.
  3. So |L| = m v d, in the same direction, for every point O.

L = mvd about every point (it depends only on r₁ − r₂).
