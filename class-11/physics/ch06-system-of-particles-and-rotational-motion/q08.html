6.8 A non-uniform bar of weight W is suspended at rest by two strings of negligible weight as shown in Fig.6.33. The angles made by the strings with the vertical are 36.9° and 53.1° respectively. The bar is 2 m long. Calculate the distance d of the centre of gravity of the bar from its left end.

Given

  • Bar length 2 m, weight W; strings at 36.9° (left) and 53.1° (right) to the vertical.

Asked

The distance d of the centre of gravity from the left end.

How the animation is drawn

  • Tension arrows to scale with W; 200 px per metre.

Answer

  1. Horizontal balance: T₁ sin 36.9° = T₂ sin 53.1°, i.e. 0.6 T₁ = 0.8 T₂.
  2. Vertical balance: T₁ cos 36.9° + T₂ cos 53.1° = W, i.e. 0.8 T₁ + 0.6 T₂ = W. These give T₁ = 0.8 W, T₂ = 0.6 W.
  3. Torques about the left end: W × d = T₂ cos 53.1° × 2 = 0.6 W × 0.6 × 2, so d = 0.72 m.

d ≈ 0.72 m from the left end.
