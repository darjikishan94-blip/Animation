6.11 A solid cylinder of mass 20 kg rotates about its axis with angular speed 100 rad s-1. The radius of the cylinder is 0.25 m. What is the kinetic energy associated with the rotation of the cylinder? What is the magnitude of angular momentum of the cylinder about its axis?

Given

  • M = 20 kg; R = 0.25 m; ω = 100 rad s−1; solid cylinder about its axis.

Asked

The rotational kinetic energy and the angular momentum.

How the animation is drawn

  • The spin is drawn much slower than 100 rad/s.

Answer

  1. I = ½MR2 = ½ × 20 × 0.252 = 0.625 kg m2.
  2. K = ½Iω2 = ½ × 0.625 × 1002 = 3125 J.
  3. L = Iω = 0.625 × 100 = 62.5 J s.

K = 3125 J; L = 62.5 J s.
