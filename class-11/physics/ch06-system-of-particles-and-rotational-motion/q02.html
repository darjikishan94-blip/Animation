6.2 In the HCl molecule, the separation between the nuclei of the two atoms is about 1.27 Å (1 Å = 10-10 m). Find the approximate location of the CM of the molecule, given that a chlorine atom is about 35.5 times as massive as a hydrogen atom and nearly all the mass of an atom is concentrated in its nucleus.

Given

  • Separation 1.27 Å; mCl = 35.5 mH; mass concentrated in the nuclei.

Asked

The location of the centre of mass.

How the animation is drawn

  • Nuclei drawn as dots (their sizes are not to scale); 350 px per Å.

Answer

  1. Take H at x = 0 and Cl at x = 1.27 Å.
  2. xCM = (m × 0 + 35.5m × 1.27)/(m + 35.5m) = 35.5 × 1.27/36.5 ≈ 1.24 Å from H.
  3. That is only about 0.035 Å from the Cl nucleus.

About 1.24 Å from the H nucleus, on the line joining them.
