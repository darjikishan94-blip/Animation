9.11 Can Bernoulli’s equation be used to describe the flow of water through a rapid in a river ? Explain.

Given

  • Water rushing through a rapid.

Asked

Whether Bernoulli’s equation applies.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for explanations rather than describing one physical situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. No. Bernoulli’s equation holds for steady, streamline (laminar), non-viscous, incompressible flow. Flow through a rapid is turbulent and unsteady.

No: the flow is turbulent, not streamline.
