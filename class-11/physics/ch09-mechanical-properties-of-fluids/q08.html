9.8 A hydraulic automobile lift is designed to lift cars with a maximum mass of 3000 kg. The area of cross-section of the piston carrying the load is 425 cm2. What maximum pressure would the smaller piston have to bear ?

Given

  • Maximum load 3000 kg; large piston area 425 cm2.

Asked

The maximum pressure on the smaller piston.

How the animation is drawn

  • Piston sizes illustrative (the small piston’s area is not given).

Answer

  1. Pascal’s law: the pressure is the same throughout the liquid (at the same level).
  2. P = F/A = 3000 × 9.8 / 425 × 10−4 ≈ 6.92 × 105 Pa. The small piston bears this same pressure.

About 6.9 × 105 Pa.
