9.14 In a test experiment on a model aeroplane in a wind tunnel, the flow speeds on the upper and lower surfaces of the wing are 70 m s–1 and 63 m s-1 respectively. What is the lift on the wing if its area is 2.5 m2 ? Take the density of air to be 1.3 kg m–3.

Given

  • vupper = 70 m s−1, vlower = 63 m s−1; wing area 2.5 m2; ρair = 1.3 kg m−3.

Asked

The lift on the wing.

How the animation is drawn

  • Streamline speeds to scale with each other; the wing section is illustrative.

Answer

  1. Bernoulli (same height): Plower − Pupper = ½ρ(vu2 − vl2) = ½ × 1.3 × (4900 − 3969) ≈ 605 Pa.
  2. Lift = ΔP × A = 605 × 2.5 ≈ 1.5 × 103 N.

About 1.5 × 103 N.
