9.12 Does it matter if one uses gauge instead of absolute pressures in applying Bernoulli’s equation ? Explain.

Given

  • Gauge pressure = absolute pressure − atmospheric pressure.

Asked

Whether it matters.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for explanations rather than describing one physical situation to follow; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. No, provided the atmospheric pressure is the same at the two points compared: Bernoulli’s equation involves differences of pressure, and the same constant subtracted from both sides cancels.

No (if the atmospheric pressure is the same at both points).
