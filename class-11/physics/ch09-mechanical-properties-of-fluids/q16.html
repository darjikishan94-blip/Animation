9.16 The cylindrical tube of a spray pump has a cross-section of 8.0 cm2 one end of which has 40 fine holes each of diameter 1.0 mm. If the liquid flow inside the tube is 1.5 m min–1, what is the speed of ejection of the liquid through the holes ?

Given

  • Tube area 8.0 cm2; 40 holes of diameter 1.0 mm; flow speed in the tube 1.5 m min−1.

Asked

The speed of ejection.

How the animation is drawn

  • Holes enlarged; speeds illustrative.

Answer

  1. Continuity: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂. Total hole area = 40 × π(0.5 × 10−3)2 ≈ 3.14 × 10−5 m2.
  2. v₁ = 1.5/60 = 0.025 m s−1. v₂ = 8.0 × 10−4 × 0.025/3.14 × 10−5 ≈ 0.64 m s−1.

About 0.64 m s−1.
