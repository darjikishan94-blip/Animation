9.19 What is the pressure inside the drop of mercury of radius 3.00 mm at room temperature ? Surface tension of mercury at that temperature (20 °C) is 4.65 × 10–1 N m–1. The atmospheric pressure is 1.01 × 105 Pa. Also give the excess pressure inside the drop.

Given

  • r = 3.00 mm; S = 4.65 × 10−1 N m−1; Patm = 1.01 × 105 Pa.

Asked

The pressure inside, and the excess pressure.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. A drop has one surface: excess pressure = 2S/r = 2 × 0.465/3.00 × 10−3 = 310 Pa.
  2. Pressure inside = 1.01 × 105 + 310 ≈ 1.013 × 105 Pa.

Excess 310 Pa; inside ≈ 1.013 × 105 Pa.
