9.9 A U-tube contains water and methylated spirit separated by mercury. The mercury columns in the two arms are in level with 10.0 cm of water in one arm and 12.5 cm of spirit in the other. What is the specific gravity of spirit ?

Given

  • Mercury levels equal; 10.0 cm of water in one arm, 12.5 cm of spirit in the other.

Asked

The specific gravity of spirit.

How the animation is drawn

  • 16 px per cm.

Answer

  1. Mercury levels equal ⇒ equal pressures on them: ρw g × 10.0 = ρs g × 12.5.
  2. Specific gravity of spirit = ρs/ρw = 10.0/12.5 = 0.8.

0.8
