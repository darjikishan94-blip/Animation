9.6 Toricelli’s barometer used mercury. Pascal duplicated it using French wine of density 984 kg m–3. Determine the height of the wine column for normal atmospheric pressure.

Given

  • Wine density 984 kg m−3; normal atmospheric pressure 1.013 × 105 Pa.

Asked

The height of the wine column.

How the animation is drawn

  • Both tubes to the same scale (30 px per metre).

Answer

  1. The atmosphere holds up a column with ρgh = Patm.
  2. h = 1.013 × 105/(984 × 9.8) ≈ 10.5 m.
