9.10 In the previous problem, if 15.0 cm of water and spirit each are further poured into the respective arms of the tube, what is the difference in the levels of mercury in the two arms ? (Specific gravity of mercury = 13.6)

Given

  • Now 25.0 cm of water and 27.5 cm of spirit; spirit s.g. 0.8 (from 9.9); mercury s.g. 13.6.

Asked

The difference in the mercury levels.

How the animation is drawn

  • 10 px per cm; the mercury level difference is exaggerated 10 times.

Answer

  1. Water column: 25.0 cm of water. Spirit column: 27.5 × 0.8 = 22.0 cm of water equivalent.
  2. Extra pressure on the water side = 3.0 cm of water.
  3. This holds up a mercury difference h: 13.6 h = 3.0 cm, so h ≈ 0.22 cm, mercury higher in the spirit arm.

About 0.22 cm (mercury higher on the spirit side).
