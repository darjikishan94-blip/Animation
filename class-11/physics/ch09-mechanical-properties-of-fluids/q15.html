9.15 Figures 9.20(a) and (b) refer to the steady flow of a (non-viscous) liquid. Which of the two figures is incorrect ? Why ?

Given

  • Fig. 9.20: a pipe with a constriction and two pressure tubes; in (a) the column over the constriction is higher, in (b) lower.

Asked

Which figure is incorrect, and why.

How the animation is drawn

  • Flow speeds illustrative; columns follow Fig. 9.20.

Answer

  1. Continuity: the liquid moves faster through the constriction.
  2. Bernoulli (horizontal pipe): where the speed is greater the pressure is lower, so the column above the constriction should be lower.
  3. Figure (a) shows it higher: (a) is incorrect.

(a) is incorrect.
