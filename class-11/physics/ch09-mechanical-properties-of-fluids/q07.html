9.7 A vertical off-shore structure is built to withstand a maximum stress of 109 Pa. Is the structure suitable for putting up on top of an oil well in the ocean ? Take the depth of the ocean to be roughly 3 km, and ignore ocean currents.

Given

  • Maximum stress 109 Pa; depth 3 km; sea water about 103 kg m−3.

Asked

Whether the structure is suitable.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. Pressure at 3 km ≈ ρgh = 103 × 9.8 × 3000 ≈ 2.9 × 107 Pa (plus 105 Pa of atmosphere).
  2. This is far less than 109 Pa, so the structure is suitable.

Yes: the pressure (≈ 3 × 107 Pa) is well below 109 Pa.
