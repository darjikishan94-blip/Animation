6.15 A tractor pulls a harrow (a ploughing tool) of mass m1 with a net force F resulting in an acceleration of a1. The same tractor pulls a trolley of mass m2 with a force F producing an acceleration of a2. If the tractor now pulls the trolley with the harrow placed on it (with the same force F), then obtain an expression for the resulting acceleration in terms of a1 and a2. Ignore friction.

Given

  • F = m₁a₁ = m₂a₂; now mass m₁ + m₂.

Asked

The new acceleration.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. m₁ = F/a₁ and m₂ = F/a₂.
  2. a = F/(m₁ + m₂) = F/(F/a₁ + F/a₂) = a₁a₂/(a₁ + a₂).

a = a₁a₂/(a₁ + a₂).
