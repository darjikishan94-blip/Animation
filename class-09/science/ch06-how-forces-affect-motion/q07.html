6.7 A sailor jumps out from a small boat to the shore (Fig. 6.38). As the sailor jumps forward, will the boat move? If yes, in which direction and why.

Given

  • A sailor jumps from a small boat towards the shore.

Asked

Does the boat move? Which way, and why?

How the animation is drawn

  • Masses and speeds illustrative.

Answer

  1. Yes. To jump forward the sailor pushes the boat backwards (action); the boat pushes him forward (reaction, equal and opposite).
  2. So the boat moves backwards, away from the shore. (Equivalently, total momentum stays zero: the light boat gets a backward momentum equal to the sailor’s forward momentum.)

Yes — backwards, away from the shore (Newton’s third law).
