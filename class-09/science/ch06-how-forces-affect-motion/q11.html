6.11 The velocity-time graph of an object of mass 10 kg moving along a straight line is shown in Fig. 6.41. Calculate the force acting on the object by using the graph.

Given

  • m = 10 kg; Fig. 6.41: v = 10 m/s at 0 s, 20 m/s at 4 s, 30 m/s at 8 s.

Asked

The force.

How the animation is drawn

  • Graph drawn from Fig. 6.41.

Answer

  1. a = slope = (30 − 10)/8 = 2.5 m s−2.
  2. F = ma = 10 × 2.5 = 25 N.

25 N.
