6.3 Two blocks P and Q on a smooth horizontal surface are shown in Fig. 6.36a and Fig. 6.36b. Two forces of magnitudes 4 N and 5 N are acting in opposite directions on block P, while block Q is moving with a constant velocity. Which of the following statement is correct? (i) P experiences a net force and Q does not experience a net force. (ii) P does not experience a net force and Q experiences a net force. (iii) Both P and Q experience a net force. (iv) Neither P nor Q experiences a net force.

Given

  • P: 5 N and 4 N in opposite directions. Q: constant velocity. Smooth surface.

Asked

The correct statement.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force arrows to scale; P’s motion illustrative (its mass is not given).

Answer

  1. P: net force = 5 − 4 = 1 N towards the 5 N side, so P accelerates.
  2. Q: constant velocity, so its net force is zero.

(i)
