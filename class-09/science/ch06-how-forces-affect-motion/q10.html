6.10 The acceleration-mass graph for the acceleration produced by a force on objects of different masses is plotted in Fig. 6.40. Plot the force-mass graph for this case.

Given

  • Fig. 6.40: a = 10 m s−2 at 1 kg, falling smoothly to about 2 m s−2 at 5 kg (a × m stays 10).

Asked

The F–m graph.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. F = ma: 1 × 10 = 10 N, 2 × 5 = 10 N, 4 × 2.5 = 10 N, 5 × 2 = 10 N.
  2. The same 10 N acts on every mass: the F–m graph is a horizontal straight line at F = 10 N.

A horizontal line at F = 10 N.
