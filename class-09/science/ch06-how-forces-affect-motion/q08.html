6.8 During a high jump event, a landing mat or sand bed is placed for the athlete to fall upon (Fig. 6.39). Explain the reason behind it.

Given

  • An athlete landing after a jump.

Asked

Why a mat or sand bed.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force–time shapes illustrative; the two areas (momentum change) are equal.

Answer

  1. The athlete must lose the same momentum whatever the surface.
  2. Force = change in momentum/time. The soft mat or sand increases the stopping time, so the force on the body is much smaller and injury is avoided.

It lengthens the stopping time, reducing the force.
