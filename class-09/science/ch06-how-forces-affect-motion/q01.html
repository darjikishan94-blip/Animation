6.1 Using a horizontal force F, a table is moved across the floor at a constant velocity. How much is the frictional force exerted by the floor on the table?

Given

  • Horizontal push F; constant velocity.

Asked

The friction force.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force arrows equal in length.

Answer

  1. Constant velocity means zero acceleration, so the net force on the table is zero (Newton’s first law).
  2. Friction must balance the push: it equals F in magnitude and acts opposite to the motion.

F, opposite to the motion.
