6.12 A bullet of mass 50 g moving with a speed of 100 m s–1 enters a heavy stationary wooden block and stops after penetrating a distance of 50 cm. Estimate the stopping force acting on the bullet (assume that the bullet undergoes constant acceleration within the block).

Given

  • m = 0.050 kg; u = 100 m/s; v = 0; s = 0.50 m.

Asked

The stopping force.

How the animation is drawn

  • Penetration to scale (500 px per m); time slowed enormously.

Answer

  1. v2 = u2 + 2as: 0 = 1002 + 2a(0.50), so a = −10 000 m s−2.
  2. F = ma = 0.050 × 10 000 = 500 N, opposite to the motion.

500 N.
