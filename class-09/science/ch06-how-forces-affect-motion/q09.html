6.9 A hand cart loaded with vegetables collides with an identical but empty hand cart. During the collision: (i) the loaded cart exerts a force of larger magnitude on the empty cart. (ii) the empty cart exerts a force of larger magnitude on the loaded cart. (iii) neither cart exerts a force on the other. (iv) the loaded cart and the empty cart, both exert an equal magnitude of force on each other.

Given

  • A loaded cart collides with an identical empty cart.

Asked

The correct statement about the forces.

How the animation is drawn

  • Force arrows equal; motion illustrative.

Answer

  1. By Newton’s third law the forces the two carts exert on each other are always equal in magnitude and opposite in direction, whatever their masses.
  2. (The lighter empty cart gets the larger acceleration.)
