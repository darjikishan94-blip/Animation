4.3 A girl is riding her scooter and finds that its speedometer reading is constant. Is it possible for her scooter to be accelerating and if so, how?

Given

  • Constant speed.

Asked

Can it still accelerate?

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. Yes. Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, and velocity has direction. If she turns (goes round a bend or a circle) at constant speed, the direction of her velocity changes, so she is accelerating.

Yes — by changing direction (e.g. going round a curve).
