4.13 A girl is preparing for her first marathon by running on a straight road. She uses a smartwatch to calculate her running speed at different intervals. The graph (Fig. 4.31) depicts her velocity versus time. Estimate the distance she ran based on the graph.

Given

  • Fig. 4.31, read as points (h, km/h): (0, 7.0), (0.6, 7.0), (1.6, 7.5), (3.0, 7.5), (4.6, 7.0), (5.6, 6.5), (6.6, 6.5).

Asked

The distance.

How the animation is drawn

  • Graph drawn through the points read from Fig. 4.31.

Answer

  1. Distance = area under the v–t graph (km/h × h = km).
  2. Strips: 7.0 × 0.6 = 4.2; 7.25 × 1.0 = 7.25; 7.5 × 1.4 = 10.5; 7.25 × 1.6 = 11.6; 6.75 × 1.0 = 6.75; 6.5 × 1.0 = 6.5.
  3. Total ≈ 46.8 km — about 47 km (the estimate depends a little on how the graph is read).

About 47 km.
