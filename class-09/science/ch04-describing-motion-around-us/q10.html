4.10 A bus is travelling at 36 km h–1 when the driver sees an obstacle 30 m ahead. The driver takes 0.5 seconds to react before pressing the brake. Once the brake is applied, the velocity of the bus reduces with constant acceleration of 2.5 m s–2. Will the bus be able to stop before reaching the obstacle?

Given

  • v = 36 km/h = 10 m/s; obstacle 30 m ahead; reaction time 0.5 s; deceleration 2.5 m s−2.

Asked

Does it stop in time?

How the animation is drawn

  • Road to scale (14 px per m); real time.

Answer

  1. During the reaction time: 10 × 0.5 = 5 m.
  2. Braking distance: v2/2a = 100/5 = 20 m (taking 4 s).
  3. Total 25 m < 30 m: yes, it stops 5 m before the obstacle.

Yes — after 25 m, 5 m short of the obstacle.
