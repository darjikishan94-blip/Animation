4.5 A motorbike moving with initial velocity 28 m s–1 and constant acceleration stops after travelling 98 m. Find the acceleration of the motorbike and the time taken to come to a stop.

Given

  • u = 28 m/s; v = 0; s = 98 m; constant acceleration.

Asked

a; t.

How the animation is drawn

  • Road to scale (4.5 px per m); real time.

Answer

  1. v2 = u2 + 2as: 0 = 784 + 196a, so a = −4 m s−2.
  2. t = (v − u)/a = (0 − 28)/(−4) = 7 s.

−4 m s−2 (retardation 4 m s−2); 7 s.
