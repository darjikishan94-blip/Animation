4.16 Rohan studies science from 6 PM to 7:30 PM at home. Consider the tip of the minute’s hand of the wall clock. During the given time interval, what is its: (i) distance travelled, (ii) displacement, (iii) speed, and (iv) velocity. The length of the minute’s hand is 7 cm (Fig. 4.32).

Given

  • Minute hand 7 cm; 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM (90 min = 5400 s).

Asked

(i) distance (ii) displacement (iii) average speed (iv) average velocity.

How the animation is drawn

  • Clock to scale (14 px per cm); time sped up.

Answer

  1. In 90 min the minute hand makes 1½ turns: the tip goes from the 12 round to the 6.
  2. (i) Distance = 1.5 × 2π × 7 ≈ 66 cm.
  3. (ii) Displacement = a diameter = 14 cm, from the 12 towards the 6 (vertically downward).
  4. (iii) Average speed = 66 cm/5400 s ≈ 0.012 cm/s (≈ 1.2 × 10−4 m/s).
  5. (iv) Average velocity = 14 cm/5400 s ≈ 0.0026 cm/s (≈ 2.6 × 10−5 m/s), downward.

(i) ≈ 66 cm (ii) 14 cm, downward (iii) ≈ 0.012 cm/s (iv) ≈ 0.0026 cm/s, downward.
