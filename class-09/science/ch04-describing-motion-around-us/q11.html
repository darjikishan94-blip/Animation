4.11 A student said, “The Earth moves around the Sun”. In this context, discuss whether an object kept on the Earth can be considered to be at rest.

Given

  • An object kept on the Earth.

Asked

Is it at rest?

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. Rest and motion are relative to a reference. Relative to the Earth (the room, the ground) the object does not change position: it is at rest.
  2. Relative to the Sun it moves with the Earth (orbiting and spinning). So it is at rest in one frame and moving in another.

At rest relative to the Earth; moving relative to the Sun.
