4.4 A car starts from rest and its velocity reaches 24 m s–1 in 6 s. Find the average acceleration and the distance travelled in these 6 s.

Given

  • u = 0; v = 24 m/s; t = 6 s.

Asked

Average acceleration; distance.

How the animation is drawn

  • Car motion to scale (5 px per m); graph computed. Uniform acceleration assumed for the distance.

Answer

  1. a = (v − u)/t = 24/6 = 4 m s−2.
  2. s = ut + ½at2 = ½ × 4 × 36 = 72 m (the area of the triangle under the v–t graph, ½ × 6 × 24).

4 m s−2; 72 m.
