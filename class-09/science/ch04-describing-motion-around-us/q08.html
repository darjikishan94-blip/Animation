4.8 A truck driver driving at the speed of 54 km h–1 notices a road sign with a speed limit of 40 km h–1 (Fig. 4.29) for trucks. He slows down to 36 km h–1 in 36 s. What was the distance travelled by him during this time? Assume the acceleration to be constant while slowing down.

Given

  • u = 54 km/h = 15 m/s; v = 36 km/h = 10 m/s; t = 36 s; constant acceleration.

Asked

Distance in the 36 s.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. With constant acceleration, s = average velocity × time = (15 + 10)/2 × 36 = 450 m.
  2. (a = (10 − 15)/36 ≈ −0.14 m s−2.)

450 m.
