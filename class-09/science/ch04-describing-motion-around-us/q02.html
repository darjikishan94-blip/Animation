4.2 A student runs from the ground floor to the fourth floor of a school building to collect a book and then comes down to their classroom on the second floor. If the height of each floor is 3 m, find: (i) the total vertical distance travelled, and (ii) their displacement from the starting point.

Given

  • Each floor 3 m; ground → 4th floor → 2nd floor.

Asked

(i) Total vertical distance (ii) displacement.

How the animation is drawn

  • Floors to scale.

Answer

  1. Up: 4 floors × 3 m = 12 m. Down: 2 floors × 3 m = 6 m.
  2. (i) Distance = 12 + 6 = 18 m.
  3. (ii) Displacement = 2 floors × 3 m = 6 m, vertically upward.

(i) 18 m (ii) 6 m upward.
