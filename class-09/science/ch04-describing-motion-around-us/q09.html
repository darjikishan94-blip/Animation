4.9 A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly to 20 m s–1 in 5 seconds. It then travels at 20 m s–1 for 10 seconds and finally applies the brake (with uniform acceleration) to stop in 6 seconds. Find the total distance travelled.

Given

  • 0 → 20 m/s in 5 s; 20 m/s for 10 s; 20 → 0 in 6 s (uniform).

Asked

Total distance.

How the animation is drawn

  • Graph to scale; the car moves in real time (1.5 px per m).

Answer

  1. Area under the v–t graph: ½ × 5 × 20 = 50 m; 10 × 20 = 200 m; ½ × 6 × 20 = 60 m.
  2. Total = 310 m.

310 m.
