4.7 A graph in Fig. 4.28 shows the change in position with time for two objects A and B moving in a straight line from 0 to 10 seconds. Choose the correct option(s). (i) The average velocity of both over the 10 s time interval is equal since they have the same initial and final positions. (ii) The average speeds of both over the 10 s time interval are equal since both cover equal distance in equal time. (iii) The average speed of A over the 10 s time interval is lower than that of B since it covers a shorter distance than B in 10 seconds. (iv) The average speed of A over the 10 s time interval is greater than that of B since B’s speed is lower than A’s in some segments.

Given

  • Fig. 4.28: A and B start at the same position and end at the same position at 10 s; neither turns back (both lines always rise).

Asked

The correct options.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a choose-the-correct-options question about a graph; the page shows the question and the reasoned answer.

Answer

  1. Same initial and final positions in the same 10 s: equal displacement, so equal average velocity — (i) is correct.
  2. Neither turns back, so each one’s distance equals its displacement: equal distances in 10 s, so equal average speeds — (ii) is correct.
  3. (iii) and (iv) are wrong: the distances are equal, whatever the speeds in between.

(i) and (ii).
