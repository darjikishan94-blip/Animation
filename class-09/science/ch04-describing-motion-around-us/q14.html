4.14 On entering a state highway, a car continues to move with a constant velocity of 6 m s–1 for 2 minutes and then accelerates with a constant acceleration 1 m s–2 for 6 seconds. Find the displacement of the car on the state highway in the 2 min 6 s time interval by drawing a velocity-time graph for its motion.

Given

  • 6 m/s for 120 s; then a = 1 m s−2 for 6 s.

Asked

Displacement in 126 s.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. v–t graph: a horizontal line at 6 m/s from 0 to 120 s, then a straight line rising to 12 m/s at 126 s.
  2. Area: 6 × 120 = 720 m; then trapezium ½(6 + 12) × 6 = 54 m.
  3. Displacement = 774 m.

774 m.
