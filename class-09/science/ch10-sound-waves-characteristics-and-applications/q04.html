10.4 In a room, the reflected sound reaches the ear 0.05 s after its production. Will it produce an echo or reverberation? Justify your answer.

Given

  • Reflected sound arrives 0.05 s after the original.

Asked

Echo or reverberation?

How the animation is drawn

  • Timeline to scale (2000 px per s).

Answer

  1. The sensation of a sound lasts about 0.1 s in our ear. A distinct echo needs the reflection to arrive at least 0.1 s later.
  2. 0.05 s < 0.1 s, so the reflected sound overlaps the original and prolongs it: reverberation, not an echo.

Reverberation (0.05 s < 0.1 s).
