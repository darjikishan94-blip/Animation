10.15 Two identical sound sources are placed at A and B — one in air and one submerged in water (Fig. 10.34). Both produce sounds at the same time, which travel horizontally to the vertical side of the cliff and come back. If the time taken by the sound to return to A is 4.5 times than that of B, what is the ratio between the speeds of sound in air and water?

Given

  • Same distance to the cliff for A (air) and B (water); tA = 4.5 tB.

Asked

vair : vwater.

How the animation is drawn

  • Times in the ratio 4.5 : 1; speeds slowed down.

Answer

  1. Both travel 2d: v = 2d/t, so vair/vwater = tB/tA = 1/4.5.
  2. vair : vwater = 1 : 4.5 = 2 : 9 (≈ 0.22).

1 : 4.5 (= 2 : 9).
