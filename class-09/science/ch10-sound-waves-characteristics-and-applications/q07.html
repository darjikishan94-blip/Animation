10.7 Draw a graph to represent a sound wave for which the density amplitude is 3 units and wavelength is 4 cm.

Given

  • Density amplitude 3 units; λ = 4 cm.

Asked

The graph.

How the animation is drawn

  • Distance axis to scale (40 px per cm).

Answer

  1. Plot density (relative to the mean) against distance: a sine curve between +3 and −3 units.
  2. Successive peaks (compressions) are 4 cm apart; troughs (rarefactions) lie midway between them.

A sine curve of amplitude 3 units repeating every 4 cm.
