10.6 The sound waves emitted by three sources A, B and C are represented in Fig. 10.31. If the frequency of A is maximum and C is minimum, identify the corresponding curves, and mark A, B and C on them.

Given

  • Fig. 10.31: three curves in the same medium (same speed) — green (shortest wavelength), red (middle), blue (longest).

Asked

Which curve is A, B, C.

How the animation is drawn

  • Curves follow Fig. 10.31.

Answer

  1. In the same medium v = νλ is the same for all, so higher frequency means shorter wavelength.
  2. A (maximum ν) is the curve with the shortest wavelength (green); C (minimum ν) has the longest (blue); B is the remaining one (red).

A: shortest wavelength (green); B: middle (red); C: longest (blue).
