10.10 A ship searching for a sunken ship sent a sonar signal and detected an echo after 5 s. If ultrasonic wave travels at 1525 m s–1 in seawater, approximately how far down in the ocean is the wreckage of the sunken ship located?

Given

  • Echo after 5 s; v = 1525 m/s.

Asked

Depth of the wreck.

How the animation is drawn

  • Depth not to scale; real time (5 s).

Answer

  1. The pulse travels down and back: 2d = vt.
  2. d = 1525 × 5/2 ≈ 3812 m ≈ 3.8 km.

About 3.8 km.
