10.13 The variation of density of medium for a sound wave propagating with a speed of 340 m s–1 is shown in Fig. 10.32. Calculate the wavelength and frequency of the sound wave.

Given

  • Fig. 10.32: 8 cm from the centre of the first compression to the centre of the third; v = 340 m/s.

Asked

λ and ν.

How the animation is drawn

  • 40 px per cm; particle pattern illustrative.

Answer

  1. The 8 cm spans two wavelengths (compression to compression twice): λ = 4 cm = 0.04 m.
  2. ν = v/λ = 340/0.04 = 8500 Hz.

λ = 4 cm; ν = 8500 Hz.
