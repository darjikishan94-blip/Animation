10.5 Graphs representing two sound waves are given in Fig. 10.30. If the scales on the X and Y axes of the two graphs are the same, which of the two sound waves has (i) greater wavelength, and (ii) smaller amplitude?

Given

  • Fig. 10.30: (a) about 3 waves across the graph with small peaks; (b) about 6 waves with taller peaks; same scales.

Asked

(i) Greater wavelength (ii) smaller amplitude.

How the animation is drawn

  • Curves follow Fig. 10.30.

Answer

  1. (i) Wavelength = distance between successive peaks: (a)’s peaks are farther apart, so (a) has the greater wavelength.
  2. (ii) Amplitude = height of a peak from the mean line: (a)’s peaks are lower, so (a) has the smaller amplitude.

(i) (a) (ii) (a)
