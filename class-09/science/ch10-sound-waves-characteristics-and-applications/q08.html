10.8 In a movie, while showing the explosion of a spacecraft in space, a flash of light is shown along with sound at the same time. What are the errors in this depiction?

Given

  • An explosion in space shown with sound and light together.

Asked

The errors.

Why there is no animation

  • This question asks for a short explanation; the page shows the question and the answer.

Answer

  1. Space is (nearly) a vacuum; sound needs a medium, so no sound would be heard at all.
  2. Even in air, light (3 × 108 m/s) travels far faster than sound (about 340 m/s), so the flash would be seen before the sound was heard, not at the same time.

No sound in space; and light would arrive before sound anyway.
