10.9 A source produces a sound wave of wavelength 3.44 m. If the wave travels with a speed of 344 m s–1 find its time period.

Given

  • λ = 3.44 m; v = 344 m/s.

Asked

T.

Why there is no animation

  • This is a direct calculation with nothing moving to show; the page shows the question and a worked answer.

Answer

  1. ν = v/λ = 344/3.44 = 100 Hz.
  2. T = 1/ν = 0.01 s.

0.01 s.
