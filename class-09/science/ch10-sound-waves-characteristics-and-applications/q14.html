10.14 The graphical representation of two sound waves A and B propagating at the same speed of 345 m s–1 is shown in Fig. 10.33. What is the wavelength of each of them? Also, calculate their frequencies.

Given

  • Fig. 10.33: A repeats every 2.5 cm; B every 5.0 cm; v = 345 m/s for both.

Asked

λ and ν of each.

How the animation is drawn

  • Curves drawn from Fig. 10.33; 70 px per cm.

Answer

  1. λA = 2.5 cm = 0.025 m: νA = 345/0.025 = 13 800 Hz.
  2. λB = 5.0 cm = 0.050 m: νB = 345/0.050 = 6900 Hz.

A: 2.5 cm, 13 800 Hz; B: 5.0 cm, 6900 Hz.
